Exam Details

The Virginia Masonry Contracting specialty is identified as BRK. The scope of Masonry Contracting includes work involving brick, concrete block, stone, marble, slate, and other units and products commonly used in the masonry industry. It also includes related masonry work such as grout installation, caulking, tuck pointing, sand blasting, mortar washing, parging, cleaning, and reinforcement steel associated with masonry construction.

The Virginia Masonry Contracting examination contains 60 questions. Candidates must earn a minimum passing score of 70 percent, equal to 42 correct answers, and the examination allows 150 minutes.

The examination content is divided into the following subject areas:

  • Estimating and Plan Reading: 5 questions
  • Unit Masonry: 12 questions
  • Mortar and Grout: 11 questions
  • Accessories and Specialties: 10 questions
  • Stone: 3 questions
  • Tools: 4 questions
  • Restoration and Cleaning: 4 questions
  • Reinforced Masonry: 6 questions
  • Safety: 5 questions

Unit masonry, mortar and grout, and accessories and specialties represent a large portion of the examination, but candidates should prepare across every listed category. Questions may be based on the published references as well as trade knowledge and general industry practices.

The Masonry specialty is one of the Virginia contractor specialties for which the Qualified Individual must meet the applicable experience requirements and successfully complete an approved technical examination.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Masonry Contracting examination is open book. Candidates must bring their own permitted reference materials because the books are not supplied at the testing location.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs may also be used. Removable tabs are not permitted, and reference materials containing writing are not allowed into the examination. Candidates may not write in reference materials during the exam session.

The open-book format makes reference navigation an important part of preparation. With 60 questions and 150 minutes, candidates should avoid spending unnecessary time searching through unfamiliar books. Studying with the rental references before exam day helps build familiarity with their chapters, tables, indexes, terminology, and major subject areas.

Students should practice identifying which book is most likely to contain a particular answer. Safety questions may lead to 29 CFR Part 1926. Building-code questions may require the 2021 International Building Code. General masonry construction topics may direct candidates toward Modern Masonry, while stone and restoration questions may involve the Indiana Limestone Handbook or the publication on repointing historic masonry buildings.

Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing is administered by the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation through the Board for Contractors. Contractor licenses are issued to businesses and include a license class along with one or more classifications or specialties.

  1. Choose the appropriate contractor license class. Virginia contractor licenses are issued as Class A, Class B, or Class C.
  2. Select the Masonry Contracting specialty. Masonry Contracting is identified by the BRK specialty designation.
  3. Identify the Qualified Individual. The business must designate a Qualified Individual for each applicable classification or specialty.
  4. Meet the required experience. The Qualified Individual must satisfy the experience requirement for the selected license class and specialty.
  5. Complete applicable pre-license education. New Virginia contractor license applicants must satisfy the state's required pre-license education requirements.
  6. Obtain approval for the Masonry technical exam. BRK Masonry is one of the specialties requiring pre-approval and successful completion of an examination.
  7. Prepare for and pass the required examination. Candidates should use the exam content outline, reference materials, and organized study practice to prepare.
  8. Complete the applicable contractor license application requirements. The business must meet the requirements applying to its license class, Responsible Management, Qualified Individual, documentation, and examinations.

State Requirements

Virginia contractor licenses combine a license class with one or more classifications or specialties. The class determines the level of contracting activity authorized, while the specialty identifies the type of contracting work the business may perform.

A contractor seeking the BRK Masonry specialty must designate a Qualified Individual who satisfies the applicable Virginia requirements. Current experience requirements depend on the license class. The Qualified Individual must have at least one year of experience for Class C, three years for Class B, or five years for Class A in the applicable classification or specialty.

Virginia also requires approved pre-license education for new contractor licenses. This requirement is separate from the BRK technical examination. Contractor applicants should distinguish between business and regulatory preparation and the trade-specific knowledge evaluated on the Masonry examination.
