### Exam Details

The Virginia Masonry Contracting specialty is identified as BRK. The scope of Masonry Contracting includes work involving brick, concrete block, stone, marble, slate, and other units and products commonly used in the masonry industry. It also includes related masonry work such as grout installation, caulking, tuck pointing, sand blasting, mortar washing, parging, cleaning, and reinforcement steel associated with masonry construction.

The Virginia Masonry Contracting examination contains 60 questions. Candidates must earn a minimum passing score of 70 percent, equal to 42 correct answers, and the examination allows 150 minutes.

### State Requirements

Virginia contractor licenses combine a license class with one or more classifications or specialties. The class determines the level of contracting activity authorized, while the specialty identifies the type of contracting work the business may perform.

A contractor seeking the BRK Masonry specialty must designate a Qualified Individual who satisfies the applicable Virginia requirements. Current experience requirements depend on the license class. The Qualified Individual must have at least one year of experience for Class C, three years for Class B, or five years for Class A in the applicable classification or specialty.

## How does the Masonry Contractor exam work?

The Masonry Contractor exam is the technical portion of the license for that classification. A Class A or B firm usually also needs the business and law portion, and every applicant needs the 8-hour pre-license course.

- Virginia issues contractor licenses in three classes. Class C covers single jobs of $1,000 to under $10,000 and under $150,000 a year; Class B covers single jobs of $10,000 to under $120,000 and under $750,000 a year; Class A has no upper limit.
- Every applicant completes 8 hours of pre-license education before the license is issued.
- Class A and B firms name a designated employee or member of responsible management who passes the exams. PSI runs them, and the fee is $40 for one portion, $72 for two and $85 for three.
