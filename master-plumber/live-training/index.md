### Open Book Test

The Virginia Master Plumber examination is open book. Candidates must bring their own permitted reference materials because the testing site does not provide the books.

The current examination references include the *Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations* for tradesman regulatory questions and the *International Plumbing Code, 2021* for Journeyman and Master Plumber technical questions.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are permitted, while removable tabs are not permitted. References containing writing are not allowed, and candidates may not write in their references during the examination session.

An open-book format does not eliminate the need for study. The International Plumbing Code contains extensive technical material, and candidates should not expect to search the entire book for every answer. A stronger approach is to understand the plumbing concept first and use the reference to confirm the applicable requirement.

Live virtual training can help candidates understand where major subjects appear in the references, while self-study provides the repetition needed to make navigation more familiar. Candidates should practice using chapter headings, the index, definitions, tables, and cross-references.

A useful method is to read the question, identify the plumbing subject, select the appropriate reference, locate the applicable section or table, and verify the answer. Repeating that process during preparation helps build a consistent open-book strategy.

### Licensing Steps

The Virginia Master Plumber credential is issued to an individual. Candidates must complete the applicable qualification, application, and examination process before licensure.

1. **Review the Master Plumber qualification requirements.** Determine which experience or existing-license pathway applies to your background.
2. **Document the required practical experience.** Applicants must provide the experience verification required by the Virginia Board for Contractors.
3. **Submit the applicable Tradesman Exam and License Application.** The Board reviews the application before granting examination eligibility.
4. **Receive Board approval to test.** Pre-approval is required before a candidate may sit for the Virginia Master Plumber examination.
5. **Register and schedule through PSI.** After Board approval, candidates may proceed with the examination provider's scheduling process.
6. **Prepare with the applicable references.** Study the Virginia regulations and the 2021 International Plumbing Code while practicing technical questions and book navigation.
7. **Complete the Master Plumber examination.** Candidates must satisfy the current examination requirement for Master-level licensure.
8. **Complete any remaining licensing requirements.** The Board must receive and approve the required licensing information before the individual credential is issued.

Virginia currently provides several qualification pathways for an applicant seeking Master licensure in a trade. An applicant may qualify after holding a valid residential plumber tradesman license for three years, after holding a valid Journeyman license in the trade for at least one year, or by documenting nine years of practical experience in the trade.

### State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates individual plumbers through the Tradesmen Program. The Master Plumber credential is an individual license and should be distinguished from a contractor business license.

A person pursuing Master Plumber licensure must satisfy the Board's applicable experience requirements before testing. The Board reviews applications and approves candidates before they may schedule the examination with PSI.

Holding a Master Plumber license does not automatically create a licensed plumbing contracting business. Virginia contractor businesses are licensed separately and must meet the requirements for the appropriate contractor license class and classification or specialty.

For professionals planning to operate through a plumbing contracting company, the Master Plumber credential can be an important individual qualification associated with the business. The company must still complete the separate contractor licensing requirements established by Virginia.

Virginia also regulates continuing education for licensed plumbers as part of the applicable renewal process. Continuing education is separate from initial examination preparation. Exam prep training is intended to help candidates prepare for the Master Plumber examination and does not replace Board-approved continuing education required for license renewal.

### Test Information and Study Materials

The current exam outline makes drainage, waste and vent systems the largest technical study area, with 24 items. Candidates should devote substantial preparation time to drainage piping, sizing, slopes, fittings, cleanouts, vents, vent configurations, trap protection, and related code requirements.

Water supply systems account for 10 items. Candidates should review water-distribution piping, sizing, pressure, valves, materials, connections, system protection, and other requirements contained in the International Plumbing Code. Practice using tables and technical provisions rather than relying only on memorized values.

Backflow prevention also accounts for 10 items. Candidates should understand the purpose of protecting potable-water systems, recognize cross-connection hazards, and become familiar with the code requirements and devices used to prevent contamination or reverse flow.

Storm drainage systems represent another 10 items. Candidates should study roof drainage, conductors, storm piping, sizing, and other provisions governing stormwater removal from buildings.

Plumbing fixtures account for 7 items. Study fixture installation, connections, clearances, required components, fixture fittings, and related provisions. Candidates should know how to use both the code chapters and the index to locate fixture-specific requirements efficiently.

General regulations account for 5 items. These questions can require familiarity with broader plumbing-code provisions that apply across multiple systems. Candidates should avoid focusing only on the largest topics and should review the general requirements that influence installation and code compliance.

Interceptors and traps account for 4 items. Study trap requirements, trap seals, prohibited arrangements, interceptors, separators, and other related provisions. Understanding the purpose of these components can make code requirements easier to interpret.

The examination also contains regulatory questions covering standards of practice, standards of conduct, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, qualification for licensure, and revocation of licensure. These topics should be reviewed directly in the Virginia regulations.

## How does the Master Plumber exam work?

The Master Plumber exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
