Exam Details

The Virginia Swimming Pool Construction Contractor examination is the Qualified Individual technical examination associated with the POL specialty. Swimming Pool Construction is among the Virginia contractor specialties requiring the Qualified Individual to successfully complete a technical examination approved by the Board for Contractors.

The POL examination contains 50 questions. Candidates receive 120 minutes to complete the exam and must answer at least 35 questions correctly to achieve the required 70% passing score.

The examination content includes:

  • Plan Reading and Estimating: 3 questions
  • Sitework and Excavating: 10 questions
  • Forming and Reinforcing: 8 questions
  • Concrete: 10 questions
  • Shotcrete: 10 questions
  • Pool Equipment, Including ADA: 6 questions
  • Pool Access Safety and Security: 3 questions

Sitework and Excavating, Concrete, and Shotcrete are the largest individual categories, with 10 questions each. Forming and Reinforcing is another major area with eight questions. Candidates should allocate their study time accordingly while still reviewing every examination topic.

The Virginia Board for Contractors uses PSI as its exam vendor for Qualified Individual specialty examinations. Candidates must complete the applicable approval process before scheduling the POL examination.

The current Qualified Individual specialty examination fee is $85, paid directly to PSI. This testing fee is separate from The 1 Package pricing.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Swimming Pool Construction Contractor examination is an open-book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their permitted reference materials because the examination center does not provide the books.

The open-book format makes familiarity with the reference library an important part of preparation. With 50 questions and 120 minutes, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds for each question. Efficient reference selection can therefore make a major difference in exam pacing.

A concrete question may lead to Design and Control of Concrete Mixtures. A shotcrete question may require the Guide to Shotcrete. Questions involving excavation and underground construction may lead to Pipe and Excavation Contracting, while reinforcing questions can be researched through Placing Reinforcing Bars.

Questions involving swimming pool code requirements, access safety, barriers, and related pool-specific provisions may require the International Swimming Pool and Spa Code, 2021. Broader building-code questions may require the International Building Code, 2021.

Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licenses combine a license class with one or more classifications or specialties. Swimming Pool Construction Contracting is identified by the specialty abbreviation POL.

  1. Determine the appropriate contractor license class. Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
  2. Select Swimming Pool Construction Contracting. The applicable specialty designation is POL.
  3. Identify the Qualified Individual. The business must designate a Qualified Individual who qualifies the company for the POL specialty.
  4. Meet the applicable experience requirement. The Qualified Individual must document experience appropriate to the requested contractor class and specialty.
  5. Complete the required experience verification. POL is one of the specialties requiring technical examination approval.
  6. Receive approval for the POL examination. The Qualified Individual must complete the applicable qualification process before scheduling.
  7. Prepare for the technical examination. Use the included references, one-year course access, practice-oriented study, and reference-navigation preparation.
  8. Complete the POL specialty examination. Successful completion satisfies the technical examination requirement for the Qualified Individual.
  9. Complete applicable pre-license education. New Virginia contractor applicants must satisfy the Board-approved pre-license education requirement.
  10. Complete additional Class A or Class B requirements when applicable. Business examination requirements are separate from the POL technical exam.
  11. Complete the contractor license application. Application Service is included with The 1 Package.
  12. Establish the business entity. Business Formation for an LLC or Corporation is included.
  13. Obtain the EIN. EIN Filing with the IRS is included with the package.
  14. Address contractor compliance. Contractor Compliance Guidance helps customers understand applicable Virginia contractor requirements.

Passing the POL technical exam does not automatically issue a contractor license. The contractor business must also satisfy the applicable license class, Qualified Individual, business, education, application, and regulatory requirements.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors licenses businesses engaged in construction, repair, removal, and improvement work on property owned by others. Contractor licenses include a license class and one or more classifications or specialties.

A contractor business requesting the POL specialty must identify a Qualified Individual who satisfies the applicable experience and examination requirements. Current minimum Qualified Individual experience requirements are one year for Class C, three years for Class B, and five years for Class A.

Swimming Pool Construction is specifically listed among the Virginia specialties requiring a Board-approved technical examination. Qualified Individuals applying for specialties that require examination approval must also submit the required experience documentation.

The POL specialty covers construction, repair, improvement, and removal of in-ground swimming pools. It does not include trade-related plumbing, electrical, backflow, or HVAC work. Those activities can require separately qualified trades or contractor authority.

Virginia contractor applicants must also address the applicable pre-license business education requirements. Class A and Class B applicants have additional Designated Employee business examination requirements that are separate from the technical POL exam.
