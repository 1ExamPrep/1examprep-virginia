### Exam Details

The Virginia Swimming Pool Construction Contractor examination is the Qualified Individual technical examination associated with the **POL** specialty. Virginia requires candidates for this specialty to receive the applicable approval before scheduling the examination.

The current Swimming Pool Construction Contracting examination contains **50 questions**. Candidates receive **120 minutes** to complete the examination and must answer at least **35 questions correctly** to achieve the required 70% passing score.

The current examination content outline includes:

- **Plan Reading and Estimating:** 3 questions
- **Sitework and Excavating:** 10 questions
- **Forming and Reinforcing:** 8 questions
- **Concrete:** 10 questions
- **Shotcrete:** 10 questions
- **Pool Equipment, Including ADA:** 6 questions
- **Pool Access Safety and Security:** 3 questions

Sitework and Excavating, Concrete, and Shotcrete are the largest examination areas, with 10 questions each. Forming and Reinforcing also represents a substantial portion of the exam. Candidates should therefore spend considerable preparation time on excavation practices, concrete materials and placement, shotcrete application, reinforcement, and structural preparation.

Pool Equipment, Including ADA, requires candidates to understand pool-related equipment and applicable accessibility concepts. Pool Access Safety and Security addresses important safety considerations associated with swimming pool construction and access.

Virginia uses PSI to administer Qualified Individual contractor specialty examinations. The current fee for a Qualified Individual specialty examination is **$85**, paid directly to PSI. That examination fee is separate from this book rental package.

### Open Book Test

The Virginia Swimming Pool Construction Contractor examination is an **open-book test**. Candidates must bring their own permitted references because reference books are not supplied at the test site.

Current examination rules allow references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the test. Permanent tabs are allowed, while removable tabs are not permitted. Reference materials containing handwritten notes are not allowed. Loose papers and unbound references are also not permitted under the testing rules.

Some books in this rental package are highlighted and tabbed for exam preparation. These features are useful because the POL exam requires candidates to work across several technical subjects. A question involving concrete mix design may require a different book from a question involving shotcrete, excavation, reinforcing bars, or pool-code requirements.

The goal during preparation should be to learn which reference corresponds to each type of question. With 50 questions and 120 minutes available, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds per question. Excessive searching can quickly consume the available testing time.

Reference-navigation practice should include the table of contents, indexes, chapter headings, diagrams, tables, definitions, and permanent tabs. Candidates should also practice recognizing terminology that points toward a particular reference before beginning a search.

### Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing combines a contractor license class with one or more classifications or specialties. Swimming Pool Construction Contracting is identified by the specialty abbreviation POL.

1. **Determine the appropriate Virginia contractor license class.** Contractor businesses may apply for Class A, Class B, or Class C depending on the work they intend to perform.
2. **Select Swimming Pool Construction Contracting.** The applicable specialty designation is POL.
3. **Identify the Qualified Individual.** The contractor business must designate an individual who qualifies the company for the Swimming Pool Construction specialty.
4. **Meet the applicable experience requirement.** The Qualified Individual must document experience appropriate to the requested specialty and contractor class.
5. **Submit the required qualification information.** Experience documentation is required for specialties that involve examination approval.
6. **Receive approval for the POL examination.** Candidates must be approved before scheduling the technical exam.
7. **Prepare for the technical examination.** Study excavation, reinforcing, concrete, shotcrete, pool equipment, safety, estimating, and the applicable code references.
8. **Complete the PSI specialty examination.** Candidates must satisfy the technical examination requirement for POL.
9. **Complete applicable pre-license education.** New Virginia contractor applicants must satisfy the Board's pre-license education requirement.
10. **Complete additional business requirements when applicable.** Class A and Class B contractor applicants have separate Designated Employee business examination requirements.
11. **Submit the contractor license application.** The business must satisfy the remaining requirements associated with the selected contractor class and specialty.

Passing the POL technical examination does not automatically issue a Virginia contractor license. Examination completion is one component of the broader licensing process for the contractor business.

### State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors licenses contractor businesses rather than issuing the contractor business license directly to the Qualified Individual. A contractor license includes both a class and one or more classifications or specialties.

The Qualified Individual must meet the experience requirements associated with the requested license class and specialty. Current minimum experience requirements are **one year for Class C**, **three years for Class B**, and **five years for Class A**.

Virginia's POL specialty covers construction, repair, improvement, and removal of **in-ground swimming pools**. The scope specifically excludes trade-related plumbing, electrical, backflow, and HVAC work. Contractors needing those functions must address the appropriate separate licensing or trade requirements.

The Commercial Building and Residential Building classifications and the Recreational Facility Contracting specialty can also provide authority for swimming pool construction work within their respective scopes.

New Virginia contractor applicants must also complete applicable pre-license education. Class A and Class B applicants have separate business examination requirements for the Designated Employee in addition to the technical examination completed by the Qualified Individual.

### Test Information and Study Materials

A productive POL study plan should begin with the examination outline and devote preparation time according to the number of questions in each category. Sitework and Excavating, Concrete, and Shotcrete should receive substantial attention because each category contains 10 questions.

**Sitework and Excavating** preparation should include excavation methods, soil and trench considerations, site layout, grading, underground work, job planning, and safe excavation practices. Candidates should connect practical field experience with the terminology and methods described in the excavation reference.

**Forming and Reinforcing** should include formwork principles, reinforcing steel placement, bar supports, ties, splices, structural preparation, and related reinforced-concrete construction concepts.

**Concrete** preparation should include materials, mixture design, batching, placement, curing, strength, durability, testing, and conditions that can affect concrete performance. Swimming pool shells depend heavily on concrete performance, making this an important exam subject.

**Shotcrete** preparation should include wet- and dry-mix concepts, equipment, materials, application, surface preparation, placement technique, curing, quality control, and construction practices associated with pneumatically applied concrete.

**Plan Reading and Estimating** requires candidates to understand drawings, dimensions, quantities, project information, and practical calculations. Candidates should practice interpreting construction information rather than relying only on memorized formulas.

**Pool Equipment, Including ADA** should include pool-related systems, equipment arrangements, accessibility considerations, and code requirements applicable to aquatic environments.

**Pool Access Safety and Security** should include barriers, access control, safety measures, and code-related provisions intended to reduce hazards around swimming pools.

Because the examination is open book, candidates should combine technical study with timed reference drills. Select a practice question, identify the subject, choose the correct reference, and locate the relevant information. Repeat this process until reference selection becomes more automatic.

When a practice question is missed, locate the supporting rule or technical explanation in the appropriate reference and review surrounding material. This approach strengthens both trade knowledge and reference-navigation ability.
