Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing involves requirements for both the contractor business and the individuals associated with the license. Candidates pursuing the Swimming Pool Construction Contracting specialty should coordinate their examination preparation with the broader licensing process.

  1. Determine the appropriate contractor license class. Virginia contractor businesses are licensed under Class A, Class B, or Class C based on the applicable requirements.
  2. Select the appropriate specialty. Swimming Pool Construction Contracting is identified by the specialty abbreviation POL.
  3. Designate the Qualified Individual. The contractor business must identify a Qualified Individual for each classification or specialty requested on the license.
  4. Meet the applicable experience requirements. The Qualified Individual must satisfy the experience requirements associated with the requested license class and specialty.
  5. Complete required pre-license education. Virginia requires eight hours of approved pre-license education for applicants seeking a new contractor license.
  6. Obtain approval for the technical examination. Swimming Pool Construction Contracting is among the specialties that require examination approval and successful completion of a technical exam.
  7. Prepare for and complete the examination. Candidates can use the technical books in this package as part of an organized study and reference-navigation routine.
  8. Complete the contractor application process. After satisfying the applicable requirements, the business submits the appropriate contractor licensing application to the Virginia Board for Contractors.

Passing a technical specialty examination does not by itself create a Virginia contractor license. The business must satisfy the complete set of requirements that applies to its license class, specialty, Qualified Individual, and organizational structure.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors licenses businesses engaged in construction, removal, repair, or improvement work on property owned by others. Virginia contractor licenses consist of a license class and one or more classifications or specialties. The class determines the permitted level of contracting activity, while the classification or specialty identifies the type of work the business may perform.

Swimming Pool Construction Contracting, abbreviated POL, covers construction, repair, improvement, or removal of in-ground swimming pools. Virginia regulations specifically state that trade-related plumbing, electrical, backflow, and HVAC work are not included within the POL specialty.

Every contractor business must designate an appropriate Qualified Individual for the classifications or specialties on the license. Swimming Pool Construction Contracting is included among the specialties for which examination pre-approval and successful completion of an approved technical examination are required.

Virginia also requires eight hours of pre-license education for new Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor license applicants. The pre-license education is a business-focused requirement addressing regulations, statutes, and responsibilities relevant to contractors. It is separate from technical preparation for the Swimming Pool Construction Contractor examination.
