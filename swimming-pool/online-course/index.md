### Open Book Test

The Virginia contractor specialty examinations administered through PSI are open book. Candidates must bring their own permitted reference materials because the books are not supplied at the test site.

PSI permits reference materials to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are allowed, while removable tabs are not permitted. References containing writing are not allowed, and candidates may not write in the books during the examination session.

These rules make advance preparation particularly important. Candidates should become familiar with every permitted reference before exam day. Large code books and technical manuals can contain hundreds of pages, so searching them without a strategy can consume valuable time.

A strong open-book method begins by identifying the subject of the question. Pool-code requirements may point toward the International Swimming Pool and Spa Code. Building-related provisions may lead to the International Building Code. Concrete mixture questions may be located in Design and Control of Concrete Mixtures, while shotcrete questions can direct candidates toward Guide to Shotcrete.

Questions involving reinforcing bars may require Placing Reinforcing Bars, while excavation and sitework concepts may be addressed through Pipe and Excavation Contracting. Pool operation or aquatic-system terminology may lead toward the Pool and Spa Operator Handbook.

The course helps candidates practice these connections so the books become familiar tools rather than unfamiliar references used only on test day.

### Licensing Steps

2. **Select the applicable specialty.** Swimming Pool Construction Contracting is identified by the abbreviation POL.

4. **Meet applicable experience requirements.** The Qualified Individual must satisfy the experience requirements associated with the license class and specialty being requested.
5. **Complete required pre-license education.** New contractor license applicants must complete eight hours of approved Virginia pre-license education through the appropriate member of the business.

8. **Complete the contractor license application.** After meeting the applicable requirements, the business submits the required application materials to the Virginia Board for Contractors.

Passing the technical examination does not by itself issue a contractor license. The business must satisfy all applicable licensing requirements before the Board can issue the requested Virginia contractor license.
