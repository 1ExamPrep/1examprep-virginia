Open Book Test

Virginia contractor examinations administered through PSI are open book. Candidates must bring their own permitted reference materials because the books are not provided at the testing site.

PSI permits approved references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Reference materials may be tabbed with permanent tabs. Removable tabs are not permitted. References containing writing are not allowed, and candidates may not write in the books during the examination.

This makes advance book preparation and navigation practice especially important. The highlighted and tabbed books in this package are organized to help candidates identify sections more efficiently while studying and preparing for the test.

An open-book examination still requires technical knowledge. Candidates should not expect to search every book from beginning to end for every question. Instead, study each reference enough to understand its purpose, organization, table of contents, index, terminology, and major chapters.

For example, a question about swimming pool code requirements may direct you toward the International Swimming Pool and Spa Code. A question involving concrete ingredients or performance may lead to Design and Control of Concrete Mixtures. Shotcrete concepts may point toward Guide to Shotcrete, while reinforcing-bar placement can lead toward Placing Reinforcing Bars. Excavation questions may be addressed through Pipe and Excavation Contracting.

Practicing those connections before the examination can help make the highlighted and tabbed references more useful when working under testing conditions.

Licensing Steps

Becoming licensed to perform Swimming Pool Construction Contracting work in Virginia involves more than completing the technical examination. Contractor licenses are issued to businesses, and the business must satisfy the requirements associated with its license class, specialty, Qualified Individual, and application.

  1. Select the appropriate Virginia contractor license class. Virginia issues contractor licenses as Class A, Class B, or Class C based on the applicable licensing requirements.
  2. Select Swimming Pool Construction Contracting. The specialty is identified by the abbreviation POL.
  3. Designate the Qualified Individual. A contractor business must identify a Qualified Individual for each classification or specialty requested on the license.
  4. Meet the experience requirement. The Qualified Individual must satisfy the applicable experience requirements for the license class being pursued.
  5. Complete required pre-license education. Applicants for a new Virginia contractor license must complete eight hours of approved pre-license education through the appropriate individual associated with the business.
  6. Obtain examination pre-approval. Swimming Pool Construction Contracting is among the specialties requiring pre-approval and successful completion of a technical examination.
  7. Prepare for and complete the examination. Use the approved technical references to build trade knowledge and practice open-book navigation.
  8. Complete the contractor license application. The business must submit the applicable licensing materials and satisfy the requirements established by the Virginia Board for Contractors.

Passing the technical examination is one part of the overall licensing process. It does not automatically issue a Virginia contractor license. Applicants must also complete the other requirements associated with their business and selected license class.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates contractor businesses operating within the Commonwealth. Every licensed contractor must identify a Qualified Individual for each classification or specialty listed on the license.

Virginia defines Swimming Pool Construction Contracting as the service that provides for the construction, repair, improvement, or removal of in-ground swimming pools. The specialty does not include trade-related plumbing, electrical, backflow, or HVAC work.

The current Virginia contractor application process requires Qualified Individuals to be either a full-time employee working a minimum of 30 hours per week with the business or a member of Responsible Management. Qualified Individuals must also meet the minimum experience requirements applicable to the license class.

The minimum experience requirement is one year for a Class C license, three years for a Class B license, and five years for a Class A license. An Experience Verification Form is required when a Qualified Individual is seeking a designation that requires pre-approval for an examination.

Virginia also requires eight hours of pre-license education for new contractor licenses. The pre-license course addresses contractor business and regulatory requirements rather than the technical details of swimming pool construction. Exam preparation for the POL specialty is therefore separate from the state's pre-license education requirement.

Practice questions should become reference-navigation exercises. Read a question, identify its main subject, determine which book is most likely to contain the information, locate the relevant section, and confirm the answer. Repeating this sequence helps candidates become more comfortable with the books and can improve efficiency during an open-book examination.
