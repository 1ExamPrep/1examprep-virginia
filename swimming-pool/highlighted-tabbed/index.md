### State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates contractor businesses operating within the Commonwealth. Every licensed contractor must identify a Qualified Individual for each classification or specialty listed on the license.

Virginia defines Swimming Pool Construction Contracting as the service that provides for the construction, repair, improvement, or removal of in-ground swimming pools. The specialty does not include trade-related plumbing, electrical, backflow, or HVAC work.

The current Virginia contractor application process requires Qualified Individuals to be either a full-time employee working a minimum of 30 hours per week with the business or a member of Responsible Management. Qualified Individuals must also meet the minimum experience requirements applicable to the license class.

The minimum experience requirement is one year for a Class C license, three years for a Class B license, and five years for a Class A license. An Experience Verification Form is required when a Qualified Individual is seeking a designation that requires pre-approval for an examination.

Virginia also requires eight hours of pre-license education for new contractor licenses. The pre-license course addresses contractor business and regulatory requirements rather than the technical details of swimming pool construction. Exam preparation for the POL specialty is therefore separate from the state's pre-license education requirement.
