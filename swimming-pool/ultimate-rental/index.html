Exam Details

The Virginia Swimming Pool Construction Contractor examination is the Qualified Individual technical examination associated with the POL specialty. Virginia requires the applicable Qualified Individual to meet experience requirements and receive approval before scheduling the specialty examination.

The POL examination contains 50 questions. Candidates receive 120 minutes to complete the test and must answer at least 35 questions correctly to achieve the required 70% passing score.

The examination content outline includes:

  • Plan Reading and Estimating: 3 questions
  • Sitework and Excavating: 10 questions
  • Forming and Reinforcing: 8 questions
  • Concrete: 10 questions
  • Shotcrete: 10 questions
  • Pool Equipment, Including ADA: 6 questions
  • Pool Access Safety and Security: 3 questions

Sitework and Excavating, Concrete, and Shotcrete are the three largest exam categories, with 10 questions each. These areas should receive substantial study time. Forming and Reinforcing is also significant, accounting for eight questions.

Candidates should be prepared to interpret drawings and project information, understand excavation and site preparation, review concrete materials and placement practices, recognize reinforcing-steel requirements, understand shotcrete construction, and study pool equipment and safety requirements.

Virginia uses PSI to administer Qualified Individual specialty examinations. The current Virginia Board for Contractors fee for a Qualified Individual specialty examination is $85, paid directly to PSI. This examination fee is separate from the Ultimate package cost.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Swimming Pool Construction Contractor examination is an open-book test. Candidates must bring their own permitted reference materials because the books are not supplied at the testing center.

The current POL exam reference rules allow permanent tabs. Removable tabs are not permitted. Candidates should therefore use their preparation period to become familiar with the books and develop an efficient system for locating information.

Some book(s) in this Ultimate package are highlighted and tabbed for exam preparation. Highlighting can draw attention to selected technical material, while permanent tabs can create navigation points for frequently studied subjects. These preparation features are most effective when candidates practice using them repeatedly before exam day.

With 50 questions and 120 minutes, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds per question. Efficient reference use matters. A candidate who searches several books for every answer can quickly lose testing time.

A stronger strategy is to identify the subject of the question first. Concrete questions may lead to Design and Control of Concrete Mixtures. Shotcrete questions may require the Guide to Shotcrete. Excavation questions may lead to Pipe and Excavation Contracting, while reinforcing questions can be supported by Placing Reinforcing Bars.

Questions involving swimming pool code requirements, barriers, equipment, access, or safety may require the International Swimming Pool and Spa Code. Broader building-code questions may require the International Building Code.

Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing combines a license class with one or more classifications or specialties. Swimming Pool Construction Contracting is identified by the specialty abbreviation POL.

  1. Determine the appropriate contractor license class. Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
  2. Select Swimming Pool Construction Contracting. The applicable specialty designation is POL.
  3. Identify the Qualified Individual. The contractor business must designate the person qualifying the company for the POL specialty.
  4. Meet the applicable experience requirement. The Qualified Individual must document sufficient work experience for the requested contractor class and specialty.
  5. Complete the required experience verification. POL is a specialty that requires examination approval.
  6. Receive approval for the examination. Candidates must complete the applicable qualification process before scheduling the technical test.
  7. Prepare for the POL examination. Use the included course, technical references, highlighted or tabbed study materials when applicable, and practice-oriented review.
  8. Complete the PSI specialty examination. The Qualified Individual must satisfy the POL technical examination requirement.
  9. Complete applicable pre-license education. New Virginia contractor applicants must satisfy the Board-approved pre-license education requirement.
  10. Complete additional Class A or Class B requirements when applicable. These business requirements are separate from the Qualified Individual technical examination.
  11. Complete the contractor application process. Application Service is included with this Ultimate package.

Application Service is included to support candidates through the applicable Virginia contractor licensing application process. Passing the POL examination alone does not automatically issue a contractor license. The contractor business must satisfy the other applicable licensing requirements.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates contractor businesses. Contractor licenses include both a license class and one or more classifications or specialties. The class determines the monetary level of contracting work the business may perform, while the specialty identifies the type of work.

The Qualified Individual must meet Virginia's eligibility and experience requirements. Current minimum experience requirements are one year for Class C, three years for Class B, and five years for Class A.

Virginia defines the POL specialty as construction, repair, improvement, or removal of in-ground swimming pools. The scope does not include trade-related plumbing, electrical, backflow, or HVAC work.

The Commercial Building, Residential Building, and Highway/Heavy classifications and the Recreational Facility Contracting specialty can also provide authority for swimming pool construction work within their applicable scopes.

New Virginia contractor applicants must satisfy the required pre-license education requirements. Class A and Class B applicants also have separate Designated Employee business examination requirements. Those requirements are different from the POL technical examination completed by the Qualified Individual.

The included NASCLA Virginia contractor guide and Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code provide additional resources for broader contractor business, law, project management, and state construction-regulation preparation.
