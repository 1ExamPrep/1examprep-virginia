### Exam Details

The Virginia Concrete Contracting examination is a technical specialty examination for candidates pursuing the Concrete Contracting specialty through the Virginia Board for Contractors. PSI administers the specialty examination for approved candidates.

- **Exam Name:** Concrete Contracting Examination
- **Specialty Abbreviation:** CEM
- **Number of Questions:** 50
- **Time Allowed:** 120 minutes
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 70%, or 35 correct answers
- **Exam Administrator:** PSI

The current published examination outline divides the 50 questions into six primary subject areas:

- **Estimating:** 5 questions
- **Layout and Excavation:** 10 questions
- **Formwork:** 6 questions
- **Reinforcing Steel:** 7 questions
- **General Concrete:** 16 questions
- **Safety:** 6 questions

General Concrete is the largest section of the exam, accounting for 16 of the 50 questions. Layout and Excavation is the second-largest content area with 10 questions. Together, those two subjects account for more than half of the scored examination questions, making them important areas for focused preparation.

Reinforcing Steel, Formwork, Safety, and Estimating make up the remainder of the exam. Candidates should build a balanced study plan instead of concentrating exclusively on concrete materials and placement. Form construction, reinforcing practices, excavation, quantities, and construction safety are all part of the technical knowledge expected for the CEM specialty.

### Open Book Test

The Virginia Concrete Contracting examination is an **open-book test**. Candidates must bring their own permitted references because books are not supplied at the test center.

### Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing is administered by the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation Board for Contractors. The Concrete Contracting specialty is identified as CEM and may be added to an appropriate Virginia contractor license when the applicable requirements are satisfied.

1. **Determine the appropriate contractor license class.** Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
2. **Select the Concrete Contracting specialty.** Candidates pursuing concrete contracting authority identify CEM as the applicable specialty.
3. **Identify the Qualified Individual.** The contractor business must designate a Qualified Individual for the specialty.
4. **Meet the applicable experience requirements.** The Qualified Individual must document the experience required for the requested contractor license class and specialty.
5. **Complete required pre-license education.** New Virginia contractor applicants must satisfy the applicable eight-hour pre-license education requirement.
6. **Obtain examination approval when required.** The Qualified Individual must satisfy the applicable eligibility requirements before scheduling the CEM examination.
7. **Schedule and take the Concrete Contracting examination through PSI.**
8. **Complete any additional examination requirements associated with the selected contractor license class.**
9. **Complete and submit the contractor license application and supporting documentation.**

Passing the Concrete Contracting technical examination is one component of the Virginia contractor licensing process. Successful examination completion does not by itself issue a contractor license.

### State Requirements

Virginia defines Concrete Contracting as work involving the processing, proportioning, batching, mixing, conveying, and placing of concrete composed of materials commonly used in the concrete industry.

The specialty includes finishing, coloring, curing, repairing, testing, sawing, grinding, grouting, placement of film barriers, sealing, and waterproofing. It also includes construction and assembly of forms, molds, slipforms, pans, centering, and the use of reinforcing steel.

Virginia also recognizes that the Commercial Building Contractor, Residential Building Contractor, and Highway/Heavy Contractor classifications provide authority for concrete work within the scope of those classifications.

Contractor applicants must satisfy the requirements applicable to their selected license class and business structure. These requirements can include a Qualified Individual, experience documentation, pre-license education, examinations, and the appropriate contractor license application.

## How does the Concrete Contractor exam work?

The Concrete Contractor exam is the technical portion of the license for that classification. A Class A or B firm usually also needs the business and law portion, and every applicant needs the 8-hour pre-license course.

- Virginia issues contractor licenses in three classes. Class C covers single jobs of $1,000 to under $10,000 and under $150,000 a year; Class B covers single jobs of $10,000 to under $120,000 and under $750,000 a year; Class A has no upper limit.
- Every applicant completes 8 hours of pre-license education before the license is issued.
- Class A and B firms name a designated employee or member of responsible management who passes the exams. PSI runs them, and the fee is $40 for one portion, $72 for two and $85 for three.
- The exams are open book. You bring your own references, which may be highlighted and permanently tabbed; removable flags and loose notes are not allowed.
