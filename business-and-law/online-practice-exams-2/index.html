Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing requirements depend on the license class, classification, specialty, and the type of work being performed. Candidates preparing for the Virginia Class A/B Business Exam should understand that the business exam is one part of the contractor licensing process.

The process begins with determining the appropriate Virginia contractor license class and classification for the work the applicant intends to perform. Class A and Class B contractor licensing involves business, financial, administrative, and regulatory responsibilities, along with the proper classification or specialty for the construction work being offered.

After selecting the proper license class and classification, candidates complete the required application and approval process for Virginia contractor licensing. Once approved for testing, candidates schedule the required exam through the approved testing process and prepare using the listed references.

Passing the Virginia Class A/B Business Exam is an important step, but contractor licensing can also include additional requirements outside the exam itself. Candidates may need to provide business information, demonstrate qualifications where required, meet financial or insurance requirements, complete additional examinations where applicable, and submit all required licensing documentation.

After licensure, contractors are responsible for operating within the scope of the license, maintaining proper business records, following Virginia laws and licensing requirements, managing contracts properly, maintaining insurance where required, supervising work responsibly, renewing the license as required, and conducting business professionally. Business exam preparation supports these responsibilities by reinforcing contractor management and compliance knowledge.
