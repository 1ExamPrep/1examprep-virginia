## How does the Class A/B Business and Law exam work?

The Class A/B Business and Law products cover the business side of a Virginia contractor license, which every Class A and B firm deals with no matter which trade classification it holds.

- Class A and B applicants pass the Virginia and general business portions, written to the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Virginia edition.
- Every Class A, B and C applicant completes 8 hours of pre-license education.
- PSI runs the exams; the fee is $40 for one portion, $72 for two and $85 for three.
- The exams are open book, with highlighting and permanent tabs allowed in your own references.

### Is the Class A/B Business and Law exam open book?

Yes. The Virginia exams PSI runs are open book. You bring your own references, which may be highlighted and permanently tabbed; removable flags and loose notes are not allowed.

### Who issues the Class A/B Business and Law license or certification in Virginia?

It comes from the Board for Contractors at the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation (DPOR). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

### Do I need the 8-hour pre-license course for a Class A/B Business and Law license?

Yes. Every Class A, B and C applicant completes 8 hours of pre-license education before the Board for Contractors issues the license. For Class A and B it is taken by the designated employee or a member of responsible management.

### What does the PSI exam cost?

PSI charges $40 for one portion, $72 for two portions and $85 for three, paid to PSI. The fee is separate from the license application fee the board charges.

### Are these the real exam questions?

No. Licensing exam questions are confidential. The courses and practice exams are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations that point to the reference section.
