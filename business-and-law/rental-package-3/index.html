When studying, begin with the subject rather than the book. Ask yourself what category the question belongs to, select the most appropriate reference, then use the index, table of contents, prepared tabs, or chapter headings to locate the relevant section.

Repeat this process until the relationship between subjects and references becomes familiar. The objective is to build a consistent navigation routine instead of searching several books whenever a topic appears unfamiliar.
