### Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing is administered by the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation through the Board for Contractors. Contractor licenses are issued to businesses and consist of a license class together with the applicable classification or specialty.

Applicants pursuing Vessel Construction Contracting must identify a qualified individual for the VCC specialty. Virginia requires qualified individuals for VCC to meet the applicable experience requirements and receive approval to take the specialty examination.

The minimum experience requirement depends on the contractor license class. Current Virginia contractor application requirements call for at least one year of applicable experience for a Class C qualified individual, three years for Class B, and five years for Class A.

The qualified individual must also have the required relationship with the contracting business. Virginia requires the qualified individual to be a full-time employee working at least 30 hours per week or a member of the business's responsible management.

After the applicable approval is obtained, the qualified individual completes the Vessel Construction Contracting examination through PSI. Successful completion of the required specialty examination satisfies the technical examination requirement associated with the VCC designation.

Virginia also requires eight hours of approved pre-license education for new contractor licenses. For Class A and Class B applicants, the education must be completed by a member of responsible management or the designated employee. For Class C applicants, a member of responsible management completes the required education.

Class A and Class B contractor applicants also have business examination requirements associated with the designated employee. These requirements are separate from the VCC specialty examination.

After the examination, education, experience, and other applicable requirements are satisfied, the contractor license application is completed and submitted to the Virginia Board for Contractors for review.

## How does the Vessel Contractor exam work?

The Vessel Contractor exam is the technical portion of the license for that classification. A Class A or B firm usually also needs the business and law portion, and every applicant needs the 8-hour pre-license course.

- Virginia issues contractor licenses in three classes. Class C covers single jobs of $1,000 to under $10,000 and under $150,000 a year; Class B covers single jobs of $10,000 to under $120,000 and under $750,000 a year; Class A has no upper limit.
