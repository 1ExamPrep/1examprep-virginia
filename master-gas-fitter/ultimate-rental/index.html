Exam Details

The Virginia Master Gasfitter credential is regulated through the Virginia Board for Contractors. Candidates must receive Board approval before taking the Master Gasfitter examination administered by PSI.

The examination covers both technical gas-fitting knowledge and Virginia regulatory material. Candidates should prepare for subjects involving natural and LP gas piping, pipe sizing, LP-gas tanks, gas piping controls, testing, standards of practice, standards of conduct, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, qualification requirements, and disciplinary provisions.

Gas piping and pipe sizing deserve substantial study. Candidates should understand the components of fuel gas piping systems and be able to work efficiently with applicable tables and technical requirements. Pipe-sizing questions require candidates to identify the relevant information and locate the appropriate table or provision before determining the answer.

LP-gas topics should also receive focused attention. Candidates should become familiar with requirements involving containers, tanks, piping, storage, installation locations, separation requirements, and related safety provisions. NFPA 58 serves as an important technical reference for these subjects.

Virginia-specific licensing and professional topics require a different type of preparation. Candidates should know how the individual licensing regulations are organized so they can efficiently locate requirements involving professional conduct, qualification, renewal, reinstatement, and related regulatory matters.

The Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation currently requires pre-approval from the Board before tradesman candidates may sit for an examination. The current Master Tradesman examination fee is paid directly to PSI.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Master Gasfitter examination is open book. Candidates must bring their own permitted references because the testing center does not supply examination books.

An open-book format does not remove the need for preparation. Candidates need enough technical knowledge to understand what a question is asking and enough familiarity with the books to know where the supporting information is likely to be located.

Gas piping, testing, controls, and pipe-sizing questions may require the International Fuel Gas Code, 2021. Licensing Steps

The Virginia Master Gasfitter license is an individual tradesman credential. Candidates must satisfy the applicable qualification and application requirements before receiving authorization to take the examination.

  1. Review the Master Gasfitter qualification requirements. Determine the education, experience, and licensing pathway that applies to your background.
  2. Document the required qualifications. Applicants must provide the information requested by the Virginia Board for Contractors to demonstrate eligibility.
  3. Submit the applicable application. The Board reviews the candidate’s application and determines examination eligibility.
  4. Receive approval to test. Pre-approval from the Virginia Board for Contractors is required before the examination can be scheduled.
  5. Schedule the examination through PSI. Once approved, candidates can complete the applicable examination registration process.
  6. Prepare with the applicable references. Study Virginia regulations, the International Fuel Gas Code, and NFPA 58 while practicing technical and regulatory questions.
  7. Complete the Master Gasfitter examination. Candidates must meet the applicable passing requirement.
  8. Complete remaining licensing requirements. Candidates must satisfy all remaining Board requirements before the individual credential is issued.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates individual tradesmen engaged in gas fitting. The Master Gasfitter license is issued to an individual and is separate from a Virginia contractor business license.

Virginia contractor businesses are licensed separately and may hold Class A, Class B, or Class C contractor licenses. The contractor license class determines the level of contracting activity permitted, while the classification or specialty determines the type of work the business may perform.

For a contractor business seeking the Gas Fitting specialty, the Qualified Individual must hold the applicable valid Master Gas Fitter tradesman credential. The business must still satisfy the separate contractor licensing requirements that apply to its class and specialty.

Virginia also requires applicable contractor businesses to complete pre-license education. New Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor applicants are subject to an eight-hour pre-license education requirement. This business requirement is separate from the individual Master Gasfitter technical examination.

How does the Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
