Exam Details

The Virginia Master Gasfitter credential is regulated by the Virginia Board for Contractors as an individual tradesman license. Candidates must meet the applicable qualification requirements and receive Board approval before sitting for the examination.

The Master Gasfitter examination evaluates both Virginia regulatory knowledge and technical gas-fitting subjects. Candidates should prepare for areas involving standards of practice, standards of conduct, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, qualifications for licensure, natural and LP gas piping, pipe sizing, LP-gas tanks, gas piping controls, and testing.

Gas piping and pipe sizing deserve substantial attention during study because they involve both technical understanding and the ability to work efficiently with code tables. Candidates should practice identifying the information provided in a question, selecting the appropriate sizing method or table, and interpreting the applicable values correctly.

LP-gas systems are another important area of preparation. Candidates should develop familiarity with requirements involving tanks, containers, storage, piping, installation locations, clearances, and safety provisions. NFPA 58 serves as an important reference for these subjects.

Virginia regulatory topics should also be included in the study plan. The individual licensing regulations address professional and administrative requirements that apply to licensed tradesmen, making regulatory navigation an important part of exam preparation.

Licensing Steps

The Virginia Master Gasfitter license is an individual tradesman credential regulated through the Virginia Board for Contractors. Candidates must satisfy applicable qualification, application, and examination requirements before the license is issued.

  1. Review the Master Gasfitter qualification requirements. Determine the education, training, experience, and licensing pathway that applies to your background.
  2. Document the required qualifications. Applicants must provide the information requested by the Virginia Board for Contractors to demonstrate eligibility.
  3. Submit the applicable application. The Board reviews the candidate’s application and qualification information.
  4. Receive approval to test. Virginia requires Board pre-approval before candidates may sit for the Master Gasfitter examination.
  5. Schedule the examination. After approval, candidates can complete the applicable examination registration and scheduling process.
  6. Prepare with the applicable references. Study the Virginia licensing regulations, 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, and NFPA 58 while developing reference-navigation skills.
  7. Complete the required examination. Candidates must satisfy the applicable examination requirement for Master-level licensure.
  8. Complete remaining Board requirements. After successfully meeting the examination requirement, candidates must satisfy any remaining licensing requirements before credential issuance.

Candidates planning to use the Master Gasfitter credential in connection with a contracting business should understand that the individual tradesman license and the Virginia contractor business license are separate credentials. A contractor business must independently satisfy the requirements that apply to its license class and specialty.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates individual tradesmen engaged in gas fitting. The Master Gasfitter credential is issued to an individual rather than directly to a contracting company.

Virginia separately regulates contractor businesses through Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses. Businesses must obtain the appropriate classification or specialty for the type of contracting services they perform.

For a contractor business seeking the Gas Fitting specialty, identified as GFC, the Qualified Individual must hold a valid Master Gas Fitter tradesman license issued by the Virginia Board for Contractors. The business must still satisfy all separate contractor licensing requirements applicable to its class and specialty.

Virginia also has pre-license education requirements for contractor businesses. New Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor applicants must complete the required contractor pre-license education. This business licensing requirement is separate from preparation for the individual Master Gasfitter examination.

The inclusion of the NASCLA Contractor’s Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Virginia 11th Edition and the Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code, 2021 Edition, USBC gives candidates additional resources for broader Virginia contractor preparation beyond the technical Master Gasfitter material.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Virginia Master Gasfitter study plan should combine technical review with direct use of the references. Candidates can divide preparation into regulatory topics, gas piping, pipe sizing, LP-gas systems, controls, and testing rather than attempting to study all subjects at once.

For fuel gas piping, candidates should work extensively with the International Fuel Gas Code, 2021. Review piping materials, installation methods, fittings, valves, regulators, appliance connections, system protection, and other requirements that apply to fuel gas systems.

Pipe sizing should be practiced repeatedly. Candidates should learn to identify the information given in a problem, choose the appropriate sizing table, follow the table correctly, and confirm that the selected pipe size meets the applicable requirements.

LP-gas study should include direct navigation of NFPA 58. Review requirements for tanks, containers, storage, piping, location, protection, separation distances, and related safety provisions. Knowing which information belongs in NFPA 58 can make reference searches more efficient.

Gas piping controls and testing should also receive focused review. Candidates should understand how controls function within gas systems and how applicable testing requirements help verify safe installation and operation.

The additional Virginia business, law, project management, and state code references support broader contractor preparation. These books are particularly useful for candidates who plan to connect their individual Master Gasfitter credential with a Virginia contracting business.

How does the Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
