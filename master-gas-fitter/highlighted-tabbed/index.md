### Open Book Test

The Virginia Master Gasfitter examination is open book. Candidates must bring their own permitted references because reference materials are not provided at the testing site.

PSI permits examination references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are permitted. Removable tabs are not allowed. Reference materials containing writing are not permitted, and candidates may not write in reference books during the examination session.

Study guides are not allowed in the examination room. Loose papers and unbound reference materials are also prohibited. Downloaded references must meet PSI binding requirements before they can be used during testing.

### State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates individual tradesmen working in gas fitting along with other regulated trades. The Master Gasfitter credential is an individual license rather than the contractor business license itself.

Businesses seeking a Virginia contractor license for Gas Fitting must separately meet the requirements that apply to the contractor business license. Virginia contractor businesses are licensed as Class A, Class B, or Class C and must hold the appropriate classification or specialty for the work they perform.

For the Gas Fitting contractor specialty, Virginia requires the Qualified Individual to hold a valid Master Gas-Fitter Tradesman License. This makes the individual Master Gasfitter credential an important qualification for contractors pursuing the GFC specialty.

Individual tradesmen and contractor businesses follow different application structures. Candidates should distinguish between earning the individual Master Gasfitter license and obtaining a contractor business license. Holding the Master Gasfitter credential does not by itself replace the separate business licensing requirements that apply to a contractor firm.

Virginia also requires continuing education for licensed gas fitters. Current Board requirements call for one hour of approved continuing education for Gas Fitters during each three-year renewal period. The continuing education course must cover the International Fuel Gas Code and must be provided through a Board-approved education provider.

## How does the Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
