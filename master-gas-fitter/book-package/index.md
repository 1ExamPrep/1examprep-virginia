### Open Book Test

### State Requirements

Virginia distinguishes between an individual tradesman license and a contractor business license. The Master Gasfitter credential is issued to an individual. A Class A, Class B, or Class C contractor license is issued to a business entity or sole proprietor.

For a Virginia contractor business seeking the Gas Fitting specialty, identified as GFC, the Qualified Individual must hold a valid Master Gas-Fitter Tradesman License issued by the Virginia Board for Contractors. This means the Master Gasfitter license can satisfy an important qualification requirement for the individual designated by a contractor business for the Gas Fitting specialty.

The business must still meet the separate requirements for contractor licensing. A Master Gasfitter license alone does not create a contractor business license or replace the requirements applying to the business entity.

Virginia contractor businesses are licensed by class and classification or specialty. The class determines the level of contracting activity allowed under the license, while the classification or specialty determines the type of work the business may perform.

Licensed Virginia Gas Fitters are also subject to continuing education requirements for renewal. Gas Fitters must complete one hour of Board-approved continuing education during each three-year renewal period. The continuing education requirement must be satisfied before the applicable license renewal, and the gas-fitting course must address the International Fuel Gas Code.

Licensing regulations can also address professional conduct, standards of practice, renewal and reinstatement, qualifications, and disciplinary matters. This is why the *Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations* is an important part of Master Gasfitter exam preparation rather than simply a general licensing reference.

## How does the Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
