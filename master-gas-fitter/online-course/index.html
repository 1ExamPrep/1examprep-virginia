Exam Details

The Virginia Master Gasfitter credential is regulated through the Virginia Board for Contractors. Candidates must receive Board approval before sitting for the applicable examination administered through PSI.

The Journeyman/Master Gasfitter examination portion contains 50 questions. Master-level candidates must answer at least 37 questions correctly. Candidates are provided 150 minutes to complete the examination.

The examination combines Virginia regulatory material with technical gas-fitting subjects. Areas of preparation include:

  • Standards of Practice
  • Standards of Conduct
  • Renewal and Reinstatement
  • Definitions
  • Qualification for Licensure
  • Revocation of Licensure
  • Natural and LP Gas Piping
  • Pipe Sizing
  • LP-Gas Tanks
  • Gas Piping Controls
  • Testing

Technical preparation should place strong emphasis on gas piping systems and pipe sizing. Candidates should understand how to identify applicable piping requirements, locate sizing tables, interpret table information, and apply the appropriate technical provisions to gas system questions.

LP-gas preparation is also important. Candidates should become familiar with requirements governing tanks, containers, piping, storage, location, installation, and related safety considerations. These subjects are supported by NFPA 58 and should be studied directly from the reference.

Virginia regulatory questions require a different type of preparation. Candidates should know how the licensing regulations are organized and where to locate information involving professional conduct, standards of practice, qualification requirements, renewal, reinstatement, and disciplinary matters.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Master Gasfitter examination is open book. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own permitted reference materials to the testing location because examination books are not provided by the testing center.

Open-book testing rewards candidates who know how their references are organized. A candidate who recognizes that a question involves LP-gas tank placement can move directly toward NFPA 58. A question involving gas piping sizing may lead to the International Fuel Gas Code. A question about renewal, professional conduct, or qualification can point toward the Virginia regulations.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination in accordance with PSI testing rules. Permanent tabs may be used. Removable tabs are not permitted. Candidates should prepare and maintain their books according to the current testing requirements.

The purpose of reference-navigation practice is not simply to find answers during study. It is to create a repeatable process that can be used under testing conditions. Students should practice reading a question, identifying the subject, choosing the appropriate reference, locating the applicable section or table, and confirming the answer.

With 150 minutes for 50 questions, candidates have an average of three minutes per question. Some questions may be answered from trade knowledge, while others require a code or regulation lookup. Practicing with the books before exam day can help candidates make better decisions about when a reference search is necessary.

Licensing Steps

The Virginia Master Gasfitter credential is an individual tradesman license. Candidates must satisfy the applicable Virginia qualification, application, and examination requirements before the license can be issued.

  1. Review the Virginia qualification requirements. Determine which applicable education, training, and experience pathway applies to your background.
  2. Document the required qualifications. Candidates must provide the information required by the Virginia Board for Contractors to demonstrate eligibility for Master-level licensure.
  3. Submit the applicable application. The Board reviews the candidate’s materials before granting permission to sit for the examination.
  4. Receive Board approval. Pre-approval from the Virginia Board for Contractors is required before examination scheduling.
  5. Schedule the examination with PSI. Once approved, candidates can follow the testing provider’s scheduling procedures.
  6. Prepare with the applicable references. Study Virginia regulations, the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, and NFPA 58 while practicing technical and regulatory questions.
  7. Take the Master Gasfitter examination. Candidates must achieve the applicable Master-level passing score.
  8. Complete remaining license requirements. After successful examination completion, candidates must satisfy the remaining Board requirements before license issuance.

Candidates who plan to use the Master Gasfitter credential as part of a contractor business should also understand that the individual tradesman license and the contractor business license are separate credentials. A contractor business must independently meet the requirements that apply to its license class and specialty.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates individual tradesmen, including Gas Fitters. Pre-approval from the Board is required before candidates may sit for the examination.

The Master Gasfitter license is an individual professional credential. A Virginia contractor license, by comparison, is issued to a business entity or sole proprietor. Businesses seeking to perform regulated gas fitting contracting work must satisfy the separate requirements applying to Virginia contractor licensing.

For the Virginia Gas Fitting contractor specialty, a valid Master Gas-Fitter Tradesman License serves as the required individual credential for the Qualified Individual associated with that specialty. The contractor business must still complete its own licensing requirements.

Virginia also requires continuing education for Gas Fitters as a condition of license renewal. Current requirements call for one hour of Board-approved continuing education during each three-year renewal period. The continuing education course for Gas Fitters must cover the International Fuel Gas Code.

Continuing education is separate from an exam preparation course. Exam preparation is designed to help candidates prepare for the initial licensing examination, while continuing education must meet Board approval requirements to count toward an existing license renewal.

Candidates should also become familiar with Virginia regulations covering standards of practice and standards of conduct. Professional licensure involves both technical competence and compliance with the rules governing licensed tradesmen.

Test Information and Study Materials

Effective Master Gasfitter exam preparation should combine technical instruction with frequent use of the reference books. Candidates can begin by separating the exam material into regulatory topics, gas piping, pipe sizing, LP-gas systems, controls, and testing.

For Virginia regulatory preparation, work directly with the Individual License and Certification Regulations. Review the sections dealing with standards of practice, professional conduct, renewal and reinstatement, qualification, definitions, and disciplinary matters. Practice locating specific provisions rather than relying entirely on memorization.

Pipe sizing should be practiced repeatedly using the International Fuel Gas Code, 2021. Candidates should become comfortable determining which table applies, identifying the variables needed for the calculation or table lookup, and interpreting the resulting information correctly.

Gas piping controls and testing should also be included in regular study sessions. Candidates should understand the purposes of control devices and know where the applicable installation and testing requirements are located in the technical references.

A useful study method is to create topic-based practice sessions. One session may focus on pipe sizing, another on LP-gas tanks, and another on Virginia licensing rules. After developing comfort with individual areas, candidates can use mixed practice questions to improve their ability to recognize topics quickly.

Timed practice is also valuable. With 150 minutes available for 50 examination questions, candidates should develop a consistent pace. Practice identifying questions that can be answered quickly and recognizing those that require a reference lookup.

Reference navigation and technical knowledge should reinforce each other. Understanding the trade helps candidates recognize the correct terminology, while knowledge of the reference books helps them confirm technical details. Is the Virginia Master Gasfitter exam open book?

Yes. The Virginia Master Gasfitter examination is open book. Candidates must bring their own permitted references and follow the applicable testing rules for how books may be prepared and used.

How many questions are on the Virginia Master Gasfitter examination?

The Journeyman/Master Gasfitter examination portion contains 50 questions. Master-level candidates must answer at least 37 correctly.

How much time is allowed for the examination?

Candidates are allowed 150 minutes to complete the Journeyman/Master Gasfitter examination portion.

What subjects are covered on the Master Gasfitter exam?

The examination includes Virginia licensing regulations, standards of practice, standards of conduct, renewal and reinstatement, qualification for licensure, natural and LP gas piping, pipe sizing, LP-gas tanks, gas piping controls, and testing.

Which reference books are used for preparation?

The primary references included for this course are the Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations, the International Fuel Gas Code, 2021, and NFPA 58 - Standard for Liquid Petroleum Gas Code, 2014.

Why is pipe-sizing practice important?

Pipe sizing is an important technical portion of Master Gasfitter preparation. Candidates should know how to select and interpret the appropriate sizing tables and identify the information required to use them correctly.

Does the online exam prep course replace the required Virginia continuing education?

No. Virginia continuing education used for license renewal must be completed through a Board-approved continuing education course. Exam preparation and license-renewal continuing education serve different purposes.

Does Virginia require continuing education for Gas Fitters?

Yes. Virginia requires one hour of Board-approved continuing education for Gas Fitters during each three-year renewal period. The applicable course must cover the International Fuel Gas Code.

Do I need Virginia Board approval before scheduling the exam?

Yes. Pre-approval from the Virginia Board for Contractors is required before candidates may sit for the Master Gasfitter examination.

Does a Master Gasfitter license automatically create a Virginia contractor license?

No. The Master Gasfitter credential is an individual tradesman license. A contractor business must separately satisfy Virginia contractor licensing requirements when operating as a licensed contractor.

How should I use the reference books while studying?

Use the references as part of regular practice. Identify the subject of each question, choose the appropriate book, locate the applicable section or table, and confirm the answer. Repeating this process helps build familiarity for open-book testing.

How does the Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
