### Open Book Test

The Virginia Master Gasfitter examination is open book. Candidates must bring their own permitted reference materials because books are not provided at the testing location.

Open-book testing makes reference navigation an important part of preparation. Candidates should learn how to recognize which reference applies to a particular question before beginning a search. A Virginia licensing question may direct the candidate toward the *Individual License and Certification Regulations*. A gas piping or pipe-sizing question may point toward the *International Fuel Gas Code, 2021*. An LP-gas tank or storage question may require *NFPA 58*.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination according to PSI testing rules. Permanent tabs may be used. Removable tabs are not permitted. Candidates should follow the current testing requirements when preparing any references they intend to bring into the exam.

The open-book format should not be treated as a substitute for studying. Candidates have 150 minutes to answer 50 questions, so excessive searching can consume valuable testing time. Effective preparation involves understanding the technical subject and knowing where supporting information is located.

A useful study routine is to identify the topic, select the correct reference, find the applicable chapter, table, or section, and confirm the information. Repeating this process during both live training and self-study can help candidates become more comfortable with the books before exam day.

### State Requirements

Virginia distinguishes between an individual tradesman license and a contractor business license. The Master Gasfitter credential is issued to an individual. A contractor business license is separately issued to a business entity or sole proprietor.

A valid Master Gas-Fitter Tradesman License is an important qualification for a contractor business seeking the Virginia Gas Fitting specialty. Virginia contractor regulations require the Qualified Individual associated with Gas Fitting contracting to hold the applicable Master-level credential.

The contracting business must still satisfy the separate requirements applicable to its selected license class and specialty. Earning the Master Gasfitter license does not automatically create a contractor business license.

Virginia also requires continuing education for licensed Gas Fitters. Current requirements call for one hour of Board-approved continuing education during each applicable three-year renewal period. The continuing education course for Gas Fitters must cover the International Fuel Gas Code.

Continuing education is separate from exam preparation. A Master Gasfitter exam prep program is designed to help candidates study for the licensing examination, while renewal continuing education must satisfy the Board's separate provider and course requirements.

## How does the Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.
