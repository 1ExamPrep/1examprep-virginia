Exam Details

The Virginia Master Gasfitter credential is regulated through the Virginia Board for Contractors. Candidates must receive pre-approval from the Board before sitting for the examination administered through PSI.

The Journeyman/Master Gasfitter examination portion contains 50 items. Master-level candidates must answer at least 37 questions correctly, and candidates are allowed 150 minutes to complete this portion of the examination.

The examination content is divided among the following areas:

  • Standards of Practice: 3 items
  • Standards of Conduct: 2 items
  • Renewal and Reinstatement: 2 items
  • Definitions: 1 item
  • Qualification for Licensure: 1 item
  • Revocation of Licensure: 1 item
  • Natural and LP Gas Piping: 12 items
  • Pipe Sizing: 11 items
  • LP-Gas Tanks: 9 items
  • Gas Piping Controls: 5 items
  • Testing: 3 items

The exam outline shows why technical preparation should focus heavily on gas piping, pipe sizing, and LP-gas systems. Together, these subjects represent a significant portion of the examination. Candidates should also prepare for the Virginia regulatory subjects because professional standards, qualification requirements, renewal, and disciplinary matters contribute to the overall score.

The current Master Tradesman examination fee is $125 and is paid directly to PSI after the candidate has received the required approval to test.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Master Gasfitter examination is open book. Candidates must bring their own permitted reference materials because books are not provided at the testing location.

Open-book testing makes reference navigation an important part of preparation. Candidates should learn how to recognize which reference applies to a particular question before beginning a search. A Virginia licensing question may direct the candidate toward the Individual License and Certification Regulations. A gas piping or pipe-sizing question may point toward the International Fuel Gas Code, 2021. An LP-gas tank or storage question may require NFPA 58.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination according to PSI testing rules. Permanent tabs may be used. Removable tabs are not permitted. Candidates should follow the current testing requirements when preparing any references they intend to bring into the exam.

The open-book format should not be treated as a substitute for studying. Candidates have 150 minutes to answer 50 questions, so excessive searching can consume valuable testing time. Effective preparation involves understanding the technical subject and knowing where supporting information is located.

A useful study routine is to identify the topic, select the correct reference, find the applicable chapter, table, or section, and confirm the information. Repeating this process during both live training and self-study can help candidates become more comfortable with the books before exam day.

Licensing Steps

The Virginia Master Gasfitter license is an individual tradesman credential regulated through the Virginia Board for Contractors. Candidates must satisfy the applicable qualification requirements before receiving authorization to take the examination.

  1. Review the Master Gasfitter qualification requirements. Candidates should determine which current education and experience pathway applies to their background.
  2. Document the required qualifications. Virginia requires candidates to provide evidence demonstrating that the applicable Master-level qualification standard has been met.
  3. Submit the required application. The Virginia Board for Contractors reviews the application before examination approval is granted.
  4. Receive Board approval. Pre-approval is required before the candidate may schedule the Master Gasfitter examination.
  5. Schedule the examination with PSI. After receiving approval, candidates can complete the applicable PSI registration and scheduling process.
  6. Prepare with the required references. Candidates should work directly with the Virginia regulations, 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, and NFPA 58 while studying.
  7. Complete the Master Gasfitter examination. Candidates must achieve the Master-level passing requirement.
  8. Complete remaining licensing requirements. After meeting the examination requirement, candidates must satisfy the remaining Board requirements before the credential is issued.

Current Virginia regulations provide multiple qualification pathways for Master tradesmen. One pathway allows an applicant who has held a valid Journeyman license in the applicable trade for at least one year to qualify for the Master examination. Another pathway is based on nine years of practical experience in the trade.

State Requirements

Virginia distinguishes between an individual tradesman license and a contractor business license. The Master Gasfitter credential is issued to an individual. A contractor business license is separately issued to a business entity or sole proprietor.

A valid Master Gas-Fitter Tradesman License is an important qualification for a contractor business seeking the Virginia Gas Fitting specialty. Virginia contractor regulations require the Qualified Individual associated with Gas Fitting contracting to hold the applicable Master-level credential.

The contracting business must still satisfy the separate requirements applicable to its selected license class and specialty. Earning the Master Gasfitter license does not automatically create a contractor business license.

Virginia also requires continuing education for licensed Gas Fitters. Current requirements call for one hour of Board-approved continuing education during each applicable three-year renewal period. The continuing education course for Gas Fitters must cover the International Fuel Gas Code.

Continuing education is separate from exam preparation. A Master Gasfitter exam prep program is designed to help candidates study for the licensing examination, while renewal continuing education must satisfy the Board's separate provider and course requirements.

Reference Books

  • Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations
    This Virginia regulatory reference addresses requirements governing individual tradesman licensing and certification. Master Gasfitter candidates should use it to review standards of practice, standards of conduct, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, qualification requirements, and disciplinary provisions. Familiarity with the regulations can help candidates locate state-specific information efficiently during the open-book examination.
  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2021
    The 2021 International Fuel Gas Code is a primary technical reference for fuel gas systems. It includes provisions involving piping materials, installation, sizing, appliances, valves, regulators, controls, combustion and ventilation considerations, testing, and related gas-system requirements. Candidates should become familiar with the code's tables, chapters, definitions, and index.
  • NFPA 58 - Standard for Liquid Petroleum Gas Code, 2014
    NFPA 58 addresses liquefied petroleum gas systems, including containers, tanks, piping, storage, transfer, installation locations, and safety requirements. Candidates should practice locating LP-gas provisions directly within the standard so that tank, container, and piping questions can be approached efficiently.

LP-gas tank preparation should involve direct work with NFPA 58. Review tank and container requirements, installation locations, separation requirements, storage, piping, protection, and related safety provisions. Knowing that a question belongs in NFPA 58 can prevent unnecessary searching through another reference.

Gas piping controls should also be part of the study plan. Candidates should understand the role of regulators, valves, shutoff devices, and other system controls and know where applicable requirements are located within the technical references.

Testing is another defined examination topic. Candidates should review requirements and procedures associated with testing gas piping systems and become comfortable locating the applicable provisions within the fuel gas code.

The Virginia regulatory topics should not be neglected. Standards of practice, standards of conduct, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, qualification for licensure, and revocation requirements collectively represent part of the examination. Candidates should practice locating these provisions within the Virginia regulations.

How does the Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Master Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.
