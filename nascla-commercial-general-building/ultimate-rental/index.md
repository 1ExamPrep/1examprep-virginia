### Exam Details

The **NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors** is a standardized commercial construction trade examination accepted by participating licensing agencies. The Virginia Board for Contractors is a participating state agency and accepts the NASCLA commercial examination for the comparable Virginia Commercial Building classification.

- **Exam:** NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors
- **Number of Questions:** 115
- **Time Allowed:** 330 minutes
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 70%, requiring 81 correct answers
- **Virginia Application:** Accepted for the comparable Commercial Building classification

The NASCLA examination covers a wide commercial construction scope. Candidates should prepare for General Requirements, Site Construction, Concrete, Masonry, Metals, Wood, Thermal and Moisture Protection, Doors, Windows and Glazing, Finishes, Mechanical and Plumbing Systems, Electrical Systems, and Procurement and Contracting Requirements.

Because these subjects span both technical construction knowledge and project-management responsibilities, preparation should not be limited to one trade. ### Licensing Steps

1. **Determine the Virginia contractor license class.** Virginia contractor businesses are licensed as Class A, Class B, or Class C according to the applicable licensing requirements.
2. **Select the applicable classification or specialty.** The applicant must identify the classification or specialty corresponding to the construction work the business intends to perform.
3. **Identify the Qualified Individual.** Virginia requires a Qualified Individual for each classification or specialty on the contractor license.
4. **Meet the Qualified Individual experience requirement.** The amount of experience required depends on the license class. Current Virginia requirements call for one year for Class C, three years for Class B, and five years for Class A.
5. **Satisfy the applicable trade examination requirement.** For the comparable Commercial Building classification, the NASCLA Accredited Examination for Commercial General Building Contractors may be used.
6. **Identify the Designated Employee for Class A or Class B.** Class A and Class B applicants must designate an eligible individual who satisfies the applicable business examination requirement.
7. **Complete the Designated Employee examination.** Class B applicants require the General and Virginia examination portions. Class A applicants require the General, Virginia, and Advanced portions.
8. **Complete the required pre-license education.** New contractor applicants must have the applicable individual complete an approved eight-hour contractor pre-license business course.
9. **Prepare and submit the contractor license application.** Provide the required business, examination, qualifying-individual, financial, and supporting documentation to the Virginia Board for Contractors.

### State Requirements

Contractor licensing in Virginia is administered by the **Virginia Board for Contractors** within the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation. Contractor licenses are issued to business entities and sole proprietors rather than simply to the individual who passes an examination.

Every contractor business must identify a **Qualified Individual** for each classification or specialty listed on the application. The Qualified Individual must be a full-time employee or a member of Responsible Management and must meet the applicable experience and prerequisite requirements for the requested classification.

Virginia's current contractor requirements specify minimum experience based on the license class. A Class C Qualified Individual must generally demonstrate at least one year of experience, Class B requires at least three years, and Class A requires at least five years in the applicable classification or specialty.

Class A and Class B applicants must also identify a **Designated Employee**. The Designated Employee must be at least 18 years old and must be a full-time employee or a member of Responsible Management. The individual must complete the business examination required for the applicable license class.

For Class B, the Designated Employee examination consists of the Virginia and General portions. For Class A, the examination includes the Virginia, General, and Advanced portions. These business examinations are separate from the trade examination used for the contractor classification.

Virginia also requires pre-license education for new contractor licenses. The required eight-hour business course must be completed by a member of Responsible Management or the Designated Employee for a Class A or Class B applicant. For a Class C applicant, a member of Responsible Management completes the course.

The NASCLA Commercial General Building Contractor examination can satisfy the applicable trade-exam requirement for the comparable Commercial Building classification, but passing NASCLA alone does not issue a Virginia contractor license. The business must still satisfy the license-class, Qualified Individual, Designated Employee, business examination, pre-license education, financial, and application requirements that apply.
