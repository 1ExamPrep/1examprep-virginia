### Exam Details

The ICC Commercial Mechanical Inspector M2 exam is a 50-question multiple-choice exam with a 2-hour time limit. The exam is based on commercial mechanical inspection duties and requires candidates to locate and apply code provisions from the listed mechanical and fuel gas references. The exam is not a general HVAC service test. It is a code inspection exam focused on determining whether commercial mechanical installations comply with the applicable code requirements, approved plans, manufacturer installation instructions, and related inspection criteria.

The exam content includes General Administration, Heating/Cooling/Water Heaters, Exhaust and Ventilation Systems, Duct Systems, Combustion Air, Chimneys and Vents, and Fuel Supply Systems. These areas reflect the practical knowledge a commercial mechanical inspector needs when reviewing installed mechanical systems in commercial buildings.

General Administration includes project administration, public information and legal responsibilities, plan reading, and manufacturer installation instructions. Students should be ready to identify whether required permits are valid, whether approved plans and specifications are available, and whether equipment is installed according to code requirements and manufacturer instructions.

Heating/Cooling/Water Heaters includes heating equipment, water heaters, boilers, cooling and refrigeration, heat pumps, solar thermal systems, and other fuel-burning mechanical systems. This area requires students to understand access, clearances, equipment installation, listing requirements, return air, supply air, duct connections, refrigeration rooms, and other inspection points connected to commercial mechanical equipment.

Exhaust and Ventilation Systems is one of the most important content areas on the M2 exam. It includes ventilation systems, commercial hood and kitchen ventilation, hazardous vapor exhaust, emergency exhaust, and product-conveying ventilation systems. Candidates should be comfortable reviewing ventilation requirements, exhaust system installation, hood clearances, filter requirements, fan and motor requirements, duct construction, terminations, and safety-related provisions.

Duct Systems includes duct construction and installation, duct and plenum use, smoke dampers, fire dampers, volume dampers, support, insulation, materials, and proper application of supply and return air ducts. Students preparing for the ICC M2 exam should spend time learning how the code organizes duct requirements and how to move quickly between duct construction rules, equipment connections, and fire and smoke control provisions.

Combustion Air, Chimneys and Vents, and Fuel Supply Systems are also essential exam areas. These topics include air supply for fuel-burning appliances, combustion air openings, combustion air ducts, venting systems, chimney and vent connectors, gas piping materials, gas piping support, gas piping sizing, tightness testing, fittings, valves, connections, and material transitions. These subjects often require careful reading because the exam may test the difference between similar terms, installation conditions, or system types.

## How does the Commercial Mechanical Inspector (M2) exam work?

The Commercial Mechanical Inspector (M2) exam is a code official certification. It is taken after the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class and leads to DHCD certification.

- Virginia code officials are certified by the Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD), not by DPOR.
- The path is the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class, the classes for your certification, then the required exam, usually an ICC exam.
- Building, fire and property maintenance officials apply within 12 months of hire; technical assistants have 18 months.
- Certified officials complete 16 hours of DHCD-approved continuing education every 2 years.
