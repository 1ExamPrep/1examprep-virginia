Virginia contractor licenses are classified as Class A, Class B, or Class C. The required business examinations depend on the license class.

PSI Services administers the Virginia contractor business examinations. Class A and Class B applicants must have a qualifying Designated Employee who completes the applicable examination portions.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Class A and Class B contractor business examinations are open-book tests. Candidates may consult authorized reference materials during their examinations, subject to PSI testing requirements.

The NASCLA Virginia 11th Edition is identified as an examination reference, making familiarity with its organization valuable during preparation.

The professionally highlighted and tabbed edition helps candidates study important passages and practice navigating the publication.

Important Virginia Open-Book Examination Rules:

  • Bring the authorized reference books to the examination center.
  • PSI does not provide the required references at the testing site.
  • Approved reference books may be highlighted or underlined before testing.
  • Reference materials may be indexed using permanent tabs.
  • Removable tabs and temporary indexing flags are prohibited.
  • Reference books containing unauthorized writing are not permitted.
  • Candidates may not write in reference materials during the examination.
  • Loose papers and unbound study materials are not permitted.
  • Reference materials are subject to inspection by testing personnel.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates contractors performing construction, repair, remodeling, improvement, and related activities within its jurisdiction.

Virginia distinguishes contractor license classes from contractor classifications and specialties.

A license class addresses the business's authorization under the state's licensing structure. A classification or specialty identifies the type of construction work the contractor may perform.

Important Virginia Contractor Licensing Requirements:

  • License Class: Obtain the appropriate Class A, Class B, or Class C contractor license.
  • Contractor Classification: Identify the classifications and specialties associated with the requested construction work.
  • Pre-License Education: Satisfy the applicable Board-approved education requirement.
  • Designated Employee: Meet the business examination requirements associated with Class A or Class B licensing.
  • Qualified Individual: Establish the required qualifications for requested construction specialties.
  • Financial Verification: Provide the financial documentation required for the selected license class.
  • Business Organization: Submit the applicable business registration and organizational information.
  • Professional Compliance: Follow Virginia contractor licensing laws and Board regulations.

Class A applicants complete the Virginia, General, and Advanced business examination portions. Class B applicants complete the Virginia and General portions.

Class C applicants do not take the Designated Employee business examinations, although other applicable licensing responsibilities remain.

Virginia also distinguishes between the Designated Employee, who satisfies Class A or Class B business examination requirements, and the Qualified Individual, who meets qualifications associated with a specific contractor classification or specialty.

Understanding these responsibilities is important when preparing a contractor licensing application and organizing a compliant business.
