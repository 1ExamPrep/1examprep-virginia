### Exam Details

The Virginia Drywall Contracting examination is a technical specialty examination associated with the Virginia Board for Contractors. Virginia identifies the specialty as **DRY**, and a Qualified Individual seeking this designation must successfully complete the approved technical examination as part of the applicable licensing process.

- **Exam Name:** Drywall Contracting Examination
- **Specialty Abbreviation:** DRY
- **Number of Questions:** 40
- **Time Allowed:** 90 minutes
- **Minimum Passing Score:** 70%, or 28 correct answers
- **Exam Administrator:** PSI

The published examination outline divides the 40 questions among five principal content areas:

The published examination rules allow references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are permitted. Removable tabs are not allowed, and references containing written notes are prohibited.

### Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing is administered by the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation Board for Contractors. A contractor license combines a license class with one or more classifications or specialty designations.

1. **Determine the appropriate contractor license class.** Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
2. **Select the Drywall Contracting specialty.** The specialty abbreviation is DRY.
3. **Identify the Qualified Individual.** The contractor business must designate a Qualified Individual for the specialty.
4. **Document applicable experience.** Qualified Individuals seeking DRY must satisfy Virginia's experience requirements and provide the applicable experience verification.
5. **Complete required pre-license education.** New Virginia contractor license applicants must complete the applicable eight-hour approved pre-license education requirement.
6. **Complete the DRY technical examination.** Virginia requires successful completion of a Board-approved technical examination for the Drywall Contracting designation.
7. **Complete any additional examinations required for the selected contractor license class.**
8. **Complete the contractor license application and supporting documentation.**
9. **Submit the applicable licensing materials to the Virginia Board for Contractors.**

Successful completion of the DRY technical examination is one component of the licensing process. Passing the examination does not by itself issue a Virginia contractor license.
