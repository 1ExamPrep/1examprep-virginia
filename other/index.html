Is the Other Exam Prep exam open book?

Yes. The Virginia exams PSI runs are open book. You bring your own references, which may be highlighted and permanently tabbed; removable flags and loose notes are not allowed.

Who issues the Other Exam Prep license or certification in Virginia?

It comes from the board for the license you are sitting. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

Which edition of a code book should I buy?

The edition named in the candidate bulletin for your exam, which is not always the newest one. Virginia adopts new code editions on its own schedule, so check the bulletin before ordering.

Can I bring a highlighted and tabbed code book into the exam?

For the PSI exams run for the Board for Contractors, yes: highlighting, underlining and permanent tabs are allowed, while removable flags and loose notes are not.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Licensing exam questions are confidential. The courses and practice exams are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations that point to the reference section.
