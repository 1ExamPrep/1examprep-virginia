### Open Book Test

The **Virginia Class A and Class B Contractor Business examinations are open-book tests**. Candidates may consult designated reference publications during testing, subject to PSI's examination requirements.

**Important Virginia Open-Book Examination Rules:**

- Candidates must bring their authorized reference books to the examination center.
- PSI does not provide reference books at the testing site.

- Reference materials may be indexed with permanent tabs.

- Books containing unauthorized writing are not allowed.
- Candidates may not write in reference books during the examination.
- Loose papers, unbound references, and study guides are not permitted.

### State Requirements

The **Virginia Board for Contractors** regulates construction contractor licensing and establishes requirements for businesses performing regulated construction activities.

Virginia contractor licenses are classified as Class A, Class B, or Class C. Each license also identifies the classifications or specialties in which the contractor may perform work.

**Important Virginia Contractor Licensing Requirements:**

- **Appropriate License Class:** Obtain a Class A, Class B, or Class C license corresponding to the business's activities.
- **Contractor Classification:** Identify the specialties or classifications associated with the work performed.
- **Pre-License Education:** Complete the applicable eight-hour Board-approved program.
- **Designated Employee:** Meet the business examination requirements for Class A or Class B licensing.
- **Qualified Individual:** Satisfy the qualification requirements for requested contractor classifications and specialties.
- **Financial Verification:** Provide required financial information for the applicable license class.
- **Business Documentation:** Submit organizational information and supporting application materials.
- **Professional Compliance:** Follow Virginia contractor licensing laws and Board regulations.

Class A applicants prepare for the Virginia, General, and Advanced business examinations. Class B applicants prepare for the Virginia and General examinations.

Class C applicants do not take the Designated Employee business examinations, although other applicable licensing requirements remain in effect.

Virginia distinguishes between a Designated Employee, who satisfies business examination requirements, and a Qualified Individual, who establishes qualifications for specific construction classifications or specialties.

Understanding these roles is important when organizing a contractor licensing application.
