Exam Details

The Virginia Residential Plumber Tradesman examination is the licensing examination for individuals pursuing the Residential Plumber designation through the Virginia Board for Contractors Tradesmen Program. Candidates must receive the applicable Board approval before scheduling the test through PSI.

The examination contains 50 items. Candidates receive 120 minutes to complete the test and need at least 35 correct answers to meet the passing requirement.

The exam content includes:

  • Standards of Practice: 3 items
  • Standards of Conduct: 2 items
  • Renewal and Reinstatement: 1 item
  • Definitions: 1 item
  • Qualification for Licensure: 1 item
  • Drainage, Waste and Vents: 12 items
  • Water Supply Systems: 8 items
  • Backflow Prevention: 6 items
  • Storm Drainage Systems: 4 items
  • Plumbing Fixtures: 4 items
  • General Regulations: 4 items
  • Interceptors and Traps: 4 items

Drainage, Waste and Vents is the largest technical category, followed by Water Supply Systems and Backflow Prevention. Candidates should devote substantial preparation time to those subjects while also reviewing each smaller category because all areas contribute to the final result.

The examination also tests Virginia-specific regulatory knowledge. Standards of Practice, Standards of Conduct, qualification requirements, renewal, reinstatement, and definitions should be studied from the Virginia Board for Contractors Individual License and Certification Regulations.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Residential Plumber Tradesman examination is an open-book test. Candidates must bring their own permitted references because the testing center does not supply the books.

The two main exam references are the Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations and the International Plumbing Code, 2021. Candidates should become comfortable deciding which reference applies to a question before beginning a search.

Questions involving drainage, waste, vents, water supply, backflow prevention, storm drainage, fixtures, interceptors, traps, or general plumbing requirements will typically require the International Plumbing Code. Questions involving professional conduct, licensing qualifications, definitions, renewal, or reinstatement may require the Virginia regulations.

State Requirements

Virginia's Residential Plumber Tradesman pathway became available beginning April 1, 2025. It provides an individual licensing option specifically for residential plumbing work and uses a combination of practical experience and vocational training to determine examination eligibility.

Current qualification pathways include:

  • 2 years of practical experience plus 160 hours of formal vocational training.
  • 3 years of practical experience plus 120 hours of formal vocational training.
  • 4 years of practical experience plus 80 hours of formal vocational training.
  • 5 years of practical experience plus 40 hours of formal vocational training.
  • 6 years of practical experience without applying formal vocational training toward the qualification pathway.

Applicants must be at least 18 years old and provide the documentation needed for their selected qualification pathway. Practical experience must relate to plumbing work, and vocational training must be documented when used toward eligibility.

The Residential Plumber Tradesman credential is limited to the residential scope established by Virginia. It does not authorize plumbing work on commercial, industrial, institutional, or government-use structures outside the residential scope.

The individual tradesman credential is also distinct from a contractor business license. A person may qualify as a Residential Plumber Tradesman while the business performing contracting work has separate contractor licensing obligations.
