### Open Book Test

The Virginia Residential Plumber Tradesman examination is an **open-book test**. Candidates are responsible for bringing their permitted reference materials because the testing center does not provide the books.

Open-book testing makes reference-navigation practice an important part of preparation. With 50 questions and 120 minutes available, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds per question. Candidates who spend excessive time searching an unfamiliar code book may have less time available for later questions.

The **International Plumbing Code, 2021** serves as the principal technical reference for plumbing preparation. Candidates should become familiar with its index, chapters, definitions, tables, and individual code sections.

The **Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations** supports preparation for the state-specific regulatory portion of the examination. Questions involving licensing qualifications, professional conduct, renewal, reinstatement, or Standards of Practice may require this reference instead of the plumbing code.

A productive open-book strategy begins with identifying the subject of the question before reaching for a reference. A drainage, venting, water-supply, backflow, fixture, or trap question will typically lead to the plumbing code, while a licensing or conduct question may lead to the Virginia regulations.

Candidates should practice using the table of contents and index rather than scanning pages one at a time. Learning alternate terminology is also helpful because the wording used in a question may not exactly match the index entry a candidate initially expects.

### State Requirements

Virginia introduced Residential Plumber and Residential HVAC tradesman credentials as additional residential licensing pathways beginning April 1, 2025. The Residential Plumber credential is specifically intended for plumbing work within the residential scope and does not permit commercial plumbing work outside that scope.

Applicants must meet an approved combination of practical plumbing experience and formal vocational training. Current pathways include:

- **2 years of practical experience** plus **160 hours of formal vocational training**.
- **3 years of practical experience** plus **120 hours of formal vocational training**.
- **4 years of practical experience** plus **80 hours of formal vocational training**.
- **5 years of practical experience** plus **40 hours of formal vocational training**.
- **6 years of practical experience** without using formal vocational training toward the qualification pathway.

Applicants using practical experience must provide the required verification. Candidates who use vocational education toward their qualification must also provide documentation supporting those training hours.

Virginia provides the Residential Tradesman Exam and License Application specifically for individuals. The current application lists a **$150 application fee**. This state application fee is separate from the Ultimate package price and the applicable PSI examination fee.

The Virginia tradesmen program also requires Board pre-approval before an applicant can sit for the examination. Candidates should therefore complete the application and qualification process before attempting to schedule testing.

The Residential Plumber credential applies to the individual tradesman. Business owners should understand that forming or operating a plumbing contracting company can involve separate Virginia contractor licensing obligations.

## How does the Residential Plumber Tradesman exam work?

The Residential Plumber Tradesman exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
