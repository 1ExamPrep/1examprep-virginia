### Open Book Test

The Virginia Residential Plumber Tradesman examination is an **open-book test**. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own permitted reference materials because books are not supplied at the testing site.

The current examination rules allow references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are permitted, while removable tabs are not allowed. Reference materials containing handwriting are not permitted, and candidates may not write in the references during the examination session.

No loose papers or unbound reference material may be used. Candidates should make sure their references comply with the applicable testing requirements before arriving for the examination.

Open-book testing does not eliminate the need to study. With 50 questions and 120 minutes available, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds per question. A candidate who searches through the plumbing code from beginning to end for every answer can quickly lose valuable testing time.

A stronger approach is to learn the major plumbing subjects first and then use the books selectively. If a question asks about drainage or venting, candidates should recognize the subject immediately and know where to begin searching in the International Plumbing Code. Questions involving licensing conduct, qualifications, or renewal may instead require the Virginia regulations.

Practice should include regular use of the table of contents, index, chapter headings, definitions, code sections, and tables. The more familiar candidates become with the structure of each reference, the easier it becomes to select the correct book and locate the relevant information efficiently.

### Licensing Steps

The Virginia Residential Plumber Tradesman credential is issued to an individual through the Virginia Board for Contractors. It is separate from a Virginia contractor business license.

9. **Complete the remaining licensing requirements.** After satisfying the applicable qualifications and examination requirement, candidates can proceed toward issuance of the Residential Plumber Tradesman credential.

The current Residential Tradesman Exam and License Application lists a **$150 application fee**. Candidates should distinguish this state application fee from the separate PSI examination fee.

The individual tradesman credential should also be distinguished from contractor business licensing. Virginia states that individuals performing contracting work over the applicable contractor licensing threshold must address contractor licensing requirements separately.

### State Requirements

Virginia's Residential Plumber Tradesman license became available as part of the residential tradesman licensing pathway effective April 1, 2025. The credential gives qualified individuals a residential plumbing licensing option with requirements different from those for the traditional journeyman plumber credential.

A Residential Plumber Tradesman is defined as a person possessing the ability, proficiency, and qualifications to perform plumbing work using working knowledge sufficient to comply with the applicable provisions of the Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code for dwellings and townhouses and structures annexed to those residential buildings.

The credential does not extend to commercial, industrial, institutional, or government-use structures outside the defined residential scope.

Applicants must be at least **18 years old** and satisfy one of Virginia's approved experience and education combinations. The current qualification pathways include:

- **160 hours of formal vocational training** plus **2 years of practical experience** in plumbing.
- **120 hours of formal vocational training** plus **3 years of practical experience**.
- **80 hours of formal vocational training** plus **4 years of practical experience**.
- **40 hours of formal vocational training** plus **5 years of practical experience**.
- **6 years of practical experience** without using formal vocational training toward the qualification pathway.

Applicants using vocational training must provide the applicable documentation. Practical experience must also be verified according to the requirements of the Virginia Board for Contractors.

Virginia also provides licensing pathways for certain applicants who hold qualifying residential tradesman credentials from other jurisdictions, subject to the applicable application and documentation requirements.

Residential Plumber Tradesman licensing is intended for individuals. A plumbing company or other business performing contracting work may also need a separate Virginia contractor license depending on the nature and value of the work.

### Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Residential Plumber study plan should begin with the exam content outline. The number of questions assigned to each subject can help candidates decide where to devote their preparation time.

**Drainage, Waste and Vents** is the largest section, with 12 questions. Candidates should understand sanitary drainage systems, venting principles, drainage connections, sizing concepts, slope requirements, cleanouts, and other provisions governing the movement of wastewater and air through plumbing systems.

**Water Supply Systems** accounts for eight questions. Candidates should review potable water distribution, piping requirements, valves, sizing concepts, water service provisions, and applicable installation requirements.

**Backflow Prevention** contributes six questions. Candidates should understand the purpose of protecting potable water supplies from contamination and become familiar with applicable backflow protection methods and code provisions.

**Storm Drainage Systems**, **Plumbing Fixtures**, **General Regulations**, and **Interceptors and Traps** each account for four questions. Combined, these areas make up a significant portion of the technical examination and should not be treated as minor topics.

Virginia regulatory questions should be studied separately from technical plumbing material. Learn the Standards of Practice and Standards of Conduct that apply to tradesmen and review definitions, qualification requirements, renewal, and reinstatement provisions.

Reference-navigation practice should be incorporated into every study session. After answering a practice question, locate the supporting code section even when you already know the answer. This helps build familiarity with where information appears within the International Plumbing Code.

Use the index strategically. Plumbing terminology can vary, so practice searching by the main subject and by related terms. Once a likely code section is found, read the surrounding provisions instead of focusing on a single isolated sentence.

Timed practice can also help candidates develop an efficient testing rhythm. Because the examination provides 120 minutes for 50 questions, candidates should learn when a quick reference lookup is useful and when it makes more sense to answer from established knowledge and move forward.

When a practice question is answered incorrectly, use the error as a study opportunity. Find the correct provision, review the surrounding section, and understand why the other answer choices do not fit. This builds stronger code knowledge than memorizing individual practice answers.

## How does the Residential Plumber Tradesman exam work?

The Residential Plumber Tradesman exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
