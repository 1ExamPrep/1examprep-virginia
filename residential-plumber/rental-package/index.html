Exam Details

The Virginia Residential Plumber Tradesman examination is administered for individuals pursuing the Residential Plumber designation through the Virginia Board for Contractors Tradesmen Program. Applicants must receive Board approval before scheduling the examination through PSI.

The current Residential Plumber examination contains 50 items. Candidates receive 120 minutes to complete the examination and must answer at least 35 questions correctly to meet the passing requirement.

The examination content outline includes:

  • Standards of Practice: 3 items
  • Standards of Conduct: 2 items
  • Renewal and Reinstatement: 1 item
  • Definitions: 1 item
  • Qualification for Licensure: 1 item
  • Drainage, Waste and Vents: 12 items
  • Water Supply Systems: 8 items
  • Backflow Prevention: 6 items
  • Storm Drainage Systems: 4 items
  • Plumbing Fixtures: 4 items
  • General Regulations: 4 items
  • Interceptors and Traps: 4 items

Drainage, Waste and Vents is the largest individual examination category. Water Supply Systems and Backflow Prevention are also major technical areas. Candidates should devote substantial study time to these subjects while still preparing for every category in the examination outline.

The examination also includes Virginia-specific regulatory questions covering Standards of Practice, Standards of Conduct, licensing qualifications, definitions, renewal, and reinstatement. These subjects are supported by the Virginia Board for Contractors Individual License and Certification Regulations.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Residential Plumber Tradesman examination is an open-book test. Candidates must bring their own permitted reference materials because the testing center does not provide the books.

The Virginia Board for Contractors Individual License and Certification Regulations serves as the regulatory reference for the examination. The International Plumbing Code, 2021 is the technical code reference identified for the Residential Plumber portion.

Permitted references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are allowed. Removable tabs are not permitted, and reference materials containing handwriting are not allowed into the examination.

Some book(s) in this package are highlighted and tabbed for exam preparation. These features can help candidates organize study and become more comfortable moving between important sections of the references.

With 50 questions and 120 minutes, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds per question. Strong preparation therefore includes learning the material and developing efficient reference-navigation habits.

A question involving drainage, vents, water supply, backflow, fixtures, storm drainage, traps, or general plumbing provisions will normally lead candidates toward the International Plumbing Code. A question involving professional conduct, licensing qualifications, renewal, reinstatement, or Standards of Practice may require the Virginia regulations instead.

Licensing Steps

The Residential Plumber Tradesman credential is issued to individuals rather than business entities. Candidates must complete the applicable qualification, application, examination, and licensing requirements established by the Virginia Board for Contractors.

  1. Review the Residential Plumber qualification requirements. Virginia provides several combinations of practical experience and formal vocational training.
  2. Determine the appropriate eligibility pathway. Candidates should identify the experience and education combination that matches their documented background.
  3. Document practical plumbing experience. Applicants must provide the required verification of qualifying work experience.
  4. Provide vocational training documentation when applicable. Candidates using formal education toward qualification must submit the required records.
  5. Submit the Residential Tradesman Exam and License Application. Select the Residential Plumber Tradesman designation.
  6. Receive Board approval. Pre-approval is required before candidates can sit for the examination.
  7. Prepare for the examination. Use the course and rental references to review technical plumbing and Virginia regulatory material.
  8. Schedule and complete the PSI examination. Approved applicants can proceed with examination scheduling.
  9. Complete the remaining licensing requirements. Candidates who satisfy the examination and application requirements can proceed toward issuance of the Residential Plumber Tradesman credential.

The Residential Plumber Tradesman credential is an individual trade license. It should not be confused with a Virginia contractor business license. A business performing contracting work may have separate licensing obligations depending on the work being performed.

State Requirements

Virginia's Residential Plumber license is intended for residential plumbing work and does not authorize commercial plumbing outside the residential scope. The Virginia Tradesmen Program regulates the individual credential, while contractor business licensing is handled separately through the Board for Contractors.

Residential Plumber applicants must satisfy the applicable combination of practical experience and vocational education established for the residential tradesman pathway. The current pathways include:

  • 2 years of practical experience plus 160 hours of formal vocational training.
  • 3 years of practical experience plus 120 hours of formal vocational training.
  • 4 years of practical experience plus 80 hours of formal vocational training.
  • 5 years of practical experience plus 40 hours of formal vocational training.
  • 6 years of practical experience without using formal vocational training toward the qualification pathway.

Applicants must be at least 18 years old and provide the documentation required for their selected qualification pathway. Practical experience must be relevant to plumbing work, and vocational education must be documented when it is being used to satisfy part of the eligibility requirement.

The Residential Tradesman Exam and License Application is for individuals, not business entities. Virginia also states that an applicant performing contracting work over the applicable contractor licensing threshold must address contractor licensing requirements separately.
