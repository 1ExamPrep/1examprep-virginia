The 8-Hour Pre-License Education products cover the business side of a Virginia contractor license, which every Class A and B firm deals with no matter which trade classification it holds.

- Class A and B applicants pass the Virginia and general business portions, written to the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Virginia edition.
