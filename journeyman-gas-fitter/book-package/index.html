Exam Details

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter examination is administered for individuals seeking licensure through the Virginia Board for Contractors Tradesmen Program. Pre-approval from the Board is required before a candidate may schedule and sit for the examination.

PSI administers Virginia tradesman examinations. The current Journeyman Gasfitter portion contains 50 items. Candidates receive 150 minutes to complete this portion and must answer at least 35 questions correctly to meet the journeyman passing requirement.

The current exam outline includes:

  • Standards of Practice: 3 items
  • Standards of Conduct: 2 items
  • Renewal and Reinstatement: 2 items
  • Definitions: 1 item
  • Qualification for Licensure: 1 item
  • Revocation of Licensure: 1 item
  • Natural and LP Gas Piping: 12 items
  • Pipe Sizing: 11 items
  • LP-Gas Tanks: 9 items
  • Gas Piping Controls: 5 items
  • Testing: 3 items

Natural and LP Gas Piping and Pipe Sizing account for a substantial portion of the examination. Candidates should devote significant study time to piping materials, installation, system layout, allowable methods, sizing procedures, pressure considerations, and code tables.

LP-Gas Tanks are another important area with nine items. Candidates should review container placement, installation, protection, associated piping, safety requirements, and other provisions addressed by NFPA 58.

The current Journeyman Tradesman examination fee is $100, paid directly to PSI after the candidate has received the required Board approval.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter examination is an open-book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own permitted references to the testing center because the books are not provided at the exam site.

The examination is based on the listed references and trade knowledge. Candidates should become familiar with the organization of each book before exam day. Open-book testing rewards efficient code navigation, but it does not eliminate the need to understand the trade.

The International Fuel Gas Code, 2021 supports preparation for natural gas and fuel-gas piping, sizing, controls, testing, and related installation requirements. NFPA 58 - Liquefied Petroleum Gas Code, 2014 supports LP-Gas tank, container, piping, and system requirements. The Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations supports regulatory questions involving conduct, practice, qualification, renewal, reinstatement, definitions, and disciplinary issues.

Candidates should practice using tables and code sections under time pressure. With 50 items and 150 minutes, there is enough time to consult references, but inefficient searching can still create time pressure. A strong strategy is to first identify whether the question is regulatory, natural-gas-related, LP-Gas-related, sizing-related, or testing-related and then move directly to the appropriate reference.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed in accordance with applicable PSI examination rules. Candidates should use the books repeatedly during preparation so they understand the chapter structure, tables, section numbering, and index terminology.

Licensing Steps

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter license is issued to an individual rather than a company. Applicants must satisfy the experience and education requirements, complete the application process, receive Board approval, and pass the applicable examination unless an exemption applies.

  1. Review the current Journeyman Gasfitter qualification pathways. Virginia provides several combinations of practical experience and formal education.
  2. Determine which pathway matches your background. Candidates should identify the education and experience combination they can document.
  3. Document qualifying practical experience. Applicants must provide the required experience verification for gas fitting work.
  4. Submit education documentation when applicable. Degrees, certificates, vocational training, or other qualifying education must be documented when used to meet eligibility requirements.
  5. Submit the applicable tradesman exam and license application. The candidate must request the Journeyman Gasfitter credential.
  6. Receive Board approval to test. Virginia requires pre-approval before scheduling the examination.
  7. Prepare for the exam using the listed references. Review regulations, gas piping, sizing, LP-Gas tanks, controls, and testing.
  8. Schedule the PSI examination. Approved candidates may proceed through PSI's exam scheduling process.
  9. Complete the Journeyman Gasfitter examination. Candidates must meet the required passing score.
  10. Complete the remaining licensing requirements. Successful applicants proceed through the final licensing process with the Virginia Board for Contractors.

Virginia also recognizes an examination exemption for individuals who successfully complete an approved apprenticeship program that qualifies under state law. Candidates using an exemption pathway must provide the applicable documentation.

State Requirements

Virginia regulates gas fitters through the Board for Contractors under the Individual License and Certification Regulations. A Journeyman Gasfitter applicant must satisfy one of the established combinations of education and practical experience before receiving examination approval.

Current Journeyman Gasfitter eligibility pathways include:

  • Bachelor's degree in an engineering curriculum related to the trade plus 1 year of practical experience.
  • Associate degree or certificate from at least a two-year tradesman-related program plus 2 years of practical experience.
  • 120 hours of formal vocational training plus 4 years of practical experience.
  • 80 hours of formal vocational training plus 5 years of practical experience.
  • 40 hours of formal vocational training plus 6 years of practical experience.
  • No formal vocational training plus 8 years of practical experience.

Applicants should maintain clear documentation of qualifying experience and education because the Board uses these materials to determine examination eligibility.

Virginia also requires continuing education for gas fitting license renewal. Approved gas-fitting continuing education must meet Board requirements and is separate from initial examination preparation.

The Journeyman Gasfitter credential is an individual trade license. A business performing contracting work may also be subject to separate Virginia contractor licensing requirements depending on the nature and value of the work performed.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive study plan should follow the exam outline and give the greatest amount of attention to Natural and LP Gas Piping, Pipe Sizing, and LP-Gas Tanks because these categories represent most of the technical questions on the examination.

Natural and LP Gas Piping preparation should include approved piping materials, installation methods, fittings, joints, routing, protection, system layout, pressure-related requirements, and other code provisions that govern gas piping systems.

Pipe Sizing is one of the most important exam subjects. Candidates should practice using sizing tables, understanding developed length, pressure, load, demand, and system configuration. Pipe-sizing questions often require careful use of tables and technical information rather than simple memorization.

LP-Gas Tanks preparation should include tank and container installation, clearances, placement, protection, piping connections, capacity considerations, and safety requirements. NFPA 58 should be used repeatedly during preparation because LP-Gas tank questions make up a significant portion of the exam.

Gas Piping Controls should include valves, regulators, shutoff devices, controls, and other components used to manage the safe flow and operation of gas systems.

Testing should include pressure testing, system verification, test procedures, and applicable requirements for confirming that gas piping is safe before use.

Regulatory topics should not be ignored. Standards of Practice, Standards of Conduct, Renewal and Reinstatement, Definitions, Qualification for Licensure, and Revocation together account for part of the exam and should be reviewed using the Virginia Board regulations.

Reference-navigation practice is especially important for an open-book gas fitter examination. Candidates should regularly work through sample problems while using the books exactly as they plan to use them on test day. This helps develop familiarity with terminology, tables, indexes, section numbers, and code structure.

When a practice question is answered incorrectly, locate the supporting code provision or regulation and review the surrounding section. Understanding the complete requirement can be more useful than memorizing a single answer because similar concepts can be tested through different scenarios.

Pipe-sizing problems deserve repeated practice. Candidates should become comfortable identifying the correct table, determining system conditions, following the required sizing method, and confirming the result before moving on.

Timed practice can also help candidates develop a realistic pace. The 150-minute exam provides time for reference use, but candidates should still avoid spending excessive time on a single difficult lookup or calculation.

How does the Journeyman Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Journeyman Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
