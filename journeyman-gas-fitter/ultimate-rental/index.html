Exam Details

Virginia regulates individual gas fitters through the Board for Contractors within the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation. The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter credential is an individual tradesman license rather than a contractor business license. Candidates pursuing the journeyman gas fitting designation must satisfy the applicable education and practical-experience requirements and receive approval from the Board before sitting for the examination.

PSI administers Virginia tradesman examinations. Board pre-approval is required before a candidate can schedule the journeyman examination. The current examination fee for a Journeyman Tradesman examination is $100 and is paid directly to PSI. This testing fee is separate from the price of the Ultimate Exam Prep Rental Package.

Licensing Steps

The Virginia journeyman gas fitter licensing process begins with determining whether you satisfy one of the Board's approved combinations of education and practical trade experience. Virginia provides several pathways, allowing candidates with formal education, vocational training, or extensive practical experience to qualify through different combinations.

Applicants must complete the applicable Virginia tradesman examination and license application and submit the documentation required by the Board. Supporting information may include identification, vocational education records, and documentation of practical experience in the gas fitting trade.

After reviewing the application, the Board determines whether the candidate qualifies for examination approval. Virginia requires this pre-approval before a tradesman candidate may sit for the examination. Once approval is received, the candidate can follow the instructions for scheduling the examination with PSI.

The examination is an important part of the licensing process, but passing an exam preparation course or purchasing this package does not independently create a Virginia license. Applicants must complete the state's required application, qualification, examination, and licensing steps.

State Requirements

Virginia journeyman gas fitter applicants must be at least 18 years old and provide the documentation required by the Board for Contractors. The Board's regulations establish several education-and-experience combinations that may qualify a candidate for the journeyman gas fitter examination.

A candidate with a bachelor's degree in an engineering curriculum related to the trade may qualify with one year of practical experience in the trade. A candidate with an associate degree or certificate of completion from at least a two-year program in a tradesman-related field may qualify with two years of practical experience.

Virginia also provides qualification pathways based on formal vocational training. A journeyman gas fitter applicant may qualify with 120 hours of formal vocational training and four years of practical experience, 80 hours of formal vocational training and five years of practical experience, or 40 hours of formal vocational training and six years of practical experience.

A candidate without formal vocational training may qualify with eight years of practical experience in the trade. Applicants must document their qualifications in the manner required by the Board.

Virginia defines gas fitting-related work as work generally performed within the HVAC or plumbing trades and regulated under the Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code. The scope includes work involving industrial gas, fuel gas, gaseous hydrogen, liquefied petroleum gas, or natural gas piping, as well as associated tanks, chimneys and vents, direct vents, equipment, and appliances annexed to real property.

Licensed gas fitters are also subject to continuing education requirements for renewal. Virginia requires one hour of Board-approved continuing education for gas fitting, natural gas, and liquefied petroleum gas designations as applicable. The continuing education must relate to applicable building code changes.

How does the Journeyman Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Journeyman Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
