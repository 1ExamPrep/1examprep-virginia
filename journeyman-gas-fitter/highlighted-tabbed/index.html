Exam Details

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter examination is administered for individuals pursuing licensure through the Virginia Board for Contractors Tradesmen Program. Applicants must receive the applicable Board approval before scheduling the examination through PSI.

The current Journeyman Gasfitter examination contains 50 items. Candidates receive 150 minutes to complete the exam and must answer at least 35 items correctly to meet the passing requirement.

Exam preparation should include both regulatory and technical gas-fitting subjects, including:

  • Standards of Practice
  • Standards of Conduct
  • Renewal and Reinstatement
  • Definitions
  • Qualification for Licensure
  • Revocation of Licensure
  • Natural and LP Gas Piping
  • Pipe Sizing
  • LP-Gas Tanks
  • Gas Piping Controls
  • Testing

Natural and LP Gas Piping, Pipe Sizing, and LP-Gas Tanks are major technical areas of preparation. Candidates should be comfortable with piping materials, fitting methods, installation requirements, system design, pressure considerations, gas demand, sizing methods, container installation, protection, and associated safety provisions.

Gas Piping Controls and Testing are also important. Candidates should review shutoff valves, regulators, control devices, pressure testing, system verification, and related code requirements.

The examination also includes Virginia regulatory material. Standards of Practice, Standards of Conduct, qualification requirements, renewal, reinstatement, definitions, and disciplinary provisions should be reviewed directly from the Virginia Board for Contractors Individual License and Certification Regulations.

PSI administers the Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter examination after candidates receive approval from the Board. The current Journeyman Tradesman examination fee is $100, paid directly to PSI.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter examination is an open-book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their permitted references to the testing center because the books are not provided at the exam site.

The open-book format makes reference navigation an important part of preparation. Having the books available does not eliminate the need to understand gas fitting principles or know where information is located. Candidates should be able to identify the subject of a question quickly and move to the appropriate reference.

A question involving Virginia licensing, professional conduct, renewal, qualifications, definitions, or disciplinary requirements may require the Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations.

Questions involving natural gas piping, sizing, materials, controls, installation procedures, appliance connections, or testing may require the International Fuel Gas Code, 2021.

Questions involving liquefied petroleum gas containers, LP-Gas tanks, placement, protection, piping, equipment, and related safety requirements may require NFPA 58 - Standard for Liquefied Petroleum Gas Code, 2014.

The books in this package are highlighted and permanently tabbed for exam preparation. Candidates should use the prepared tabs throughout study so they become familiar with the location of important sections before the exam.

With 50 items and 150 minutes, candidates have an average of three minutes per question. That provides time to use the references, but efficient navigation is still important. Candidates should avoid searching through multiple books when the topic clearly points to one specific reference.

Licensing Steps

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter credential is an individual trade license. Applicants must meet the applicable education and practical experience requirements, complete the required application process, receive approval to test, and satisfy the examination requirement unless an applicable exemption applies.

  1. Review the Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter eligibility requirements. Candidates should determine which combination of practical experience and formal vocational training applies to their background.
  2. Document practical gas-fitting experience. Virginia requires experience verification for candidates qualifying through the standard journeyman pathway.
  3. Document formal vocational training when applicable. Applicants using trade education toward eligibility should provide the required certificates, transcripts, or other documentation.
  4. Complete the applicable Tradesman Exam and License Application. Candidates must select the Gasfitter trade and journeyman level.
  5. Submit supporting documentation. Experience verification and training records must accompany the application when required.
  6. Receive Board approval. Candidates must receive approval before scheduling the Journeyman Gasfitter examination.
  7. Prepare with the highlighted and tabbed references. Study Virginia regulations, natural gas piping, pipe sizing, LP-Gas systems, controls, and testing.
  8. Schedule the PSI examination. Approved applicants may proceed through the testing vendor's scheduling process.
  9. Complete the Journeyman Gasfitter exam. Candidates must meet the required passing score.
  10. Complete the remaining licensing process. Candidates who satisfy the applicable requirements proceed toward issuance of the individual Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter license.

Certain approved apprenticeship completion pathways may provide an exemption from the examination requirement. Applicants using an apprenticeship-based exemption must provide the documentation required by the Virginia Board for Contractors.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates individual gas fitters through the Tradesmen Program. Candidates applying for a Journeyman Gasfitter license must satisfy one of the Board's approved combinations of practical trade experience and formal vocational training unless they qualify under an approved exemption pathway.

Current journeyman qualification options listed by Virginia include:

  • 4 years of practical experience plus 240 hours of formal vocational training in the trade.
  • 5 years of practical experience plus 160 hours of formal vocational training.
  • 6 years of practical experience plus 80 hours of formal vocational training.
  • 7 or more years of practical experience plus 40 hours of formal vocational training.

Applicants must submit the required Individual Experience Verification Form and documentation of completed vocational training for the qualification pathway they select.

Virginia also recognizes certain apprenticeship-based examination exemptions. Candidates who successfully complete qualifying apprenticeship programs should follow the documentation requirements established by the Board.

The Journeyman Gasfitter license is issued to an individual and is different from a Virginia contractor business license. A business performing gas-fitting contracting work may have separate contractor licensing obligations depending on the work being performed.

Virginia gas fitters must also comply with applicable renewal and continuing education requirements after licensure. Those ongoing requirements are separate from initial examination preparation.

Test Information and Study Materials

A strong Journeyman Gasfitter study plan should focus first on the major technical categories while also allocating time for Virginia regulations.

Natural and LP Gas Piping preparation should include approved materials, fittings, joints, routing, piping protection, installation practices, pressure considerations, valves, and system layout.

Pipe Sizing deserves repeated practice. Candidates should become comfortable working with gas-demand calculations, developed length, pressure, system configuration, and sizing tables. These questions often require candidates to use several pieces of information together before selecting the correct answer.

LP-Gas Tanks should be reviewed directly from NFPA 58. Candidates should study container placement, protection, installation, separation requirements, associated piping, and other safety-related provisions.

Gas Piping Controls should include regulators, valves, shutoff devices, control components, and their proper application within gas piping systems.

Testing should include pressure testing, test conditions, verification procedures, and requirements used to confirm that gas piping is suitable for service.

Virginia regulatory material should also be part of regular preparation. Candidates should review licensing qualifications, Standards of Practice, Standards of Conduct, renewal, reinstatement, definitions, and disciplinary provisions.

Highlighted and tabbed books can make this process more efficient by creating clear navigation points. Candidates should practice moving from a sample question to the correct tab, section, table, or definition until the book layout becomes familiar.

When a practice question is answered incorrectly, locate the supporting provision and read the surrounding material. This helps candidates understand conditions, exceptions, and related rules rather than memorizing one isolated answer.

Timed practice can also help candidates use the available 150 minutes effectively. Candidates should develop a habit of answering familiar questions efficiently and reserving additional lookup time for technical sizing problems or code-specific questions.
