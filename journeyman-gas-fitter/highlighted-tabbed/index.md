### Exam Details

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter examination is administered for individuals pursuing licensure through the Virginia Board for Contractors Tradesmen Program. Applicants must receive the applicable Board approval before scheduling the examination through PSI.

The current Journeyman Gasfitter examination contains **50 items**. Candidates receive **150 minutes** to complete the exam and must answer at least **35 items correctly** to meet the passing requirement.

Exam preparation should include both regulatory and technical gas-fitting subjects, including:

- Standards of Practice
- Standards of Conduct
- Renewal and Reinstatement
- Definitions
- Qualification for Licensure
- Revocation of Licensure
- Natural and LP Gas Piping
- Pipe Sizing
- LP-Gas Tanks
- Gas Piping Controls
- Testing

Natural and LP Gas Piping, Pipe Sizing, and LP-Gas Tanks are major technical areas of preparation. Candidates should be comfortable with piping materials, fitting methods, installation requirements, system design, pressure considerations, gas demand, sizing methods, container installation, protection, and associated safety provisions.

Gas Piping Controls and Testing are also important. Candidates should review shutoff valves, regulators, control devices, pressure testing, system verification, and related code requirements.

The examination also includes Virginia regulatory material. Standards of Practice, Standards of Conduct, qualification requirements, renewal, reinstatement, definitions, and disciplinary provisions should be reviewed directly from the Virginia Board for Contractors Individual License and Certification Regulations.

PSI administers the Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter examination after candidates receive approval from the Board. The current Journeyman Tradesman examination fee is **$100**, paid directly to PSI.

### State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates individual gas fitters through the Tradesmen Program. Candidates applying for a Journeyman Gasfitter license must satisfy one of the Board's approved combinations of practical trade experience and formal vocational training unless they qualify under an approved exemption pathway.

Current journeyman qualification options listed by Virginia include:

- **4 years of practical experience** plus **240 hours of formal vocational training** in the trade.
- **5 years of practical experience** plus **160 hours of formal vocational training**.
- **6 years of practical experience** plus **80 hours of formal vocational training**.
- **7 or more years of practical experience** plus **40 hours of formal vocational training**.

Applicants must submit the required Individual Experience Verification Form and documentation of completed vocational training for the qualification pathway they select.

Virginia also recognizes certain apprenticeship-based examination exemptions. Candidates who successfully complete qualifying apprenticeship programs should follow the documentation requirements established by the Board.

The Journeyman Gasfitter license is issued to an individual and is different from a Virginia contractor business license. A business performing gas-fitting contracting work may have separate contractor licensing obligations depending on the work being performed.

Virginia gas fitters must also comply with applicable renewal and continuing education requirements after licensure. Those ongoing requirements are separate from initial examination preparation.

## How does the Journeyman Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Journeyman Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
