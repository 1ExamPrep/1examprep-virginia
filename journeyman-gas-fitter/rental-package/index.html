Exam Details

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter examination is associated with individual gas fitter licensure regulated through the Virginia Board for Contractors under the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation. Candidates must obtain Board approval before sitting for the examination. PSI administers examinations for Virginia tradesmen.

The Journeyman Tradesman examination fee listed by the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation is paid directly to the testing provider and is separate from this exam preparation package. Candidates who receive approval from the Board can proceed with examination scheduling according to the instructions provided for their authorization.

Preparation for a gas fitter examination should emphasize both practical gas fitting knowledge and efficient use of the listed references. Candidates may encounter subject matter associated with fuel gas piping, materials, sizing, installation, gas controls, testing, appliances, combustion and ventilation considerations, venting systems, liquefied petroleum gas requirements, and the Virginia regulations governing individual licensure.

The goal is not simply to memorize isolated rules. A more effective approach is to understand how each reference is organized, recognize which reference applies to the question being asked, locate the relevant chapter or provision, and carefully interpret tables, definitions, exceptions, and installation requirements.

Licensing Steps

Virginia requires pre-approval from the Board before a tradesman candidate may sit for the examination. A candidate pursuing journeyman gas fitter licensure should begin by reviewing the Virginia Board for Contractors application requirements and determining which education and practical-experience pathway applies to the individual applicant.

Applicants submit the required application information and documentation to the Board. Virginia regulations require applicants to provide documentation relating to identification, vocational training, and experience. Practical trade experience must be documented as required by the Board, and education may be documented through appropriate educational records.

After the Board approves a candidate for examination, the candidate can follow the examination scheduling instructions and contact the testing provider. Successful completion of the required examination is part of the pathway toward obtaining the applicable Virginia tradesman license.

Because licensing and testing are separate from purchasing an exam preparation package, candidates should manage their Board application and examination authorization independently from their study schedule. Starting the preparation process early can provide more time to become comfortable with the reference books while application and scheduling steps are being completed.

State Requirements

Virginia's current regulations establish several education-and-experience pathways for examination approval as a journeyman gas fitter. An applicant may qualify with a bachelor's degree in an engineering curriculum related to the trade and one year of practical experience, or with an associate degree or certificate from at least a two-year tradesman-related program and two years of practical experience.

Other pathways combine formal vocational training with practical trade experience. For journeyman gas fitter applicants, the regulations provide pathways that include 120 hours of formal vocational training with four years of practical experience, 80 hours of formal vocational training with five years of practical experience, or 40 hours of formal vocational training with six years of practical experience. An applicant with no formal vocational training may qualify with eight years of practical experience in the trade.

Applicants must also be at least 18 years of age and submit the documentation required by the Board. Experience is generally verified through qualifying references such as current or former employers, contractors, building officials, inspectors, engineers, architects, or clients who can attest to the applicant's work in the trade. Educational qualifications may be documented through transcripts or diplomas from the applicable institution.

Virginia defines gas fitting-related work to include work involving industrial gas, fuel gas, gaseous hydrogen, liquefied petroleum gas or natural gas piping, tanks, chimneys and vents, direct vents, equipment, and appliances annexed to real property within the scope established by Virginia law and regulation.

Virginia gas fitter licenses are also subject to continuing education requirements for renewal. Gas fitters must complete the applicable Board-approved continuing education relating to building code changes as required during the licensing cycle.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter exam study plan should include more than reading the reference books from beginning to end. Open-book examinations reward candidates who understand the material and can navigate references efficiently. Begin by learning the structure of each book. Review tables of contents, indexes, definitions, chapter headings, appendices, and major tables so you know where different subjects are located.

For the International Fuel Gas Code, spend time developing confidence with the sections that address gas piping systems, materials, sizing, installation practices, testing, appliances, combustion air, chimneys, and venting. When working with tables, practice identifying exactly which variables control the answer instead of selecting a number before reading headings, notes, and conditions.

NFPA 58 deserves its own review routine. Learn how the standard organizes LP-Gas requirements and practice distinguishing an LP-Gas question from a general fuel gas question. Recognizing the appropriate reference before opening a book can save time.

The Virginia regulations should also be part of regular study. Regulatory questions often require precise reading because the answer may depend on a definition, qualification, renewal rule, or other specific requirement. Practice locating these provisions directly instead of relying only on memory.

Use practice questions to build a repeatable process: identify the subject, decide which reference applies, select the likely chapter or section, confirm the answer in the book, and review why competing choices are incorrect. Over time, this process develops stronger reference-navigation habits and helps you become more comfortable working under timed conditions.

How does the Journeyman Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Journeyman Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
