Exam Details

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter exam is part of Virginia’s individual license and certification examination program. It is used for candidates pursuing the Journeyman Gasfitter credential through the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation and the Virginia Board for Contractors. Candidates must follow the Board’s application process and receive approval before scheduling the examination.

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter exam contains 50 questions. Candidates are allowed 150 minutes to complete the exam. The passing score is 35 correct answers. Because the exam is timed, students should prepare for both direct knowledge questions and reference-based questions that require use of the code books or regulations.

The exam content includes Virginia licensing regulations and technical gasfitting topics. Candidates should prepare for standards of practice, standards of conduct, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, qualification for licensure, revocation of licensure, natural and LP-gas piping, pipe sizing, gas piping controls, and testing. These subject areas require a balanced study plan because the exam can include regulatory questions as well as practical trade and code-based questions.

Natural and LP-gas piping questions may involve materials, installation requirements, piping layout, system components, fuel gas code rules, and LP-gas provisions. Pipe sizing questions may require candidates to read tables carefully, understand system conditions, and apply sizing methods correctly. Gas piping controls and testing questions may involve shutoff valves, regulators, pressure testing, inspections, appliance connections, and safe system operation.

The regulatory portion of the exam should not be overlooked. The Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations help candidates prepare for questions related to individual licensing, definitions, qualifications, professional conduct, renewal, reinstatement, and disciplinary provisions. These questions may be more direct than fuel gas code questions, but they still require careful study.

Students preparing with this online exam prep course should plan to review each reference multiple times. The goal is not simply to memorize scattered facts. Open Book Test

The Virginia Journeyman Gasfitter exam is an open-book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved reference books to the testing center. Reference books are not provided at the exam site, so candidates should have the proper materials before exam day and should practice using them during preparation.

Open book does not mean the exam is simple. Candidates still need to understand the trade content, the structure of the references, and the rules for using materials during the exam. With 50 questions and 150 minutes, candidates have an average of three minutes per question. Some questions may be answered quickly, while others may require code lookup, table review, or careful reading of regulatory language.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam when prepared according to testing rules. Study guides are not permitted in the examination room. Reference materials containing writing are not allowed. Candidates may not write in their reference materials during the exam. Permanent tabs are allowed, while removable tabs are not allowed. Loose papers and unbound references are not permitted.

These rules make preparation and organization important. Candidates should become familiar with each reference before the exam. Useful preparation habits include reviewing the table of contents, learning the index, marking important sections properly, practicing with definitions, locating major code chapters, and becoming comfortable using tables. The more familiar a candidate is with the books, the more efficiently they can respond during the timed exam.

For the Journeyman Gasfitter exam, reference navigation is especially important for pipe sizing, gas piping controls, LP-gas requirements, fuel gas code provisions, appliance-related rules, and system testing. Candidates should practice identifying keywords in questions and matching those keywords to the correct reference. This helps reduce wasted time and improves confidence during the exam.

How does the Journeyman Gas Fitter (LP) exam work?

The Journeyman Gas Fitter (LP) exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
