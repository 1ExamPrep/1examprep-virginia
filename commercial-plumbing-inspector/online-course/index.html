Exam Details

The ICC Commercial Plumbing Inspector P2 exam is a national certification exam offered by the International Code Council. It is used to measure knowledge related to commercial plumbing inspection duties. For Virginia code official certification, the Virginia Building Code Academy and Department of Housing and Community Development identify the P2 Commercial Plumbing Inspector exam as one accepted exam option for the Commercial Plumbing Inspector certification path, along with the required Virginia Building Code Academy coursework.

The P2 exam includes 60 multiple-choice questions and is administered as an open book exam with a 2 1/2-hour time limit. The exam content is organized around commercial plumbing inspection responsibilities and the listed reference materials. Questions focus on locating and applying code requirements, not simply memorizing facts. Students should be comfortable moving through definitions, general provisions, fixture requirements, water heater provisions, water supply and distribution rules, drainage sizing, venting provisions, indirect waste, interceptors, neutralization systems, storm drainage, and accessibility-related fixture requirements.

Major content areas for the P2 Commercial Plumbing Inspector exam include general requirements, piping protection, piping installation, fixtures, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, separators, special piping, storage systems, and storm drainage. The exam also includes accessibility-related fixture inspection knowledge through the use of ICC A117.1-2017.

Because the exam is code-based, students should develop the ability to quickly find relevant sections, interpret tables, recognize defined terms, and connect inspection scenarios to the correct code provision. Good preparation includes repeated practice with the reference books, steady review of chapter organization, and familiarity with how commercial plumbing systems are inspected from installation through final approval.

Open Book Test

The ICC Commercial Plumbing Inspector P2 exam is an open book test. Open book does not mean easy. It means your exam performance depends heavily on how well you know your references, how quickly you can find the right section, and how accurately you can apply code language to a question.

Students preparing for this exam should spend time working directly with the 2021 International Plumbing Code, the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, and ICC A117.1-2017. The references should become familiar tools, not last-minute resources. You should know where to find definitions, fixture provisions, drainage tables, venting rules, water supply sizing requirements, fuel gas piping and combustion air provisions, accessibility dimensions, and inspection requirements for common commercial plumbing installations.

For an open book exam, organization matters. Tabbing, highlighting, indexing, and repeated code navigation practice can help reduce wasted time. The best preparation is not only learning the subject matter, but learning how the book is structured. Students should practice moving from a question to the correct topic, from the topic to the correct chapter, and from the chapter to the exact table, section, exception, or definition needed to answer accurately.

Reference Books

  • International Plumbing Code, 2021
    The primary plumbing code reference used for commercial plumbing inspection topics, including fixtures, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, special piping systems, storm drainage, materials, joints, connections, sizing, installation, and general plumbing provisions.
  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2021
    A key reference for fuel gas provisions connected to commercial plumbing inspection, including fuel gas piping, combustion air, venting, appliance-related requirements, installation conditions, safety provisions, and code requirements related to fuel gas systems.
  • ICC A117.1-2017 Standard for Accessible and Usable Buildings and Facilities
    The accessibility standard used for accessible and usable building features, including requirements that support inspection of accessible plumbing fixtures, clearances, heights, spacing, arrangement, and related accessibility provisions.

Test Information and Study Materials

The P2 Commercial Plumbing Inspector exam is built around inspection tasks. Students should study by topic and by reference, using the exam content areas as a roadmap. The main subject areas include general requirements, piping protection and installation, fixtures, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, separators, special piping, storage systems, and storm drainage.

Fixture-related questions may involve approved fixture materials, installation requirements, accessibility provisions, fixture clearances, waste receptors, appliances, sealing, appurtenances, and inspection of accessible fixture installations. Because the P2 exam includes accessibility-related fixture knowledge, students should be prepared to work with both the plumbing code and ICC A117.1-2017.

Water heater content may include installation conditions, safety devices, temperature and pressure relief requirements, discharge piping, thermal expansion, approved materials, clearances, appliance approval, combustion air, venting, and related fuel gas provisions. This is an area where students often need to connect the plumbing code and fuel gas code together.

Water supply and distribution is one of the most important study areas for commercial plumbing inspection. Students should be comfortable with approved materials, joints, connections, system installation, hot and cold water delivery, pressure requirements, volume requirements, pipe sizing, identification, valves, insulation, water treatment systems, potable water protection, and nonpotable water distribution.

Sanitary drainage and venting are also major exam areas. Students should practice using drainage sizing tables, stack and branch requirements, cleanout provisions, indirect waste rules, air gaps, air breaks, condensate drains, sumps, ejectors, backwater valves, vent sizing, vent installation, vent grades, and minimum venting requirements. Many exam questions require careful reading because a small condition, exception, or table note can change the correct answer.

For traps, interceptors, separators, special piping, and storage systems, students should focus on proper use, installation, connections, chemical treatment, high-temperature waste, neutralization systems, and inspection responsibilities. Storm drainage study should include roof drains, conductors, leaders, approved materials, sizing, sumps, sub-soil drains, and building sub-drain requirements.

Effective study materials should help you move from broad topic review into targeted code navigation. A strong study routine includes reviewing the major subject areas, working practice questions, locating the supporting code sections, reviewing mistakes, and returning to the reference books until the structure becomes familiar. Students should avoid relying only on memorization, because open book ICC exams reward accurate reference use and careful code interpretation.

How does the Commercial Plumbing Inspector (P2) exam work?

The Commercial Plumbing Inspector (P2) exam is a code official certification. It is taken after the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class and leads to DHCD certification.
