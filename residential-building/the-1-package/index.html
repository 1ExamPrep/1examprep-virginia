Exam Details

The Virginia Residential Building Contractor examination is the technical examination associated with the RBC classification. The examination is administered through PSI for the Virginia Board for Contractors, and successful completion of the required examination is part of the licensing process for the Qualified Individual.

The RBC examination contains 80 questions. Candidates receive 160 minutes to complete the examination and must achieve a minimum score of 70%, or 56 correct answers.

The examination covers a broad range of residential construction topics, including layout, excavation, foundations, framing, insulation, ventilation, windows, doors and skylights, exterior construction, decks, walks and ramps, finishing, masonry, swimming pools and hot tubs, prints, plans and specifications, safety and employee protection, and construction debris cleanup and disposal.

Residential construction experience provides an important foundation, but the examination can also require candidates to work with written code requirements, technical terminology, construction documents, tables, and reference material. A builder who is highly experienced in framing, for example, may still need additional review of code navigation, plans, foundations, masonry, safety, or contractor business topics.

For candidates pursuing Class A or Class B licensing, separate business examination requirements apply to the Designated Employee. These business requirements are distinct from the RBC technical examination completed by the Qualified Individual, which is why The 1 Package includes both residential trade references and Virginia contractor business materials.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Residential Building Contractor examination is an open-book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their permitted references because books are not supplied at the testing center.

Open-book testing makes reference familiarity an essential part of preparation. Having several large books available is useful only when candidates understand how to navigate them. Candidates should know which reference is most appropriate for a particular subject and be comfortable using indexes, tables of contents, chapters, technical tables, and code sections.

Permitted references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination according to applicable testing rules. Contractor business questions can require a different reference entirely.

With 160 minutes available for 80 questions, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes per question. Effective preparation therefore combines construction knowledge with selective reference use rather than attempting to search multiple books for every answer.

Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing combines a license class with one or more classifications or specialties. A company pursuing Residential Building Contracting must satisfy the requirements associated with both the selected contractor class and the RBC classification.

  1. Determine the appropriate contractor license class. Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
  2. Select Residential Building Contracting. The applicable classification abbreviation is RBC.
  3. Identify the Qualified Individual. The contractor business must identify a Qualified Individual for the RBC classification.
  4. Document the required experience. The Qualified Individual must satisfy the applicable experience requirement associated with the contractor class.
  5. Complete the RBC examination process. The Qualified Individual must satisfy the applicable technical examination requirement.
  6. Complete contractor pre-license education. New Virginia contractor license applicants must complete eight hours of Board-approved pre-license education.
  7. Complete applicable Class A or Class B business examinations. A Designated Employee must satisfy the applicable business examination requirements for these classes.
  8. Establish the business entity. The 1 Package includes Business Formation assistance for an LLC or Corporation.
  9. Obtain an EIN. EIN Filing with the IRS is included with this package.
  10. Complete the contractor application. Application Service is included to support the application portion of the licensing process.

The technical examination is only one piece of the complete process. The contractor business must also address its license class, Responsible Management, Qualified Individual, Designated Employee when applicable, pre-license education, business structure, and application requirements.

State Requirements

Virginia requires a Qualified Individual for every classification or specialty listed on a contractor license. The Qualified Individual must have relevant work experience within the scope of the classification being requested.

Current Virginia contractor requirements establish a minimum of one year of applicable experience for a Class C Qualified Individual, three years for Class B, and five years for Class A. The Qualified Individual must also meet the required relationship to the contractor business.

Residential Building is one of the classifications requiring the applicable examination process. Experience verification is part of the qualification process for designations that require examination approval.

Virginia also requires eight hours of Board-approved pre-license education for all new Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses. This pre-license course is a general contractor business course and is separate from the RBC technical examination.

Class A and Class B applicants must also address separate Designated Employee business examination requirements. These business examinations cover contractor management and Virginia-specific subjects and should be treated as a separate study track from the technical Residential Building examination.

The RBC classification focuses on residential construction within Virginia's defined scope. It does not independently authorize separately regulated electrical, plumbing, HVAC, or gas-fitting services. A contractor offering those services must have the applicable authority for that work.
