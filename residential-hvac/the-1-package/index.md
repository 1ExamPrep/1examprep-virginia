### State Requirements

Virginia's Residential HVAC Tradesman license became available as an additional residential licensing pathway beginning April 1, 2025. The credential applies to residential HVAC work and does not authorize commercial work.

Applicants must be at least **18 years old** and meet an approved combination of practical trade experience and formal vocational training. Current qualification pathways include:

- **2 years of practical experience** plus **160 hours of formal vocational training**.
- **3 years of practical experience** plus **120 hours of formal vocational training**.
- **4 years of practical experience** plus **80 hours of formal vocational training**.
- **5 years of practical experience** plus **40 hours of formal vocational training**.
- **6 or more years of practical experience** without using one of the listed vocational-training combinations.

Applicants using a vocational-training pathway must provide documentation of their formal training, while practical experience must be verified according to Virginia's application requirements.

The Virginia Residential Tradesman application is for individuals rather than business entities. Virginia also states that applicants performing contracting work above the applicable contractor licensing threshold must address a Virginia contractor license separately. The individual HVAC credential and contractor business license serve different purposes.

This distinction is especially important for candidates purchasing The 1 Package because the package includes business setup services in addition to individual Residential HVAC exam preparation. Business Formation and EIN Filing can help establish the legal and tax identity of the company, while Contractor Compliance Guidance addresses the separate regulatory considerations involved in operating as a contractor.

## How does the Residential HVAC Tradesman exam work?

The Residential HVAC Tradesman exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
