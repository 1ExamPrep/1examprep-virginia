Exam Details

The Virginia Painting and Wallcovering Contractor examination is administered as a Qualified Individual specialty examination. The exam contains 50 questions, allows 120 minutes for completion, and requires a minimum passing score of 70%, or 35 correct answers.

The examination content is organized into four primary subject areas:

  • General Knowledge: 16 questions
  • Materials and Application: 18 questions
  • Wall Coverings: 6 questions
  • Safety and Employee Protection: 10 questions

Materials and Application represents the largest portion of the examination. Candidates should devote significant study time to coatings, painting materials, preparation methods, application practices, surface conditions, tools, and related professional techniques. General Knowledge is another major category and requires candidates to draw on broad painting and decorating trade knowledge.

Safety and Employee Protection accounts for 10 examination questions. The included OSHA reference provides the construction safety regulations used for this area of study. Wall Coverings accounts for six questions and covers knowledge associated with surface coverings and related installation practices.

Virginia contractor licensing can also require separate business examination components for Class A and Class B applicants. The included NASCLA Contractor’s Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Virginia 11th Edition and the Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code, 2021 Edition, USBC provide important reference material for applicable contractor business and regulatory preparation.

Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing is administered through the Virginia Board for Contractors. A contractor license combines a license class with one or more classifications or specialties. Painting and Wallcovering Contracting is identified by the specialty abbreviation PTC.

  1. Determine your contractor license class. Virginia offers Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses. Qualification requirements differ depending on the selected class.
  2. Select the PTC specialty. Applicants seeking authority for painting and wallcovering work select Painting and Wallcovering Contracting.
  3. Identify the Qualified Individual. The contractor business must identify a Qualified Individual who meets the applicable requirements for the specialty.
  4. Meet the applicable experience requirement. Required experience varies according to contractor license class.
  5. Complete the required pre-license education. Virginia requires eight hours of approved pre-license education for new contractor licenses.
  6. Complete applicable examinations. Successful completion of required examinations must occur before applying for licensure.
  7. Meet Class A or Class B examination requirements when applicable. These license classes require a Designated Employee who completes the applicable contractor business examination portions.
  8. Complete the contractor application. Required business, Responsible Management, Qualified Individual, Designated Employee when applicable, and specialty information must be provided.
  9. Submit the application for review. The Virginia Board for Contractors reviews the completed licensing application before a license is issued.

State Requirements

Virginia contractor applicants must satisfy requirements associated with both the license class and the requested specialty. Painting and Wallcovering Contracting is a specialty that requires an appropriately qualified individual for the contractor business.

The Qualified Individual must meet Virginia's applicable trade experience requirements. The experience requirement depends on whether the business is applying for a Class A, Class B, or Class C contractor license. The Qualified Individual must also maintain the required relationship with the licensed business and satisfy applicable examination requirements.

All new Virginia contractor license applicants must satisfy the state's eight-hour pre-license education requirement. The pre-license course is a basic business course covering regulations, statutes, and requirements relevant to contractor businesses. It is separate from the Painting and Wallcovering specialty examination.

Class A and Class B businesses must also identify a Designated Employee who meets Virginia's applicable examination requirements. Class B Designated Employees complete the required General and Virginia portions, while Class A Designated Employees complete the General, Virginia, and Advanced portions.

The PTC specialty covers protective and decorative painting work, installation of specified surface coverings, and related preparation. Understanding the distinction between the specialty examination and the broader contractor licensing requirements helps candidates create a complete preparation plan.
