Exam Details

The Virginia Painting and Wall Covering Contractor examination is a specialty examination associated with the Painting and Wallcovering Contracting designation. The exam contains 50 questions. Candidates are given 120 minutes to complete the test and must earn a minimum score of 70%, or 35 correct answers.

The current examination content outline organizes the test into four major subject areas:

  • General Knowledge: 16 questions
  • Materials and Application: 18 questions
  • Wall Coverings: 6 questions
  • Safety and Employee Protection: 10 questions

Materials and Application represents the largest portion of the examination, followed by General Knowledge. Safety and Employee Protection also accounts for a significant part of the test, making familiarity with the OSHA construction reference important. Wall Coverings is a smaller section but remains part of the official examination outline and should not be overlooked during preparation.

The examination may include questions drawn from listed references as well as questions based on general trade knowledge and accepted industry practices. Candidates should therefore prepare by combining book study with practical knowledge of painting and wall covering work. Understanding terminology, materials, preparation procedures, application methods, jobsite safety, and wall covering practices can help create a more complete preparation strategy.

Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing is administered by the Virginia Board for Contractors through the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation. Contractor licenses combine a license class with a classification or specialty. For painting and wall covering work, the relevant specialty is Painting and Wallcovering Contracting, abbreviated PTC.

  1. Determine the appropriate Virginia contractor license class. Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses. The class determines the level of contracting authority and establishes different qualification requirements.
  2. Select the Painting and Wallcovering Contracting specialty. Applicants seeking authority to perform work within this trade select the PTC specialty designation.
  3. Identify the Qualified Individual. The contractor business must identify a Qualified Individual for the requested classification or specialty. The individual must meet Virginia's applicable relationship, experience, and qualification requirements.
  4. Complete required pre-license education. New Virginia contractor license applicants must satisfy the state's eight-hour pre-license education requirement.
  5. Complete the applicable examination requirement. Painting and Wallcovering Contracting is among the specialties that requires pre-approval and successful completion of the applicable trade examination.
  6. Complete the contractor application. The business must provide the information required for its license class, responsible management, Qualified Individual, specialty designation, and other applicable licensing requirements.
  7. Submit the completed application and required documentation. The Board reviews the application before licensing is granted.

The PTC trade examination is one component of the overall licensing process. Applicants pursuing Class A or Class B licensing may also have examination and designated-employee requirements associated with those license classes. The highlighted and tabbed book package is intended for preparation for the Painting and Wall Covering specialty examination and does not replace required pre-license education or other licensing obligations.

State Requirements

Virginia requires contractor applicants to meet qualifications associated with both the contractor license class and the classification or specialty being requested. For the Painting and Wallcovering Contracting specialty, the business must identify a Qualified Individual who meets the applicable requirements for the requested license class.

The Qualified Individual must have the minimum trade experience required for the class of contractor license. Virginia requires at least one year of experience for a Class C Qualified Individual, three years for Class B, and five years for Class A. The Qualified Individual must also have the required relationship to the contractor business and successfully complete the appropriate prerequisite or examination applicable to the specialty.

New applicants for Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses must also complete eight hours of Board-approved pre-license education. This course covers general business and regulatory information and is separate from the trade-specific Painting and Wall Covering examination.

The Painting and Wallcovering Contracting specialty includes applying materials commonly used in the painting and decorating industry for protective or decorative purposes. It also includes installing surface coverings such as vinyl, wallpaper, and cloth fabrics and performing related surface preparation such as caulking, sanding, and cleaning. Both interior and exterior work fall within the specialty definition.

Applicants should consider the complete licensing structure when preparing. Passing the PTC examination alone does not automatically issue a contractor license. The business must complete all requirements applicable to its chosen class and specialty and receive approval through the Virginia Board for Contractors licensing process.
