Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing is administered by the Virginia Board for Contractors through the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation. A contractor license consists of a license class and a classification or specialty. The class determines the level of contracting activity permitted, while the classification or specialty determines the type of work the business may perform.

  1. Select the appropriate contractor license class. Virginia contractor licenses are issued as Class A, Class B, or Class C. Each class has different qualification requirements.
  2. Select the Painting and Wallcovering Contracting specialty. The Virginia specialty abbreviation is PTC.
  3. Identify the Qualified Individual. The contractor business must identify a Qualified Individual for each requested classification or specialty.
  4. Meet applicable experience requirements. Required trade experience depends on the contractor license class being sought.
  5. Complete pre-license education. New Virginia contractor licenses require Board-approved pre-license education.
  6. Complete applicable examinations. Candidates must satisfy examination requirements associated with the trade specialty and, when applicable, the contractor license class.
  7. Complete the contractor license application. The business must provide the required information regarding responsible management, the Qualified Individual, license class, specialty, and other applicable requirements.
  8. Submit the application for Board review. Licensure is issued after applicable requirements are satisfied and the application is approved.

The PTC trade examination and Virginia contractor business requirements should be treated as separate parts of the licensing process. This package includes references that support preparation across both trade-specific and contractor business-law subject areas.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors licenses businesses engaged in construction, removal, repair, or improvement work on property owned by others. Contractor licensing combines a Class A, Class B, or Class C designation with one or more classifications or specialties.

For Painting and Wallcovering Contracting, the business must identify a Qualified Individual who meets the applicable requirements. Virginia regulations define the PTC specialty as work involving materials common to the painting and decorating industry for protective or decorative purposes, installation of surface coverings such as vinyl, wallpaper, and cloth fabrics, and surface preparation including caulking, sanding, and cleaning. The specialty includes interior and exterior surfaces.

New contractor license applicants must also satisfy Virginia's required pre-license education. The pre-license education course is a business-focused requirement covering regulations, statutes, and requirements relevant to contractor businesses. It is separate from trade-exam preparation.

Class A and Class B contractor applicants also have requirements involving a designated employee and applicable business examination components. Candidates pursuing these license classes should prepare for the business, law, project management, regulatory, and code subjects applicable to their licensing path in addition to the PTC trade examination.
