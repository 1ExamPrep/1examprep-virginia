Exam Details

The Virginia Master Natural Gas Fitter Provider credential is regulated through the Virginia Board for Contractors. Virginia treats Natural Gas Fitter Provider as a distinct individual trade designation. Candidates must satisfy the applicable qualification requirements and receive Board pre-approval before sitting for the examination administered through PSI.

Virginia defines a natural gas fitter provider as an individual performing specified natural gas piping or fitting work for the public for compensation. The regulated scope includes certain incidental repair, testing, or removal of natural gas piping or fittings attached to real property, subject to the limitations and exclusions established by Virginia regulations.

The Master designation represents a higher level of proficiency and qualification within the regulated trade. Candidates preparing at the Master level should understand natural gas systems well enough to work with technical requirements, plan and interpret piping-related information, and apply applicable code provisions.

Important preparation areas include natural gas piping systems, pipe sizing, gas-system controls, testing, fuel gas code requirements, Virginia licensing provisions, professional requirements, and reference navigation. Candidates should be able to recognize which technical subject a question addresses and determine where the supporting information is located.

Virginia currently requires Board pre-approval before candidates may sit for tradesman examinations. Examination administration is handled through PSI after approval has been granted.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Natural Gasfitter examination is open book. Candidates should prepare by working directly with the permitted technical references and becoming familiar with how each book is structured.

Open-book testing does not mean candidates can depend on searching every page during the examination. Technical references contain extensive information, and efficient lookup requires practice. Candidates should develop enough subject knowledge to identify where to begin searching before opening the book.

A Virginia licensing or professional-requirements question may direct the candidate toward the Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations. Questions involving natural gas piping, pipe sizing, controls, installation, or testing may require the International Fuel Gas Code, 2021.

Reference materials should be prepared according to current testing rules. Candidates who highlight, underline, index, or tab their books should make sure the preparation method complies with PSI requirements before arriving at the examination.

A useful reference-navigation routine is to read the question, identify the subject, select the most appropriate reference, locate the chapter or table, and confirm the applicable requirement. This process should be practiced during both live virtual instruction and self-study.

Students should also become comfortable using indexes and definitions. Exam questions may use technical terminology that directs candidates toward a specific code section. Understanding the language of the trade can make reference searches significantly more efficient.

Licensing Steps

The Virginia Master Natural Gas Fitter Provider credential is an individual license. Candidates must satisfy the Board's current qualification, application, and examination requirements before receiving the license.

  1. Review the qualification requirements. Determine the education, training, experience, or existing-license pathway that applies to the Master Natural Gas Fitter Provider designation.
  2. Document the required qualifications. Candidates must provide the information requested by the Virginia Board for Contractors to demonstrate eligibility.
  3. Submit the applicable application. The Board reviews the candidate's qualifications before examination authorization is issued.
  4. Receive Board approval to test. Virginia requires pre-approval before candidates may sit for the applicable tradesman examination.
  5. Schedule the examination through PSI. After receiving approval, candidates can proceed with the testing provider's registration process.
  6. Prepare with the technical references. Study Virginia regulations and the fuel gas code materials while practicing natural gas piping, sizing, controls, and testing.
  7. Complete the required examination. Candidates must meet the examination requirement applicable to the Master Natural Gas Fitter Provider credential.
  8. Complete remaining licensing requirements. Any outstanding Board requirements must be satisfied before the individual license is issued.

Candidates planning to work through a contracting business should understand that the individual Master Natural Gas Fitter Provider license is separate from the Virginia contractor business license. A contractor company must independently satisfy the requirements for its class and specialty.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates natural gas fitting through the Tradesmen Program. The Natural Gas Fitter Provider designation is an individual credential and should not be confused with a contractor business license.

Virginia contractor businesses are separately licensed as Class A, Class B, or Class C contractors. Each business must also hold the appropriate classification or specialty for the type of contracting work it performs.

For a contractor business seeking the Natural Gas Fitting Provider specialty, identified as NGF, Virginia contractor regulations identify a Master Natural Gas Fitter Provider license issued by the Board for Contractors as the applicable Qualified Individual credential.

This means the individual Master Natural Gas Fitter Provider license can serve as an important qualification for the person associated with an NGF contracting business, but the business must still satisfy the remaining contractor licensing requirements.

Virginia also requires continuing education for individuals holding natural gas licenses. Current Board requirements call for one hour of approved continuing education for the natural gas trade during the applicable renewal cycle. The continuing education course must cover the International Fuel Gas Code and be approved by the Board for Contractors.

Continuing education is separate from exam preparation. Live virtual and self-study exam training is designed to help candidates prepare for the initial licensing examination, while continuing education used for license renewal must meet the Board's separate course-approval requirements.

Reference Books

  • Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations
    This Virginia regulatory reference addresses requirements governing individual trade licenses and certifications. Candidates can use it to review licensing terminology, qualifications, professional responsibilities, renewal provisions, and other state-specific requirements. Familiarity with the structure of the regulations can help candidates locate regulatory information more efficiently.
  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2021
    The 2021 International Fuel Gas Code is a primary technical reference for fuel gas systems. It addresses piping materials, installation, pipe sizing, valves, regulators, controls, appliances, system testing, and other fuel gas requirements. Candidates should practice locating sizing tables, definitions, installation provisions, and testing requirements throughout their preparation.
  • NFPA 58 - Standard for Liquid Petroleum Gas Code, 2014
    NFPA 58 contains requirements involving liquefied petroleum gas systems, including containers, tanks, piping, storage, transfer, installation, and safety provisions. This reference is included in the study set and supports familiarity with broader gas-system requirements.

Candidates should also learn how to work with the entire reference rather than relying only on memorized section numbers. Index terminology, definitions, cross-references, and chapter structure can help locate information even when an exam question is worded differently than expected.

How does the Master Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

The Master Natural Gas Fitter exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
