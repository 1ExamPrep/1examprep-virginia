### Exam Details

The Virginia Master Natural Gasfitter examination is part of the individual tradesman licensing program administered by the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation through the Board for Contractors. Examination services are provided by PSI, and applicants must obtain authorization through the Board’s application process before scheduling the exam.

The examination is designed to confirm that candidates possess the knowledge needed to perform and supervise natural gas fitting work at the Master level. Exam preparation should cover both regulatory responsibilities and technical trade subjects. Candidates may be tested on standards of practice, standards of conduct, licensing qualifications, renewal and reinstatement, disciplinary provisions, gas piping installation, pipe sizing, piping controls, and testing procedures.

The technical portion requires candidates to work with fuel gas requirements and recognize how code provisions apply to actual installations. Questions may involve approved piping materials, fittings, valves, regulators, appliance connections, system demand, developed pipe length, pressure limitations, allowable pressure drop, testing methods, and installation safety.

Pipe-sizing questions require careful use of code tables. Candidates must identify the applicable fuel, system pressure, demand, developed length, and sizing method before selecting an answer. Strong familiarity with the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code can reduce the time needed to find the correct table and interpret its notes.

The state-regulation portion is equally important. Master-level professionals are responsible for complying with licensing rules and professional standards, not only performing technical work. Candidates should understand the definitions used by the Board, conduct expected of licensed tradespeople, renewal responsibilities, qualification pathways, and actions that may result in disciplinary proceedings.

The examination is computer-delivered. Candidates answer questions at an approved testing location while using reference materials permitted under the current examination rules. Examination registration fees charged by PSI are paid separately and are not included in the package price.

### Open Book Test

The Virginia Master Natural Gasfitter examination is an open-book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own permitted references to the testing center. Reference books are not supplied by the examination location.

An open-book format allows candidates to consult approved materials, but it does not eliminate the need for focused preparation. Exam time is limited, and candidates who are unfamiliar with the books may spend too long searching for individual answers. Successful preparation should include learning the organization of each reference, identifying major chapters, practicing with indexes, and locating frequently tested tables.

The Virginia regulations book is used for questions concerning licensing qualifications, professional conduct, standards of practice, renewal, reinstatement, and regulatory definitions. The International Fuel Gas Code supports questions involving natural gas piping, pipe sizing, materials, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, pressure regulation, and testing. NFPA 58 addresses liquefied petroleum gas systems and related installation and safety requirements.

Candidates should prepare their books in accordance with PSI’s current reference-material rules. Permitted highlighting, underlining, and permanent indexing can make important sections easier to locate. Removable tabs, loose papers, and unbound materials are generally not allowed. Written notes may also be restricted. Every reference should be reviewed before test day to confirm that it complies with the testing policy.

Timed practice is especially valuable for an open-book exam. Candidates should practice reading a question, identifying the relevant subject, selecting the correct reference, and locating the applicable section without searching multiple books unnecessarily. This process builds speed and reduces uncertainty during the examination.

### State Requirements

Virginia regulates individual tradespeople and contractor businesses through related but separate licensing processes. The Master Natural Gasfitter credential is issued to an individual who satisfies the applicable experience, application, and examination requirements. A Virginia contractor license is issued to the business entity that offers or performs contracting work.

A customer who plans to operate through an LLC or corporation must establish the business entity and complete the contractor licensing requirements that apply to the selected license class and specialty. Forming a business entity alone does not authorize the company to offer regulated natural gas fitting services.

The contractor business application identifies responsible management, the Qualified Individual, and, when required, the Designated Employee. Responsible management generally includes the owners or officers who control the business. The Qualified Individual provides the trade qualification for the classification or specialty. Class A and Class B contractor applicants also have business examination requirements for the Designated Employee.

For the Virginia natural gas fitting provider specialty, the Qualified Individual must hold the appropriate current Master-level credential issued by the Board. The individual license therefore serves an important role in the contractor company’s specialty qualification.

Contractor license classes are based on the size and value of projects the business intends to undertake. Each class has its own experience, financial, education, and examination requirements. The customer should select a license class that matches the anticipated scope of business rather than choosing solely by company size or personal preference.

Virginia contractor applicants must complete the required pre-license education before submitting the business license application. The appropriate person must complete the course and be identified on the application. Class A and Class B applicants must also satisfy the applicable business examination requirements.

Business information should remain consistent across entity formation records, tax registration, insurance documents, examination records, and contractor applications. Differences in names, addresses, ownership information, or business type may delay processing.

Local requirements may apply in addition to state licensing. Depending on the city or county, a contracting business may need local registration, a business license, permits, inspections, or tax accounts. State licensing does not replace local building department or business requirements.

## How does the Master Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

The Master Natural Gas Fitter exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
