### Open Book Test

The Virginia Master Natural Gasfitter exam is an **open-book test**. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved references to the examination. Reference books are not provided at the test site.

Open-book testing requires preparation before exam day. Candidates should not expect to search through unfamiliar books for the first time during the test. With 50 questions and 150 minutes, students have an average of three minutes per question. Some questions may be answered quickly, while others may require careful code lookup, table review, or regulatory reading.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam when prepared according to the testing rules. Study guides are not permitted in the examination room. References containing writing are not allowed, and candidates may not write in the reference materials during the exam. Permanent tabs are allowed, while removable tabs are not allowed. Loose papers and unbound references are not permitted.

Because the exam is open book, students should build a clear system for using the books. Good preparation includes learning each reference’s table of contents, index, chapter structure, definitions, tables, and common topic locations. Pipe sizing, testing, gas piping controls, LP-gas provisions, and regulatory questions often require candidates to know where to look and how to interpret the information once they find it.

Students should also practice identifying keywords in exam-style questions. A question about professional conduct may point to the Virginia regulations. A question about LP-gas containers or components may point to NFPA 58. A question about fuel gas pipe sizing or appliance installation may point to the International Fuel Gas Code. Choosing the correct book quickly is an important exam skill.

## How does the Master Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

The Master Natural Gas Fitter exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
