Exam Details

The Virginia Master Natural Gasfitter exam is part of Virginia’s individual tradesman licensing and certification examination program. The exam is administered for candidates pursuing the master-level natural gasfitter credential through the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation and the Virginia Board for Contractors.

The Master Natural Gasfitter exam contains 50 questions. Candidates are allowed 150 minutes to complete the exam. The passing score is 37 correct answers. Because the exam is timed, candidates should prepare to answer both knowledge-based questions and reference-based questions efficiently.

The exam includes technical gasfitting topics and Virginia regulatory content. Candidates should prepare for natural gas piping, LP-gas provisions where referenced, pipe sizing, gas piping controls, system testing, fuel gas code requirements, and professional licensing responsibilities. The listed references support these areas and should be studied together as part of a complete preparation plan.

The International Fuel Gas Code, 2021 is used for fuel gas system requirements, including piping, materials, installation, combustion air, venting, appliances, inspections, testing, and safety provisions. Candidates should become familiar with how the book is organized and how to locate tables, definitions, and sections related to gasfitting work.

NFPA 58 - Standard for Liquid Petroleum Gas Code, 2014 supports LP-gas topics, including storage, handling, installation, system components, piping, and safety requirements. Students should understand where LP-gas requirements are located and how they differ from general fuel gas provisions. Questions involving LP-gas may require candidates to choose the correct reference before finding the correct answer.

The Virginia Board for Contractors, Individual License and Certification Regulations supports the regulatory portion of the exam. This reference includes licensing rules, definitions, qualifications, standards of practice, standards of conduct, renewal, reinstatement, and disciplinary provisions. Candidates should not overlook this book, because regulatory questions can affect the final score.

The business books included in this package support Virginia contractor business preparation. The NASCLA Contractor’s Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, Virginia 11th Edition helps students review business organization, licensing, contracts, estimating, project management, employment responsibilities, insurance, lien law, financial management, taxes, safety, and Virginia-specific contractor responsibilities. The Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code (USBC), Part 1 supports preparation for code administration, permits, inspections, enforcement, and construction compliance topics.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Master Natural Gasfitter exam is an open-book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved references to the examination. Reference books are not provided at the test site.

Open-book testing requires preparation before exam day. Candidates should not expect to search through unfamiliar books for the first time during the test. With 50 questions and 150 minutes, students have an average of three minutes per question. Some questions may be answered quickly, while others may require careful code lookup, table review, or regulatory reading.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam when prepared according to the testing rules. Study guides are not permitted in the examination room. References containing writing are not allowed, and candidates may not write in the reference materials during the exam. Permanent tabs are allowed, while removable tabs are not allowed. Loose papers and unbound references are not permitted.

Because the exam is open book, students should build a clear system for using the books. Good preparation includes learning each reference’s table of contents, index, chapter structure, definitions, tables, and common topic locations. Pipe sizing, testing, gas piping controls, LP-gas provisions, and regulatory questions often require candidates to know where to look and how to interpret the information once they find it.

Students should also practice identifying keywords in exam-style questions. A question about professional conduct may point to the Virginia regulations. A question about LP-gas containers or components may point to NFPA 58. A question about fuel gas pipe sizing or appliance installation may point to the International Fuel Gas Code. Choosing the correct book quickly is an important exam skill.

How does the Master Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

The Master Natural Gas Fitter exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
