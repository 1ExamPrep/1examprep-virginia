Exam Details

The Virginia Master Natural Gas Fitter Provider credential is regulated through the Virginia Board for Contractors. Virginia identifies Natural Gas Fitter Provider as a distinct individual license designation, separate from the general Gas Fitter and Liquefied Petroleum Gas Fitter designations.

Virginia defines a natural gas fitter provider as an individual who performs or offers specified natural gas piping or fitting work for the public for compensation. The regulated scope includes incidental repair, testing, or removal of natural gas piping or fittings attached to real property. Virginia's definition excludes certain new natural gas piping installations associated with equipment requiring HVAC or plumbing certification.

The Master designation reflects a higher level of trade proficiency and qualification. Virginia defines a Master as an individual possessing the ability, proficiency, and qualifications necessary to plan and lay out installation details and supervise applicable installation, repair, and maintenance work while working in accordance with relevant Virginia building-code requirements.

Preparation for the Master Natural Gasfitter examination should emphasize natural gas piping systems, pipe sizing, gas-system controls, testing, technical code navigation, and applicable Virginia licensing requirements. Candidates should be prepared to work with both trade concepts and regulatory material.

Virginia requires Board pre-approval before candidates may sit for the applicable tradesman examination. After approval, examination scheduling is handled through PSI. Candidates should complete the applicable application and qualification requirements before attempting to schedule the exam.

How does the Master Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

The Master Natural Gas Fitter exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
