The Virginia Master Natural Gasfitter credential is regulated through the Virginia Board for Contractors. Natural gas fitting is treated as a specialized individual credential, and candidates must satisfy the applicable Virginia requirements before receiving authorization to take the examination.

Preparation for the Master Natural Gasfitter exam should emphasize natural gas piping systems, pipe sizing, gas piping controls, testing procedures, applicable code provisions, and Virginia regulatory requirements. Licensing Steps

The Virginia Master Natural Gasfitter credential is an individual license associated with natural gas fitting work. The licensing process is separate from the contractor business license process.

  1. Review the applicable Virginia qualification requirements. Candidates should determine the education, training, experience, and licensing pathway that applies to the Master Natural Gasfitter credential.
  2. Prepare the required application information. Applicants must provide the information requested by the Virginia Board for Contractors to establish eligibility.
  3. Submit the applicable application. The Board reviews the candidate’s qualifications before examination authorization is issued.
  4. Receive Board approval to test. Virginia requires pre-approval before tradesman candidates can sit for the examination.
  5. Schedule the examination through PSI. Approved candidates can proceed with the applicable examination scheduling process.
  6. Prepare with the technical references. Candidates should use the Virginia regulations, International Fuel Gas Code, and other applicable references throughout their preparation.
  7. Complete the required examination. Candidates must satisfy the examination requirements applicable to the Master Natural Gasfitter credential.
  8. Complete remaining licensing requirements. After satisfying the examination requirement, candidates must complete any remaining Board requirements before the credential is issued.

Candidates planning to operate through a contracting business should understand that the individual Master Natural Gas Fitter credential and the contractor business license are separate. Virginia contractor firms are licensed independently and must meet the requirements for the appropriate license class and specialty.

State Requirements

Virginia defines a natural gas fitter provider as an individual performing specified natural gas piping or fitting work for the public for compensation. The regulatory definition focuses on incidental repair, testing, or removal of natural gas piping or fitting attached to real property and identifies exclusions for new installation associated with certain equipment that requires other trade credentials.

For a Virginia contractor business seeking the Natural Gas Fitting Provider specialty, identified as NGF, the applicable Qualified Individual credential is a valid Master Natural Gas Fitter license issued by the Board for Contractors. The contractor business must still meet the separate requirements applying to its contractor license.

This distinction is important for candidates planning a contracting career. Earning an individual Master Natural Gasfitter credential establishes the applicable individual qualification, but it does not automatically create a licensed contractor business.

Virginia also requires continuing education for natural gas fitters as part of license renewal. The Board currently requires one hour of continuing education for the natural gas trade, and the course must cover the International Fuel Gas Code. Continuing education must be completed through an approved provider to count toward renewal.

How does the Master Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

The Master Natural Gas Fitter exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.
