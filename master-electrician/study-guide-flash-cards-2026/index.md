### Open Book Test

The Virginia Master Electrician examination is an **open book test**. Candidates must bring their own approved references because references are not provided at the test site. No study guides are permitted in the examination room.

Virginia exam rules allow references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Reference materials containing writing are not allowed into the examination. Candidates may not write in reference materials during the examination session. References may be tabbed with permanent tabs only. Removable tabs are not allowed.

No loose papers or unbound references are permitted. Downloaded references must be properly bound, such as spiral bound, hole-punched and placed in a binder, or stapled in the left-hand corner, according to the examination rules. Candidates should follow PSI’s current instructions for identification, scheduling, calculator use, remote proctoring, test center rules, and exam security.

Open-book electrical exams still require serious preparation. The exam is not simply about having reference books available. Candidates need to understand how the National Electrical Code is organized, how definitions control meaning, how tables apply, how exceptions are written, and how to move quickly from the question topic to the correct Article, section, table, or calculation method.

Virginia’s Master Electrician license is an individual tradesman license. Candidates must meet the entry requirements set by Virginia law and Board regulations. Virginia provides several routes to qualify for individual tradesman licensure. Candidates may qualify through a combination of practical experience and formal vocational training, apprenticeship completion, an associate degree or certificate of completion from an approved program, a bachelor’s degree in an engineering curriculum related to the trade, or prior licensure and experience. Virginia distinguishes individual tradesman licensing from contractor business licensing. A Master Electrician license supports individual trade qualification, but a business that contracts for electrical work may also need the appropriate Virginia contractor license, class, classification, qualified individual, designated employee, and business compliance documents. Candidates should match the license path to the type of work and business activity they plan to perform.

## How does the Master Electrician exam work?

The Master Electrician exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
