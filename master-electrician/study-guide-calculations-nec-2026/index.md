### Open Book Test

The Virginia tradesman candidate bulletin states that the examinations are open book. Candidates must bring their own references because references are not provided at the test site. The bulletin also states that no study guides are permitted in the examination room, no loose papers or unbound references are permitted, and reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, or indexed before the exam.

Reference materials containing writing are not allowed into the examination. Candidates may not write in reference materials during the examination session. Permanent tabs are allowed, while removable tabs are not allowed. These rules make preparation with your books especially important before exam day. ## How does the Master Electrician exam work?

The Master Electrician exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
