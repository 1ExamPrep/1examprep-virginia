### Open Book Test

The Virginia tradesman examinations are open book. Candidates must bring their own approved references because references are not provided at the test site. Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam. Permanent tabs are allowed, but removable tabs are not allowed.

The open-book format makes preparation even more important. Having a book available does not automatically make the exam simple. Candidates still need to know where information is located, how to read code language, how to compare answer choices, and how to work within the time allowed. The best preparation includes repeated codebook practice, timed question review, and careful study of the exam outline.

Virginia exam rules also place limits on materials used during testing. No loose papers or unbound references are permitted. Downloaded internet references must be properly bound, hole-punched in a binder, or stapled in the left-hand corner. Reference materials containing writing are not allowed into the examination. Candidates may not write in reference materials during the exam session. Study guides are not permitted as exam-room references, so the study guides in this package should be used for preparation before test day.

The included National Electrical Code 2023 Paperback with Tabs is especially useful during study because it helps you practice finding code sections quickly. For an open-book exam, the goal is to be comfortable using the book before the exam begins. You should know how the code is organized, where major articles are located, and how to move from a question to the correct code section efficiently.

## How does the Master Electrician exam work?

The Master Electrician exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
