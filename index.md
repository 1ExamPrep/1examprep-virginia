## How do Virginia contractor license classes work?

The class decides how large a job your firm may take. The trade classification on the license decides what kind of work it may do, and each classification has its own exam.

Class | Single contract | Total in 12 months | Exams
Class C | $1,000 to under $10,000 | Under $150,000 | 8-hour pre-license course; no PSI exam for most classifications
Class B | $10,000 to under $120,000 | $150,000 to under $750,000 | Virginia and general business portions plus the trade exam
Class A | $120,000 or more | $750,000 or more | Virginia, general and advanced business portions plus the trade exam

Limits from section 54.1-1100 of the Code of Virginia. Check the [Board for Contractors](https://www.dpor.virginia.gov/Boards/Contractors) before applying, since limits and exam requirements change.

## What do people ask before buying?

### Which Virginia license do I need?

The right license depends on the work and the size of the jobs. Contractor firms need a Class A, B or C license with a classification for each trade they perform; individual electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians and gas fitters need a tradesman license; and building inspectors and plans examiners need DHCD certification.

### Are the Virginia contractor exams open book?

Yes. The exams PSI runs for the Board for Contractors are open book. You bring your own references, which may be highlighted and permanently tabbed; removable flags and loose notes are not allowed.

### Do I need the 8-hour pre-license course?

Yes, for any Class A, B or C contractor license. The course is 8 hours and is taken by a member of responsible management or, for Class A and B, the designated employee.

### What do the exams cost?

Contractor exams cost $40 for one portion, $72 for two and $85 for three. Tradesman exams cost $100 for journeyman and $125 for master. Fees are paid to PSI, separately from the board's application fee.

### Are these the real exam questions?

No. Licensing exam questions are confidential. The courses and practice exams here are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

### Does buying a package register me for the exam?

No. The Board for Contractors, or DHCD for code officials, approves you first; PSI or ICC then schedules the exam. A package gets you ready for the test itself.
