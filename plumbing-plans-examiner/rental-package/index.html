Exam Details

The ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam is a national certification exam offered by the International Code Council. It is associated with plumbing plan review and evaluates a candidate’s ability to review plumbing construction documents for compliance with plumbing code, fuel gas code, and related accessibility requirements.

The ICC P3 exam is listed as a 60-question multiple-choice exam with a 2 1/2-hour time limit. Candidates should expect questions that require code lookup, interpretation, calculation, table use, and practical application of plumbing plan review requirements. The 2021 version of the exam is connected to the 2021 International Plumbing Code, the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, and ICC A117.1-2017 Standard for Accessible and Usable Buildings and Facilities.

Major study areas for the P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam include general regulations, administrative requirements, plumbing fixtures and fixture counts, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, special waste, indirect waste, storm drainage, fuel gas provisions, accessibility requirements, and plumbing plan review procedures. Candidates should be prepared to apply code requirements to submitted construction documents rather than simply recall isolated facts.

Plumbing plan review questions may require candidates to determine required plumbing fixtures, evaluate fixture schedules, calculate drainage fixture units, review water supply sizing, evaluate sanitary drainage design, check venting systems, determine storm drainage requirements, identify interceptor requirements, review indirect waste provisions, apply fuel gas requirements, or evaluate accessible fixture layout requirements.

The 2021 International Plumbing Code is the central reference for plumbing plan review. Candidates should become familiar with chapters covering administration, definitions, general regulations, fixtures, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, indirect waste, vents, traps, interceptors, special piping, and storm drainage.

The 2021 International Fuel Gas Code supports exam preparation for fuel gas system requirements. Candidates should study fuel gas piping, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, installation requirements, pipe sizing concepts, and safety provisions that may appear in plumbing plan review situations.

ICC A117.1-2017 supports preparation for accessibility-related plumbing fixture requirements. Candidates should study accessible routes, clear floor spaces, reach ranges, toilet compartments, water closets, lavatories, sinks, drinking fountains, bathing fixtures, and related dimensional requirements that can affect plumbing plan review.

Open book testing does not mean the exam is easy. It means candidates must know how to use the references efficiently, accurately, and under time pressure.

For an open book plumbing plans examiner exam, speed and familiarity are essential. Test Information and Study Materials

The best preparation for the ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam begins with direct use of the 2021 International Plumbing Code, the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, and ICC A117.1-2017. Candidates should study from the same references used for exam preparation so the books become familiar before test day.

Start by learning the structure of the International Plumbing Code. Review the table of contents, definitions, chapter titles, key tables, figures, and index. Plumbing plans examiner candidates should understand where to find administration, general regulations, fixtures, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, indirect waste, vents, traps, interceptors, special piping, and storm drainage provisions.

Fixture count preparation should be a major part of the study routine. Candidates should review occupancy-based fixture requirements, fixture schedules, required fixture calculations, and how fixture requirements are shown on submitted plans. Plan review questions may require candidates to identify whether enough fixtures are provided or whether accessible fixture requirements apply.

Water supply and distribution should receive focused review. Candidates should study water supply fixture units, sizing concepts, pressure considerations, pipe materials, protection of potable water, backflow prevention, hot water distribution, and related design provisions. Questions may require candidates to use tables or evaluate whether the submitted documents include enough information for review.

Sanitary drainage is central to plumbing plan review. Candidates should review drainage fixture units, building drains, branches, stacks, cleanouts, pipe slope, backwater protection, drainage materials, fittings, and sizing tables. Questions may require candidates to evaluate proposed drainage design or calculate requirements from fixture information.

Venting should be studied in detail. Candidates should review vent sizing, vent terminals, trap-to-vent relationships, wet venting, common venting methods, circuit venting, combination waste and vent systems, island fixture venting, and air admittance valves where permitted by the code. Venting questions often require identifying the system type before applying the requirement.

Storm drainage preparation should include roof drainage, secondary drainage, overflow requirements, leaders, conductors, horizontal storm drains, roof area, rainfall rates, and sizing tables. Plan review questions may require candidates to work through roof drainage information and select the applicable provision or table.

Accessibility preparation should include regular use of ICC A117.1-2017. Candidates should review accessible plumbing fixtures, clear floor spaces, turning spaces, toilet compartments, water closets, lavatories, sinks, drinking fountains, reach ranges, knee clearance, toe clearance, and fixture heights. Accessibility questions often depend on exact dimensions, so candidates should practice locating requirements quickly.

The International Fuel Gas Code should be included in regular study. Candidates should review fuel gas piping materials, joints, fittings, pipe sizing concepts, protection from damage, appliance connections, shutoff valves, testing, combustion air, chimneys, vents, and appliance installation requirements. Plumbing plan review work may overlap with fuel gas requirements, so candidates should know when to use the fuel gas reference.

Definitions should be reviewed throughout preparation. Many exam questions turn on the exact meaning of a term. When a question uses words related to plumbing fixture, trap, vent, drainage system, building drain, indirect waste, interceptor, potable water, accessible route, clear floor space, fuel gas, or approved construction documents, definitions may help guide the correct answer.

Use the rental books actively during study. Practice locating sections by topic, using the indexes, reading surrounding code language, checking table notes, and following cross-references. The books become more useful when they are part of regular study practice rather than something opened only near exam day.

Timed practice is one of the most important study habits. Since the P3 exam has 60 questions and a 2 1/2-hour time limit, candidates should develop a pacing strategy. Practice answering questions under time pressure, mark longer calculation or table-based questions when needed, and return after completing faster questions. This helps build confidence and reduces exam-day stress.
