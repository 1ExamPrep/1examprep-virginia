Open Book Test

The ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam is an open book test. Candidates should prepare to use the approved references efficiently during the exam. Open book testing does not remove the need to study. It makes reference navigation one of the most important skills a candidate can develop.

Because the exam is timed, candidates should not rely on searching through unfamiliar books on exam day. The best preparation includes repeated practice locating code sections, using indexes, reading tables, checking definitions, and confirming answers from the code language. Candidates should learn which topics are found in the International Plumbing Code, which topics are found in the International Fuel Gas Code, and when accessibility provisions from ICC A117.1-2017 may apply.

For the International Plumbing Code, candidates should become familiar with chapters covering general regulations, fixtures, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, indirect waste, vents, traps, interceptors, special piping, and storm drainage. For the International Fuel Gas Code, candidates should study the layout of fuel gas piping, combustion air, venting, and appliance-related requirements. For ICC A117.1-2017, candidates should focus on accessible plumbing fixture provisions, including clearances, heights, reach ranges, and approach requirements.

Timed practice is essential. A useful method is to read a question, identify the subject, select the correct reference, locate the applicable section, and verify the answer from the code text. Candidates should practice this process until it becomes natural. The goal is to build speed without sacrificing accuracy.

Plumbing plans examiner questions often include details that affect the answer. A question may depend on occupancy type, fixture count, pipe size, drainage fixture units, venting method, roof area, rainfall rate, accessibility dimension, or fuel gas installation condition. Careful reading is just as important as fast code navigation.

Test Information and Study Materials

The best preparation for the ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam begins with direct use of the code books. Candidates should not treat the books as last-minute references. They should become familiar tools used throughout the entire study process.

Start with the 2021 International Plumbing Code. Review the table of contents, definitions, and major chapter organization. Pay close attention to fixture requirements, water heater provisions, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, indirect waste, venting, traps, interceptors, special piping, and storm drainage. These areas are central to plumbing plan review.

Fixture count preparation should include reviewing minimum plumbing fixture requirements, fixture schedules, occupancy-related provisions, and the way fixture counts are documented on plans. Candidates should practice locating the correct tables and applying them to plan-review scenarios.

Water supply preparation should include water supply fixture units, pipe sizing, pressure requirements, backflow protection, hot water distribution, and related design considerations. Plans examiner questions may require candidates to evaluate whether a proposed system is properly shown or whether additional information is needed.

Sanitary drainage preparation should include drainage fixture units, building drains, branches, stacks, cleanouts, pipe sizing, slope, backwater valves, and materials. Candidates should practice using drainage tables and understanding how fixture connections affect system design.

Venting is another major study area. Candidates should review vent sizing, vent terminals, trap-to-vent relationships, common venting methods, wet venting, circuit venting, combination waste and vent systems, island fixture venting, and air admittance valves. Plan-review questions may require identifying whether a venting layout is acceptable or whether the submitted information is incomplete.

Storm drainage study should include roof drainage, secondary drainage, leaders, conductors, horizontal storm drains, rainfall rates, sizing tables, and overflow provisions. Candidates should practice working with roof area and sizing requirements so they are comfortable using the storm drainage chapter under timed conditions.

Accessibility preparation should include studying ICC A117.1-2017 requirements for plumbing fixtures and accessible facilities. Important areas include clear floor space, turning space, lavatory heights, knee and toe clearance, toilet compartments, grab bar coordination, drinking fountain requirements, sinks, and reach ranges. Plumbing plans examiner candidates should understand how accessibility rules can affect fixture placement and restroom layouts.

Fuel gas preparation should include reviewing fuel gas piping, pipe sizing concepts, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, and general installation requirements. Even when fuel gas is not the main subject of a plumbing plan review question, candidates should know where to find related provisions in the International Fuel Gas Code.

Definitions should be reviewed regularly. Code questions often turn on the exact meaning of a term. When a question includes a specific phrase related to fixtures, drainage systems, vents, traps, indirect waste, potable water, fuel gas, accessibility, or building elements, the definitions may help guide the correct answer.

Timed practice is one of the most useful study habits. Since the P3 exam has 60 questions and a 2 1/2-hour time limit, candidates should develop a pacing strategy. Practice moving quickly through questions while still reading carefully. If a question requires a longer calculation, mark it and return after answering faster lookup questions.
