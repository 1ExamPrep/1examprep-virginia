Exam Details

The ICC Plumbing Plans Examiner P3 exam is a multiple-choice certification exam focused on plumbing plan review. The exam is commonly administered with 60 questions and a 2.5-hour time limit. Candidates should be prepared to use the approved references to evaluate plumbing construction documents, identify code requirements, perform basic plan-review analysis, and apply the correct code provisions to the question being asked.

The P3 exam focuses on plan review duties related to plumbing systems. Candidates may need to determine whether submitted plans include enough information for review, whether plumbing fixtures are properly provided, whether drainage and vent systems comply with the code, whether water supply systems are properly shown, whether storm drainage provisions apply, and whether special systems require additional review. Because this is a plans examiner exam, students should prepare to think in terms of drawings, schedules, riser diagrams, fixture counts, pipe sizing, design conditions, and plan approval issues.

Common study areas for the ICC P3 exam include General Requirements, Fixtures, Water Heaters, Water Supply and Distribution, Sanitary Drainage, Vents, Traps, Interceptors, Separators, Special Piping and Storage Systems, Storm Drainage, Health Care Plumbing, Fuel Gas provisions when applicable, and accessibility requirements related to plumbing fixtures. These areas reflect the technical knowledge a plumbing plans examiner needs when reviewing proposed plumbing work for code compliance.

General Requirements include administrative provisions, permits, construction documents, required information on plans, inspection and approval concepts, testing requirements, materials, installation requirements, protection of piping, and general code application. Students should understand that plan review begins with determining whether the documents are complete enough to show compliance with the code.

Fixtures and accessibility requirements are also important. Plumbing plans examiners may review fixture counts, fixture locations, toilet facilities, drinking fountains, lavatories, service sinks, showers, bathtubs, clearances, accessible plumbing elements, and related fixture requirements. The ICC A117.1-2017 Standard for Accessible and Usable Buildings and Facilities is included for accessibility-related plan review topics. Students should be prepared to identify when accessibility requirements affect plumbing fixtures and how accessible fixture layouts are reviewed.

Water Supply and Distribution includes water service pipe, water distribution pipe, approved materials, pipe sizing concepts, valves, backflow prevention, hot water distribution, fixture supply requirements, pressure concerns, and protection of potable water systems. Plan review questions may describe a proposed system and ask whether the plans show proper sizing, protection, materials, or required components.

Sanitary Drainage and Vents are central to plumbing plan review. These topics include drainage fixture units, horizontal drainage pipe, building drains, building sewers, slope, fittings, cleanouts, traps, trap arms, vent sizing, vent terminals, individual vents, common vents, wet vents, circuit vents, and other venting methods. Students should be ready to work through questions that require careful use of tables, definitions, and system arrangement rules.

Storm Drainage, Special Piping, Storage Systems, Interceptors, Separators, and Health Care Plumbing may also appear in plan review preparation. These subjects require students to recognize when specialized code provisions apply and how those provisions affect submitted documents. Plans examiner questions often test whether a candidate can identify missing information, improper design assumptions, or code sections that control a specific plumbing condition.

Open Book Test

The ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam is an open-book exam. Students should still prepare carefully because the time limit requires quick code navigation, strong familiarity with the references, and careful reading. Open book does not mean the answers will be easy to find without study. Candidates must know how the 2021 International Plumbing Code, the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, and ICC A117.1-2017 are organized.

Open-book preparation should focus on finding information quickly and applying it correctly. Students should become familiar with definitions, chapter organization, fixture tables, drainage tables, venting provisions, water supply requirements, storm drainage provisions, accessibility requirements, and fuel gas-related provisions that may apply to plumbing plan review. A student who knows where key subjects are located will be able to move through questions more efficiently.

Because this is a plans examiner exam, students should practice reading each question as a plan review scenario. The question may describe what is shown on submitted drawings, what is missing from a fixture schedule, how a drainage or vent system is arranged, or whether a proposed design meets the code. The correct answer should be based on the applicable reference, not on assumptions or field habits.

FAQ Section

What exam does this course prepare me for?

This course prepares students for the ICC Plumbing Plans Examiner P3 exam using the ICC A117.1-2017 Standard for Accessible and Usable Buildings and Facilities, the 2021 International Plumbing Code, and the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code.

Is the ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam open book?

Yes. The ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam is an open-book exam. Students should prepare by learning how to navigate the approved references quickly and accurately.

How many questions are on the ICC P3 exam?

The ICC Plumbing Plans Examiner P3 exam is commonly administered with 60 multiple-choice questions and a 2.5-hour time limit.

What books are used for this course?

This course uses ICC A117.1-2017, the 2021 International Plumbing Code, and the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code. Students should use the references that match the exam they are registered to take.

What is the difference between the P2 and P3 exams?

The ICC P2 exam is the Commercial Plumbing Inspector exam and focuses on inspection of installed plumbing work. The ICC P3 exam is the Plumbing Plans Examiner exam and focuses on reviewing plumbing construction documents before work is installed.

What topics should I study for the ICC P3 exam?

Students should study general requirements, fixtures, accessibility requirements, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, separators, storm drainage, special systems, health care plumbing, and applicable fuel gas provisions.

Does this course guarantee that I will pass the exam?

No. This course supports exam preparation through organized study, plumbing code review, accessibility reference practice, code navigation, and plan-review-focused preparation. Exam results depend on the student’s preparation, knowledge, study time, and performance on exam day.

How does the Plumbing Plans Examiner (P3) exam work?

The Plumbing Plans Examiner (P3) exam is a code official certification. It is taken after the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class and leads to DHCD certification.

  • Virginia code officials are certified by the Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD), not by DPOR.
  • The path is the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class, the classes for your certification, then the required exam, usually an ICC exam.
  • Building, fire and property maintenance officials apply within 12 months of hire; technical assistants have 18 months.
  • Certified officials complete 16 hours of DHCD-approved continuing education every 2 years.
