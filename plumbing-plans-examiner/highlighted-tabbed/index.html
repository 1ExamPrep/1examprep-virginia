Exam Details

The ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam is a national certification exam offered by the International Code Council. The certification is associated with plumbing plan review and is used to evaluate a candidate’s ability to review plumbing construction documents for compliance with applicable plumbing code provisions.

The ICC P3 exam is listed as a 60-question multiple-choice exam with a 2 1/2-hour time limit. Candidates should expect questions that require code lookup, code interpretation, plan-review judgment, and calculation-based application. The exam is built around the way plumbing plans examiners review submitted documents before permit approval or correction comments are issued.

Major study areas for the P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam include general regulations and administration, plumbing fixtures and fixture counts, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, special waste, indirect waste, storm drainage, and plan-review procedures. Candidates should be prepared for questions that involve both direct code provisions and table-based calculations.

Plumbing plans examiner questions may ask a candidate to evaluate whether a plan includes the required information, determine the number of fixtures required for a building, size water distribution piping, calculate drainage fixture units, evaluate sanitary drainage design, review venting methods, determine storm drainage requirements, or apply provisions related to interceptors and special waste systems. Some questions may require more than one step, especially when calculations or plan-review conditions are involved.

The 2021 International Plumbing Code is a central reference for the exam. Candidates should study the layout of the book carefully, including the table of contents, definitions, fixture requirements, water supply provisions, sanitary drainage chapters, venting chapters, indirect waste provisions, storm drainage requirements, and related tables. The 2021 International Fuel Gas Code and ICC A117.1-2017 can also support study for fuel gas and accessibility-related topics included in this package.

A strong P3 candidate should be comfortable working with code tables. Plans examiner questions often involve fixture count tables, water supply fixture unit information, pipe sizing tables, drainage fixture unit tables, vent sizing provisions, and storm drainage data. The ability to locate and apply the correct table is one of the most important skills to build before exam day.

Test Information and Study Materials

The best preparation for the ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam begins with direct use of the code books. Candidates should study from the 2021 International Plumbing Code, the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, and ICC A117.1-2017 throughout preparation so the references become familiar working tools.

Start with the International Plumbing Code. Review the table of contents, definitions, general regulations, fixture requirements, water heater provisions, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, indirect waste, vents, traps, interceptors, special piping, and storm drainage. Pay close attention to tables, since many plan-review questions require calculations or sizing decisions.

Fixture count preparation should include practice with occupancy-based fixture requirements, fixture schedules, and minimum fixture calculations. Water supply preparation should include water supply fixture units, demand, pipe sizing, pressure considerations, backflow protection, and related distribution provisions. Sanitary drainage preparation should include drainage fixture units, pipe sizing, slope, cleanouts, building drains, branches, stacks, and backwater protection.

Venting is another important study area. Candidates should review vent sizing, vent terminals, wet venting, circuit venting, island fixture venting, air admittance valves, and trap-to-vent relationships. Plan-review questions may require a candidate to identify whether a proposed venting method is acceptable or whether additional information is needed on the submitted documents.

Storm drainage study should include roof drainage, secondary drainage, sizing, rainfall rates, leaders, conductors, and overflow requirements. Plans examiner candidates should be comfortable reviewing both the code provisions and the design information that would appear on plans.

Accessibility preparation should include fixture clearances, mounting heights, turning space, approach requirements, drinking fountains, toilet compartments, lavatories, sinks, and related accessibility provisions. ICC A117.1-2017 can be especially useful when a plumbing plan review question involves the layout or usability of accessible plumbing fixtures.

Use the highlighted and tabbed books actively during study. Practice locating sections by topic, using the index, checking definitions, and reading surrounding code language before choosing an answer. A highlighted and tabbed reference works best when it becomes part of daily study practice, not just something used on exam day.

How does the Plumbing Plans Examiner (P3) exam work?

The Plumbing Plans Examiner (P3) exam is a code official certification. It is taken after the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class and leads to DHCD certification.

  • Virginia code officials are certified by the Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD), not by DPOR.
  • The path is the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class, the classes for your certification, then the required exam, usually an ICC exam.
  • Building, fire and property maintenance officials apply within 12 months of hire; technical assistants have 18 months.
  • Certified officials complete 16 hours of DHCD-approved continuing education every 2 years.
