### Exam Details

The Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter examination is administered by PSI for the Virginia Board for Contractors. Candidates must receive examination approval before scheduling the test.

The current Journeyman/Master Natural Gasfitter examination portion contains **50 items**. A Journeyman candidate must answer **35 questions correctly** to meet the Journeyman passing requirement. Candidates receive **150 minutes** to complete the examination.

The examination content includes the following areas:

- **Standards of Practice:** 3 items.
- **Standards of Conduct:** 2 items.
- **Renewal and Reinstatement:** 2 items.
- **Definitions:** 1 item.
- **Qualification for Licensure:** 1 item.
- **Revocation of Licensure:** 1 item.
- **Natural and LP Gas Piping:** 18 items.
- **Pipe Sizing:** 14 items.
- **Gas Piping Controls:** 5 items.
- **Testing:** 3 items.

Natural and LP Gas Piping and Pipe Sizing together represent 32 of the 50 examination items. Candidates should therefore give these areas substantial attention during preparation. Understanding how piping systems are installed is important, but candidates should also become comfortable using the International Fuel Gas Code to locate technical requirements and sizing information.

Gas Piping Controls and Testing add another important technical component. Preparation should include regulators, shutoff valves, pressure-related devices, controls, and procedures used to test gas piping systems.

The examination also includes Virginia regulatory questions. Standards of practice, professional conduct, license qualifications, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, and disciplinary provisions should remain part of the study schedule throughout preparation.

### Open Book Test

The Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter examination is an **open book test**. Candidates may bring qualifying reference materials into the examination in accordance with current PSI requirements.

Qualifying references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs may be used. Candidates should ensure all examination references comply with the current PSI rules regarding markings, notes, binding, and tabs.

The open-book format makes familiarity with the references an important preparation skill. Candidates receive 150 minutes to complete 50 items, giving an average of three minutes per question. A candidate who spends several minutes searching through unrelated chapters can quickly lose valuable testing time.

During preparation, practice recognizing which reference applies to each subject. Virginia licensing, professional conduct, qualifications, and renewal questions generally point toward the Individual License and Certification Regulations. Fuel-gas piping, sizing, system installation, controls, and testing questions may require the International Fuel Gas Code. LP-gas-related requirements may involve NFPA 58.

Live instruction can help explain technical subjects, while self study provides the repetition needed to become comfortable navigating the books. When working through a practice question, identify the topic first, choose the most appropriate reference, locate the relevant section, and then confirm the answer.

### Test Information and Study Materials

A strong Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter preparation plan should reflect the actual emphasis of the examination. Natural and LP Gas Piping and Pipe Sizing account for most of the technical questions, making these areas especially important.

**Natural and LP Gas Piping** study should include piping materials, fittings, joining methods, supports, protection, regulators, valves, appliance connections, system configuration, and installation requirements. Candidates should work directly with the International Fuel Gas Code while reviewing these topics so they become familiar with both the trade concept and the applicable code section.

**Pipe Sizing** requires dedicated practice. Candidates should learn how to identify the information provided in a sizing problem, select the appropriate sizing method and table, and accurately interpret the values. Because sizing represents 14 exam items, repeated practice can be valuable.

Instead of memorizing individual sizing answers, develop a repeatable process. Identify the gas demand, system conditions, pressure, and piping length information presented in the problem. Then select the applicable table and determine the appropriate pipe size.

**Gas Piping Controls** preparation should include valves, regulators, shutoff devices, pressure controls, and system safety components. Understand the purpose of each component and the requirements governing installation and use.

**Testing** should include pressure tests, system verification procedures, and requirements that apply before a gas piping system is placed into operation. Candidates should carefully read the technical conditions presented in questions because small differences can affect which requirement applies.

The **Virginia regulatory portion** should be studied consistently. Review standards of practice, professional conduct, license qualifications, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, and disciplinary provisions using the Individual License and Certification Regulations.

NFPA 58 should be used during study of LP-gas concepts. Become familiar with how the reference is organized and where requirements involving containers, piping, equipment, installation, and protection are located.

The self study portion of the training allows candidates to reinforce what is covered during live sessions. After a class topic is reviewed, candidates can return to the reference books, locate the applicable material, work through additional questions, and spend more time on areas that remain difficult.

Timed practice can also help candidates develop better pacing. Work through groups of questions while monitoring how long it takes to answer technical questions, perform reference lookups, and complete sizing problems.

When an answer is missed, locate the supporting provision and read the surrounding code section. Understanding why an answer is correct can provide more useful preparation than simply memorizing a practice answer.

## How does the Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

The Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
