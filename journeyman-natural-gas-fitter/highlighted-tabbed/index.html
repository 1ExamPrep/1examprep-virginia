Exam Details

The Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter examination is administered by PSI for the Virginia Board for Contractors. Candidates must receive examination approval from the Board before scheduling their test.

The current Journeyman/Master Natural Gasfitter examination portion contains 50 items. Journeyman candidates must answer 35 questions correctly to meet the passing requirement. Candidates receive 150 minutes to complete the examination.

The examination includes both Virginia regulatory material and technical Natural Gasfitter subjects. The current content outline includes:

  • Standards of Practice: 3 items.
  • Standards of Conduct: 2 items.
  • Renewal and Reinstatement: 2 items.
  • Definitions: 1 item.
  • Qualification for Licensure: 1 item.
  • Revocation of Licensure: 1 item.
  • Natural and LP Gas Piping: 18 items.
  • Pipe Sizing: 14 items.
  • Gas Piping Controls: 5 items.
  • Testing: 3 items.

Natural and LP gas piping and pipe sizing represent the largest part of the exam. Together, those subjects account for 32 of the 50 examination items. Candidates should therefore spend substantial preparation time reviewing piping materials, installation requirements, sizing methods, code tables, system design considerations, regulators, shutoff devices, appliance connections, pressure-related requirements, and other fuel-gas system provisions.

Gas piping controls and testing also deserve focused review. Candidates should understand the purpose and application of controls, valves, regulators, and related system components, along with requirements involving pressure testing and system verification.

The Virginia regulatory portion is smaller but still important. Standards of practice, professional conduct, renewal and reinstatement, licensing qualifications, definitions, and disciplinary requirements all appear on the published examination outline.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter examination is an open book test. Candidates may bring qualifying reference materials into the examination subject to PSI rules.

Reference books may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination when prepared in accordance with current testing requirements. Permanent tabs may be used for indexing. Removable tabs and handwritten notes are not permitted in exam references.

Because the examination is open book, knowing how to use the references efficiently can be an important advantage during preparation. Candidates receive 150 minutes for 50 questions, giving an average of three minutes per item. Spending too much time searching through an unfamiliar code book can reduce the time available for technical questions and pipe-sizing problems.

The highlighted and tabbed format of this package is designed to make study navigation more organized. Candidates should use the prepared books repeatedly before exam day so they become familiar with what each tab represents and where important code subjects are located.

Virginia licensing and regulatory questions generally direct candidates toward the Individual License and Certification Regulations. Questions involving piping, sizing, system installation, controls, appliance requirements, testing, combustion air, or related fuel-gas topics may require the International Fuel Gas Code. LP-gas-specific provisions can involve NFPA 58.

A productive study strategy is to identify the topic first, select the correct reference second, and then use the index, tabs, chapter headings, and tables to find the applicable information. Repeating this process during study can help develop stronger open-book navigation habits.

Licensing Steps

Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter licensing is an individual tradesman licensing process. Applicants must meet the applicable education and experience requirements before receiving approval to take the examination.

  1. Select the Journeyman Natural Gasfitter credential. Confirm that the Natural Gas Fitter Provider designation matches the individual trade license you intend to pursue.
  2. Review Virginia qualification requirements. Applicants must satisfy one of the Board-approved combinations of practical experience, vocational training, education, apprenticeship, or other qualifying credentials.
  3. Document practical gasfitting experience. Applicants must provide the experience verification required for the qualification pathway being used.
  4. Document training or education when applicable. Candidates using formal vocational training, technical education, or another educational qualification must provide supporting records.
  5. Submit the applicable Virginia tradesman application. The Virginia Board for Contractors reviews the candidate’s eligibility.
  6. Receive examination approval. Board approval is required before the candidate may schedule the examination.
  7. Prepare for the exam. Study Virginia regulations, natural and LP gas piping, pipe sizing, controls, testing, and the applicable technical references.
  8. Schedule the examination through PSI. After Board approval, candidates follow PSI procedures for scheduling the test.
  9. Complete the Journeyman Natural Gasfitter examination. Candidates must meet the Journeyman passing requirement.
  10. Complete the remaining licensing process. The Virginia Board for Contractors handles the individual license after the applicable requirements have been satisfied.

State Requirements

Virginia identifies Natural Gas Fitter Provider as an individual tradesman license. Candidates must be at least 18 years old and provide the documentation required to support identification, training, and practical experience.

Current Virginia regulations provide specific qualification pathways for Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter Provider applicants. One pathway requires 80 hours of formal vocational training and four years of practical experience in the trade.

Another pathway allows an applicant to qualify with 40 hours of formal vocational training and five years of practical experience in the trade.

Applicants must provide documentation supporting the pathway used for examination approval. The Virginia Board for Contractors reviews the required experience and training information before authorizing the candidate to take the examination.

Virginia also recognizes certain other licensing and apprenticeship circumstances under its tradesman regulations. Candidates should submit the documentation that accurately reflects their own training, experience, apprenticeship, and licensing history.

Continuing education is required after initial licensure. Virginia requires one hour of Board-approved continuing education for gas fitting, natural gas, or liquefied petroleum gas trades during the applicable renewal period. The continuing education course must be approved by the Board for Contractors to count toward renewal requirements.

Exam preparation and licensing eligibility are separate parts of the process. Owning or studying from the exam books does not replace Virginia’s experience, training, application, examination, or continuing education requirements.

Test Information and Study Materials

A strong Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter study plan should focus heavily on piping and pipe sizing while still covering controls, testing, and Virginia regulations. The published exam outline makes it clear that piping and sizing are central to the technical portion.

Begin with Natural and LP Gas Piping. Review piping materials, installation methods, joints, supports, protection, regulators, shutoff valves, appliance connections, piping configurations, and related fuel-gas system requirements. Use the International Fuel Gas Code during study so technical concepts are connected directly to the source material.

Pipe Sizing should receive dedicated practice. With 14 examination items in this category, candidates should become comfortable identifying the information provided in a sizing problem, selecting the correct table, following the applicable sizing method, and accurately interpreting the results.

Practice locating sizing tables using the highlighted and tabbed books. Familiarity with the table locations and organization of the code can help reduce search time. Candidates should avoid relying only on memorized examples because an exam question can present different pipe lengths, loads, pressures, or system configurations.

Gas Piping Controls should include review of shutoff devices, regulators, controls, valves, pressure-related components, and system safety features. Candidates should understand the purpose of these components and the requirements governing their installation.

Testing preparation should cover pressure testing, system verification, and applicable procedures for confirming that gas piping is ready for operation. Testing questions may depend on specific conditions or code requirements, making careful reading important.

Regulatory study should include the Virginia Board for Contractors Individual License and Certification Regulations. Candidates should review professional standards, conduct, renewal and reinstatement, licensing qualifications, definitions, and disciplinary provisions throughout the preparation period rather than leaving them until the final days before the exam.

NFPA 58 should also be incorporated into study sessions involving LP-gas concepts. Become familiar with its organization and terminology, including sections involving containers, piping, equipment, installation, and protection. The exam outline includes Natural and LP Gas Piping, so candidates should be prepared to distinguish between general fuel-gas provisions and LP-gas-specific requirements.

The highlighted and tabbed preparation can support faster navigation, but candidates should practice with the books repeatedly. Tabs are most useful when the candidate understands what each tab represents and can quickly connect an exam topic with the appropriate section.

Timed study can help develop better pacing. Work through practice-oriented questions with the references available and monitor how long it takes to locate supporting information. If a simple code lookup regularly takes several minutes, spend additional time learning that section of the book.

When reviewing incorrect answers, locate the supporting code provision rather than simply memorizing the correct choice. Reading the surrounding section can help candidates understand the underlying requirement and prepare for questions that test the same concept using different wording.

How does the Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

The Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.
