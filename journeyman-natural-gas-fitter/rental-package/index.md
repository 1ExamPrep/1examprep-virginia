A repeatable approach can be useful. Identify gas demand, piping length, pressure conditions, and other relevant information. Then select the applicable table and determine the pipe size that satisfies the conditions presented.

**Gas Piping Controls** preparation should address regulators, valves, shutoff devices, pressure controls, and related safety components. Candidates should understand both component function and the applicable code requirements governing installation.

The Virginia regulatory portion should remain part of the study routine. Review standards of practice, standards of conduct, qualification requirements, definitions, renewal and reinstatement, and disciplinary provisions throughout preparation.

NFPA 58 should be incorporated into review of LP-gas subjects. Become familiar with its organization and the sections addressing containers, piping, equipment, storage, installation, and protection.

## How does the Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

The Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
