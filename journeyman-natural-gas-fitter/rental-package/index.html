Exam Details

The Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter examination is administered by PSI for the Virginia Board for Contractors. Candidates must receive approval from the Board before scheduling the examination.

The current Journeyman/Master Natural Gasfitter examination portion contains 50 items. Journeyman candidates must answer 35 questions correctly to meet the passing requirement. Candidates receive 150 minutes to complete the examination.

The current examination outline includes:

  • Standards of Practice: 3 items.
  • Standards of Conduct: 2 items.
  • Renewal and Reinstatement: 2 items.
  • Definitions: 1 item.
  • Qualification for Licensure: 1 item.
  • Revocation of Licensure: 1 item.
  • Natural and LP Gas Piping: 18 items.
  • Pipe Sizing: 14 items.
  • Gas Piping Controls: 5 items.
  • Testing: 3 items.

Natural and LP Gas Piping and Pipe Sizing make up most of the technical examination. Candidates should give these subjects substantial attention while also reviewing controls, testing procedures, and Virginia regulatory requirements.

Technical preparation can include piping materials, installation requirements, valves, regulators, appliance connections, system configuration, sizing tables, pressure considerations, and system testing. Candidates should understand the underlying trade concepts while becoming familiar with where corresponding requirements are located in the reference materials.

Licensing Steps

Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter licensing is an individual tradesman licensing process. Candidates must satisfy the applicable qualification requirements before receiving authorization to take the examination.

  1. Confirm the appropriate credential. Determine that the Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter Provider license matches the trade and licensing level you intend to pursue.
  2. Review Virginia eligibility requirements. Applicants must satisfy an applicable qualification pathway recognized by the Virginia Board for Contractors.
  3. Document practical experience. Provide the required verification of qualifying natural gasfitting experience.
  4. Document training or education when applicable. Candidates using formal vocational training or another recognized educational pathway must provide appropriate supporting records.
  5. Submit the applicable tradesman application. The Virginia Board for Contractors reviews the applicant’s eligibility.
  6. Receive examination approval. Board pre-approval is required before candidates may schedule the exam.
  7. Prepare for the examination. Review Virginia regulations, natural and LP gas piping, pipe sizing, controls, testing, and reference navigation.
  8. Schedule the examination through PSI. Candidates may schedule after receiving the required approval.
  9. Complete the Journeyman Natural Gasfitter examination. The applicable passing requirement must be satisfied.
  10. Complete the remaining licensing process. License issuance is handled through the Virginia Board for Contractors after applicable requirements are completed.

State Requirements

Virginia issues tradesman licenses to individuals rather than companies. Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter Provider applicants must satisfy the Board’s applicable entry requirements before receiving examination approval and licensure.

Virginia uses documented training, education, apprenticeship, practical experience, and other recognized qualification methods when determining eligibility. Candidates should use the pathway that accurately reflects their background and provide the required supporting documentation with their application.

Pre-approval from the Virginia Board for Contractors is required before sitting for the examination. After approval, candidates work with PSI to schedule the applicable exam.

The individual tradesman license is separate from a Virginia contractor business license. Virginia’s application materials specifically distinguish individual tradesman licensing from contractor business licensing. Individuals performing work that requires contractor business licensing must comply with the separate contractor requirements that apply to that work.

After initial licensure, Virginia Natural Gasfitter licensees are also subject to renewal and continuing education requirements. For gas fitting, natural gas, and liquefied petroleum gas trades, Virginia currently requires one hour of Board-approved continuing education during the applicable renewal period.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter preparation plan should reflect the weight of the examination. Natural and LP Gas Piping and Pipe Sizing together represent the majority of the published exam content, making these subjects especially important.

For Natural and LP Gas Piping, review piping materials, fittings, joining methods, supports, protection, regulators, shutoff valves, appliance connections, system configuration, and installation requirements. Work directly with the International Fuel Gas Code during study so technical concepts become connected with the applicable source material.

Pipe Sizing should receive dedicated practice. Candidates should learn how to evaluate the information presented in a sizing problem, identify the correct sizing method, locate the applicable table, and interpret table values accurately.

A repeatable approach can be useful. Identify gas demand, piping length, pressure conditions, and other relevant information. Then select the applicable table and determine the pipe size that satisfies the conditions presented.

Gas Piping Controls preparation should address regulators, valves, shutoff devices, pressure controls, and related safety components. Candidates should understand both component function and the applicable code requirements governing installation.

Testing should include pressure testing, verification procedures, and requirements that apply before systems are placed into service. Careful reading is important because test questions may depend on specific system conditions or code language.

The Virginia regulatory portion should remain part of the study routine. Review standards of practice, standards of conduct, qualification requirements, definitions, renewal and reinstatement, and disciplinary provisions throughout preparation.

NFPA 58 should be incorporated into review of LP-gas subjects. Become familiar with its organization and the sections addressing containers, piping, equipment, storage, installation, and protection.

The additional NASCLA and Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code references can support broader contractor and Virginia code familiarity. Candidates should distinguish these additional package materials from the references directly associated with specific Natural Gasfitter exam topics.

Timed practice can help candidates develop better pacing. Work through questions while using the books and monitor how long it takes to locate technical requirements or sizing information. Spend additional preparation time on reference sections that remain difficult to navigate.

How does the Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
