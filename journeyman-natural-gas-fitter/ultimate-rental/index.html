Exam Details

The Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter examination is administered by PSI for the Virginia Board for Contractors. Candidates must obtain approval through the Board before scheduling the examination.

The current Journeyman/Master Natural Gasfitter examination portion contains 50 items. A Journeyman candidate must answer 35 questions correctly to meet the Journeyman passing requirement. Candidates receive 150 minutes to complete the examination.

The current content outline includes both Virginia regulatory material and technical natural gasfitting subjects:

  • Standards of Practice: 3 items.
  • Standards of Conduct: 2 items.
  • Renewal and Reinstatement: 2 items.
  • Definitions: 1 item.
  • Qualification for Licensure: 1 item.
  • Revocation of Licensure: 1 item.
  • Natural and LP Gas Piping: 18 items.
  • Pipe Sizing: 14 items.
  • Gas Piping Controls: 5 items.
  • Testing: 3 items.

Natural and LP Gas Piping and Pipe Sizing together account for 32 of the 50 examination items. Candidates should therefore devote substantial preparation time to piping materials, joining methods, installation requirements, regulators, valves, appliance connections, system configurations, sizing methods, and the technical tables used to determine proper pipe sizes.

Gas Piping Controls and Testing are additional technical areas. Candidates should understand the purpose and installation of regulators, shutoff devices, valves, pressure-related components, and control equipment as well as the procedures used to test and verify gas piping systems.

Virginia regulatory questions make up another portion of the examination. Standards of practice, professional conduct, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, qualification requirements, and disciplinary provisions should remain part of the preparation schedule throughout the study period.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter examination is an open book test. Candidates may bring qualifying reference materials into the examination subject to PSI rules governing permitted books and reference preparation.

PSI permits qualifying references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs may be used when they comply with testing requirements. Candidates should make sure their references follow the current rules regarding notes, tabs, markings, and binding before arriving for testing.

An open-book examination still requires preparation. Candidates receive 150 minutes for 50 items, which provides an average of three minutes per question. A candidate who spends too much time searching unfamiliar books may have less time available for detailed code questions and pipe-sizing problems.

This Ultimate package includes highlighting and tabbing on some books to support reference navigation. The prepared books should be used regularly during study so candidates become familiar with the location of major subjects and understand what each tab represents.

Questions involving Virginia licensing, professional conduct, renewal, or qualifications generally direct candidates toward the Virginia Board for Contractors Individual License and Certification Regulations. Questions involving piping systems, sizing, installation, controls, and testing may require the International Fuel Gas Code. LP-gas-specific information may require NFPA 58.

A useful open-book strategy is to identify the subject first, choose the most relevant reference, and then locate the applicable chapter, table, definition, or section. Practicing this process repeatedly can help candidates become more efficient with the references before test day.

Licensing Steps

Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter licensing is an individual tradesman licensing process. Candidates must satisfy the applicable Virginia Board for Contractors requirements before receiving examination approval and completing the licensing process.

  1. Select the appropriate credential. Confirm that the Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter Provider designation matches the work and licensing level you intend to pursue.
  2. Review Virginia eligibility requirements. Applicants must qualify under a pathway recognized by the Virginia Board for Contractors.
  3. Document practical experience. Candidates must provide the required verification of applicable natural gasfitting experience.
  4. Document qualifying training or education when applicable. Applicants using formal vocational training, education, apprenticeship, or another recognized pathway must provide supporting documentation.
  5. Submit the applicable application. The Board reviews the candidate’s qualifications before granting examination approval.
  6. Receive examination authorization. Pre-approval from the Virginia Board for Contractors is required before scheduling the exam with PSI.
  7. Prepare for the examination. Study natural and LP gas piping, pipe sizing, controls, testing, Virginia regulations, and reference navigation.
  8. Schedule the examination with PSI. After approval, candidates follow PSI registration and scheduling procedures.
  9. Complete the Journeyman Natural Gasfitter examination. Candidates must achieve the applicable Journeyman passing score.
  10. Complete the remaining licensing requirements. The Virginia Board for Contractors handles license issuance after applicable requirements have been satisfied.

The included Application Service provides support for organizing the licensing application process. It is intended to help candidates work through the administrative side of their licensing path while they focus on exam preparation.

State Requirements

Virginia regulates Natural Gas Fitter Providers through its individual tradesman licensing program. Applicants must demonstrate that they satisfy the experience, training, education, apprenticeship, or other qualifying requirements applicable to the license they are seeking.

The Virginia Board for Contractors requires pre-approval before a candidate may take the examination. The applicant must submit the required licensing materials and provide documentation supporting the qualification pathway being used.

Qualification pathways can involve combinations of practical trade experience and formal vocational training as well as other circumstances recognized under the Board’s regulations. Candidates should use the pathway that accurately reflects their own professional background and submit the corresponding documentation.

Individual Natural Gasfitter licensing is distinct from contractor business licensing. An individual tradesman credential establishes the person’s trade qualification, while a contractor business may be subject to separate licensing requirements depending on the contracting work being performed.

After initial licensure, Natural Gasfitter licensees must also comply with applicable renewal requirements. Virginia currently requires one hour of Board-approved continuing education for gas fitting, natural gas, and liquefied petroleum gas trades during the applicable renewal period.

Continuing education must be completed through an approved provider to satisfy the Board’s renewal requirement. Initial exam preparation, continuing education, and contractor business licensing are separate parts of Virginia’s regulatory framework.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Virginia Journeyman Natural Gasfitter study plan should closely reflect the examination content. Natural and LP Gas Piping and Pipe Sizing represent most of the test, so these subjects deserve substantial attention while candidates continue reviewing controls, testing, and Virginia regulations.

For Natural and LP Gas Piping, review piping materials, fittings, joining methods, installation practices, supports, protection, regulators, valves, appliance connections, and system configurations. Candidates should work with the International Fuel Gas Code during technical review so the information becomes connected with the actual reference used in open-book preparation.

Pipe Sizing requires repeated practice. Candidates should learn how to identify the conditions in a problem, determine the appropriate sizing method, locate the correct table, and accurately interpret the result. Pipe sizing should be practiced as a process rather than treated as a collection of memorized answers.

Begin by identifying the system information presented. Determine gas demand, piping length, pressure conditions, and any other factors relevant to the problem. Then select the applicable table and use the information to determine the proper pipe size.

Gas Piping Controls preparation should include regulators, valves, shutoff devices, control components, pressure-related equipment, and safety devices. Candidates should understand what these components do as well as the installation rules that apply to them.

Testing preparation should include gas-piping pressure tests, verification procedures, and requirements that apply before a system is placed into service. Questions in this area may depend on specific system conditions, making careful reading and accurate code navigation important.

The Virginia regulatory section should be studied throughout the preparation period. Review standards of practice, standards of conduct, qualification requirements, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, and disciplinary provisions rather than waiting until the final study session.

NFPA 58 should be incorporated into study involving LP-gas concepts. Learn how the standard is organized and become familiar with provisions covering containers, piping, equipment, installation, storage, and protection.

Candidates can use the books alongside the course so technical review and reference navigation become part of the same preparation process.

Some books are highlighted and tabbed for exam preparation. These features can help candidates identify important sections, but regular practice is still essential. A tab is most useful when the candidate understands which subject it identifies and how the surrounding section is organized.

Timed practice can also improve preparation. Candidates can work through groups of questions while monitoring how long they spend on direct knowledge questions, code lookups, and pipe-sizing problems. This can reveal areas where additional reference familiarity is needed.

How does the Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter exam work?

The Journeyman Natural Gas Fitter exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
