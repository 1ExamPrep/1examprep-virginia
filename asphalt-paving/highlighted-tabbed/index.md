### State Requirements

Virginia identifies **Asphalt Paving and Sealcoating Contracting** with the specialty abbreviation **PAV**. The specialty applies to services involving asphalt paving and/or sealcoating on several common types of surfaces and facilities.

The defined scope includes work on **subdivision streets and adjacent intersections, driveways, parking lots, tennis courts, running tracks, and play areas**, using materials and accessories common to asphalt paving and sealcoating operations.

The specialty also includes the **height adjustment of existing sewer manholes, storm drains, water valves, sewer cleanouts, and drain grates** when associated with the work.

Necessary **excavation and grading** are included within the PAV specialty scope. This helps explain why excavation, compaction, and grading receive significant attention on the technical examination.

Virginia also identifies the Highway/Heavy classification as providing for this type of work. Contractors should operate only within the classifications and specialties authorized by their Virginia contractor license.

The technical PAV examination is only one component of contractor licensing. The contractor business must satisfy the applicable requirements established by the Virginia Board for Contractors for the license and specialty being pursued.
