Exam Details

The ICC R3 Residential Plans Examiner exam is a certification examination offered by the International Code Council. The exam title is Residential Plans Examiner, and the exam code is R3. The exam is designed for individuals who review residential construction documents for compliance with the International Residential Code.

The ICC R3 exam contains 60 multiple-choice questions. Candidates are given 2 hours to complete the exam. The exam is code based and requires candidates to locate and apply IRC requirements to residential plan review situations. Candidates should prepare to work efficiently because the time limit requires steady pacing and strong reference navigation.

The R3 exam covers plan review subjects connected to residential construction. Major content areas include administrative provisions, site requirements, architectural and life safety, structural requirements, energy efficiency, mechanical systems, plumbing systems, and electrical systems. These areas reflect the broad knowledge expected of a residential plans examiner reviewing construction documents for one- and two-family dwellings.

Administrative questions may involve permit documents, construction document review, code compliance responsibilities, and general provisions that affect the plan review process. Site-related questions may involve drainage, address identification, flood hazard considerations, or other site conditions covered by the residential code. Architectural and life safety questions may involve room sizes, ceiling heights, emergency escape and rescue openings, smoke alarms, carbon monoxide alarms, exits, stairways, guards, glazing, fire separation, and similar residential safety provisions.

Structural questions are an important part of R3 preparation. Candidates should study foundations, footings, floors, wall construction, braced wall lines, roof framing, headers, beams, spans, fastening, lateral support, and the use of IRC tables. A plans examiner must be able to recognize when residential plans show code-compliant construction and when more information is needed.

The R3 exam also includes energy, mechanical, plumbing, fuel gas, and electrical provisions. Candidates should understand how these systems are presented on residential plans and how to locate the applicable IRC requirements. A residential plans examiner may need to review insulation, fenestration, mechanical equipment, ducts, combustion air, fuel gas piping, plumbing fixtures, water distribution, drainage, vents, service equipment, branch circuits, receptacle locations, lighting outlets, GFCI protection, AFCI protection, and related system requirements.

Open Book Test

The ICC R3 Residential Plans Examiner exam is an open book test. The approved reference for this package is the International Residential Code, 2021. Candidates should prepare by studying directly from the IRC and learning how to locate code sections quickly during the exam.

Open book does not mean the exam is simple. The R3 exam requires candidates to interpret questions, identify the plan review issue, select the correct area of the code, and apply the code language accurately. Candidates who rely only on general construction experience may struggle if they are not comfortable navigating the IRC under time pressure.

During preparation, candidates should become familiar with the structure of the IRC. The table of contents, chapter layout, definitions, illustrations, tables, exceptions, and index are all useful exam tools. Candidates should practice moving between the building chapters and the energy, mechanical, fuel gas, plumbing, and electrical chapters so they are not slowed down by the broad scope of the exam.

For exam-day book use, candidates must follow the current ICC testing rules for the selected testing method. Candidates should prepare their reference materials according to the rules that apply to their exam appointment and should avoid bringing loose notes or unapproved materials into the testing environment.

Test Information and Study Materials

The R3 Residential Plans Examiner exam requires candidates to think like a plan reviewer. A strong study plan should include both code reading and plan-based application. Candidates should understand what information appears on residential drawings and how that information connects to IRC requirements.

Start by reviewing the structure of the 2021 International Residential Code. Learn where the major subjects are located and how the chapters are organized. The building chapters are especially important because they cover many of the items seen on residential plans, including room planning, light and ventilation, means of egress, smoke alarms, carbon monoxide alarms, stairways, guards, foundations, floors, walls, and roofs.

Structural preparation should receive serious attention. Candidates should practice using span tables, footing tables, fastening tables, bracing provisions, foundation requirements, header requirements, and roof framing provisions. Plan review questions may require the candidate to compare plan information to a table or determine whether a proposed detail satisfies the code.

Energy efficiency review is also important. Candidates should understand how insulation, windows, doors, air sealing, duct insulation, equipment, and energy compliance information may appear on residential plans. The plans examiner must know when the drawings provide enough information and when corrections or additional details are needed.

Mechanical, fuel gas, plumbing, and electrical review topics should not be ignored. These chapters may not feel as familiar to candidates with a building-only background, but they are part of the R3 exam. Candidates should review mechanical ventilation, exhaust, ducts, combustion air, appliances, gas piping, water heaters, plumbing fixtures, water supply, drainage, vents, electrical services, receptacles, lighting outlets, GFCI protection, AFCI protection, and equipment clearances.

During study, candidates should practice locating answers rather than only reading chapters. Exam-style questions often require quick movement from the question topic to the correct section. When reviewing a missed question, the best approach is to find the code section, read the surrounding language, and understand why the answer is correct. This builds real exam skill and improves long-term code understanding.

Timed practice is also important. With 60 questions in 2 hours, candidates have about 2 minutes per question. Some questions can be answered quickly, while others require careful reference lookup. A practical exam strategy is to answer familiar questions first, mark difficult questions, and return to them after completing the easier items.

How does the Residential Plans Examiner (R3) exam work?

The Residential Plans Examiner (R3) exam is a code official certification. It is taken after the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class and leads to DHCD certification.

  • Virginia code officials are certified by the Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD), not by DPOR.
  • The path is the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class, the classes for your certification, then the required exam, usually an ICC exam.
  • Building, fire and property maintenance officials apply within 12 months of hire; technical assistants have 18 months.
  • Certified officials complete 16 hours of DHCD-approved continuing education every 2 years.
