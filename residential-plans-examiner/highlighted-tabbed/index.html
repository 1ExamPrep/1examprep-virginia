Exam Details

The ICC R3 Residential Plans Examiner exam is a national certification exam offered by the International Code Council. It is associated with residential plan review and evaluates a candidate’s ability to review residential construction documents for compliance with the applicable provisions of the International Residential Code.

The ICC R3 exam is listed as a 60-question multiple-choice exam with a 2-hour time limit. Candidates should expect questions that require code lookup, interpretation, plan-review judgment, and practical application of residential code provisions. The 2021 version of the exam uses the 2021 International Residential Code as the primary reference.

Major study areas for the R3 Residential Plans Examiner exam include residential plan review procedures, administrative provisions, building planning, fire safety, means of egress, light and ventilation, room dimensions, emergency escape and rescue openings, stairs, guards, smoke alarms, carbon monoxide alarms, foundations, wall construction, floor construction, roof-ceiling construction, roof assemblies, energy efficiency, and basic mechanical, plumbing, fuel gas, and electrical provisions contained in the IRC.

Residential plan review questions may require candidates to determine whether a submitted plan includes enough information, whether a foundation detail complies, whether joist or rafter spans are acceptable, whether a stairway is properly shown, whether emergency escape and rescue openings meet requirements, whether wall bracing information is adequate, or whether energy-related details are properly addressed. The exam focuses on applying code requirements to documents before construction begins.

The International Residential Code is a broad reference. It contains building, energy, mechanical, fuel gas, plumbing, and electrical provisions for one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses within the scope of the code. Candidates preparing for the R3 exam should give special attention to the building portions of the IRC while still being familiar with the organization of the full book.

For many candidates, the most challenging part of the R3 exam is not one individual topic, but the timed open book format. Sixty questions in 2 hours requires steady pacing. Candidates should practice locating answers quickly, reading questions carefully, and avoiding long searches in the wrong chapter. The highlighted and tabbed IRC is intended to support that kind of exam preparation.

Open Book Test

The ICC R3 Residential Plans Examiner exam is an open book test. Candidates preparing for the 2021 version should study with the 2021 International Residential Code. Open book testing does not mean the exam is easy. It means that the ability to use the code book efficiently is one of the most important parts of preparation.

Candidates should become comfortable with the structure of the IRC before exam day. That includes the table of contents, definitions, administrative provisions, building planning chapter, foundation requirements, floor and wall construction chapters, roof-ceiling construction, energy efficiency, mechanical provisions, plumbing provisions, fuel gas provisions, electrical provisions, appendices when applicable to the exam reference, and the index.

Tabs can help create a visual roadmap through the book. When you are working on plan review questions involving building planning, egress, fire separation, foundations, framing, wall bracing, energy, or residential systems, tabs can help you move to the correct area faster. Highlighting can help reinforce key provisions as you review the code repeatedly.

For an open book plans examiner exam, candidates should practice more than simple code lookups. Plan review questions often require understanding how code provisions appear on drawings. A question may involve a detail, a dimension, a plan note, a section view, a fixture layout, a framing member, or a table. The candidate must connect the information in the question to the applicable IRC provision.

Timed practice is essential. Read the full question first, identify the subject, move to the most likely IRC chapter, confirm the requirement from the code text, and then choose the best answer. If a question requires a longer table lookup or calculation, candidates should manage their pacing and return to harder questions after completing easier ones.

Test Information and Study Materials

The best preparation for the ICC R3 Residential Plans Examiner exam begins with direct use of the 2021 International Residential Code. Candidates should study from the same book they will use for exam preparation so the reference becomes familiar before the test.

Start by learning the structure of the IRC. Review the table of contents, definitions, chapter titles, major building sections, tables, and index. Plans examiner candidates should understand where to find general requirements, building planning provisions, foundation requirements, wall construction, floor construction, roof-ceiling construction, energy provisions, and residential system requirements.

Building planning is a major study area. Candidates should review occupancy and scope provisions, fire separation, light and ventilation, minimum room areas, ceiling heights, sanitation, toilet and bathroom requirements, glazing, safety glazing, means of egress, stairways, ramps, guards, emergency escape and rescue openings, smoke alarms, and carbon monoxide alarms.

Structural plan review preparation should include foundation design, footings, slabs, foundation walls, anchorage, wood floor framing, joist spans, girder and header requirements, wall framing, braced wall panels, roof framing, rafters, ceiling joists, trusses, roof sheathing, roof coverings, and related tables. Candidates should practice using span tables and reading plan-review questions that require table-based answers.

Energy efficiency should also be part of the study routine. Residential plans may include insulation values, fenestration information, air sealing requirements, duct insulation, equipment efficiency details, and documentation showing compliance with energy-related provisions. Candidates should become familiar with where energy requirements are located in the IRC and how they are reviewed on plans.

Although the R3 exam focuses heavily on residential plan review and building-related provisions, candidates should also understand the organization of the mechanical, plumbing, fuel gas, and electrical sections in the IRC. Plan review questions may require basic familiarity with where system requirements are located, especially when reviewing complete residential construction documents.

Definitions should be reviewed often. Many code questions turn on the exact meaning of a term. When a question uses a phrase related to dwelling units, townhouses, emergency escape and rescue openings, habitable space, story, basement, fire separation distance, braced wall line, or structural element, the definition may help guide the correct answer.

Use the highlighted and tabbed IRC actively. Practice locating sections by topic, checking definitions, reading surrounding code language, and using tables correctly. The book becomes more useful when it is part of regular study practice. The goal is to develop a smooth process for moving from a plan-review question to the correct code requirement.

Timed practice is one of the most important study habits. Since the R3 exam has 60 questions and a 2-hour time limit, candidates should develop a pacing strategy. Practice answering questions under time pressure, mark longer table-based questions when needed, and return after completing faster lookup questions. This helps build confidence and reduces exam-day stress.
