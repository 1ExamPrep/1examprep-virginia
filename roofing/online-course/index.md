### Exam Details

The Virginia Roofing Contractor examination is the Qualified Individual technical examination associated with the **ROC** specialty. Roofing is one of the Virginia contractor specialty designations requiring the Qualified Individual to receive the applicable examination approval and successfully complete the required technical exam.

The Virginia ROC examination contains **50 questions**. Candidates receive **120 minutes** to complete the examination and need **36 correct answers** to meet the current passing requirement.

The current examination outline includes the following major areas:

- **Estimating and Plan Reading:** 5 questions
- **Surface Preparation and Planning:** 5 questions
- **Steep and Low Slope Roofing, including Built-Up Roofing and Waterproofing:** 18 questions
- **Roofing Components:** 10 questions
- **Safety:** 5 questions
- **Repairs and Reroofing:** 7 questions

Steep- and low-slope roofing is the largest individual examination category. Candidates should be prepared to study roofing assemblies, materials, membranes, underlayments, flashing, drainage, insulation, attachment, waterproofing, and installation principles.

Roofing Components is another major category. Preparation can include roof decks, flashing, insulation, drainage components, fasteners, penetrations, transitions, underlayments, edges, and other elements that work together as part of a complete roofing system.

Estimating, surface preparation, repairs, reroofing, and OSHA safety also represent important portions of the examination. Candidates should prepare across the entire outline rather than concentrating only on the roof systems they encounter most frequently in the field.

Virginia uses PSI as the examination vendor for Qualified Individual contractor specialty exams. Candidates must receive the applicable Board approval before scheduling the ROC examination.

### Open Book Test

The Virginia Roofing Contractor examination is an **open-book test**. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own permitted reference materials to the testing center.

The open-book format makes efficient navigation a major part of exam preparation. With 50 questions and 120 minutes available, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds per question. Searching through multiple unfamiliar references for every answer can quickly consume valuable testing time.

A stronger strategy combines roofing knowledge with selective reference use. Candidates should identify the subject first and then choose the book most likely to contain the information. A construction safety question may lead to OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926. A building-code question may require either the International Building Code or International Residential Code, depending on the type of structure and subject being tested.

Estimating or general roofing construction questions may be supported by Roofing Construction and Estimating. Technical questions about steep-slope systems can be studied through the NRCA Roofing Manual: Steep Slope Roof Systems, while membrane and low-slope subjects can be reviewed through the NRCA Roofing Manual: Membrane Roofing Systems.

Reference navigation should be practiced repeatedly. Candidates should learn how to use indexes, chapter headings, tables of contents, code sections, roof details, tables, and diagrams. The objective is to locate specific information efficiently rather than read large sections of a book during the examination.

### Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing combines a contractor license class with one or more classifications or specialties. Roofing Contracting is identified by the specialty abbreviation **ROC**.

1. **Determine the appropriate contractor license class.** Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
2. **Select Roofing Contracting.** The applicable specialty designation is ROC.
3. **Identify the Qualified Individual.** The contractor business must designate a Qualified Individual for Roofing Contracting.
4. **Document applicable roofing experience.** The Qualified Individual must satisfy the experience requirement associated with the contractor class and specialty.
5. **Complete the applicable experience verification.** Roofing is among the specialties requiring pre-approval for the technical examination.
6. **Receive approval for the ROC examination.** Candidates must be approved before scheduling the specialty test.
7. **Prepare for the technical examination.** Study roofing systems, codes, safety, estimating, waterproofing, reroofing, and reference navigation.
8. **Successfully complete the ROC examination.** The Qualified Individual must satisfy the applicable technical exam requirement.
9. **Complete pre-license education.** New Virginia contractor applicants must complete the required eight-hour Board-approved pre-license course.
10. **Complete applicable business examinations.** Class A and Class B applicants have separate Designated Employee business examination requirements.
11. **Submit the contractor license application.** The business must complete the remaining requirements for its selected class and ROC specialty.

Passing the Roofing Contractor examination does not automatically issue a contractor license. The exam is one part of the licensing process. The contractor business must also satisfy the requirements associated with its class, Responsible Management, Qualified Individual, pre-license education, and other applicable licensing requirements.

### State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors licenses businesses engaged in construction, repair, removal, and improvement work on property owned by others. Contractor licenses consist of a license class and one or more classifications or specialties.

The Qualified Individual for the ROC specialty must be a full-time employee of the contractor business or a member of Responsible Management. The individual must also have the applicable roofing experience required for the contractor class.

Current minimum Qualified Individual experience requirements are **one year for Class C**, **three years for Class B**, and **five years for Class A**.

Virginia's Roofing Contracting scope includes installation, repair, removal, and improvement of materials used to create watertight and weather-resistant roof and deck surfaces. It also includes roofing components installed with a roofing project, dampproofing, waterproofing, and roof insulation systems installed above the roof deck.

New Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor applicants must complete **eight hours of Board-approved pre-license education**. This course is separate from technical ROC exam preparation.

Class A and Class B contractor applicants also have separate Designated Employee business examination requirements. Those examinations address business and Virginia contractor requirements and are separate from the Roofing Contractor technical test completed by the Qualified Individual.
