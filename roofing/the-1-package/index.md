### Exam Details

The Virginia Roofing Contractor examination is the Qualified Individual technical examination associated with the **ROC** specialty. Virginia requires a contractor business requesting the Roofing specialty to identify a Qualified Individual who meets the applicable experience requirements and successfully completes the required technical examination.

The current Virginia Roofing Contractor examination contains **50 questions**. Candidates receive **120 minutes** to complete the examination and must achieve a minimum passing score of **75%**.

The examination is organized into the following areas:

- **Estimating and Plan Reading:** 5 questions
- **Surface Preparation and Planning:** 5 questions
- **Steep and Low Slope Roofing, including Built-Up Roofing and Waterproofing:** 13 questions
- **Asphalt, Wood, and Hard Tile Shingles and Wood Shakes:** 5 questions
- **Roofing Components:** 10 questions
- **Repairs and Reroofing:** 6 questions
- **OSHA Safety:** 6 questions

Steep- and low-slope roofing is the largest examination category. Candidates should be familiar with roofing assemblies, built-up roofing, membrane systems, waterproofing, insulation, flashing, drainage, attachment methods, penetrations, transitions, and other installation principles.

Roofing Components is another major area. Preparation should include roof decks, underlayments, insulation, fasteners, flashing, drainage components, roof edges, penetrations, and related materials that work together as part of a complete roofing system.

Virginia uses PSI to administer Qualified Individual contractor specialty examinations. The current Qualified Individual specialty examination fee is **$85**, paid directly to PSI and separate from The 1 Package pricing.

### Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licenses combine a license class with one or more classifications or specialties. Roofing Contracting is identified by the specialty abbreviation ROC.

1. **Choose the appropriate contractor license class.** Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
2. **Select Roofing Contracting.** The Roofing specialty is identified as ROC.
3. **Identify the Qualified Individual.** The contractor business must designate a Qualified Individual for Roofing Contracting.
4. **Meet the experience requirement.** The Qualified Individual must have sufficient experience within the roofing specialty for the requested license class.
5. **Submit experience verification.** Roofing is a specialty requiring examination pre-approval and applicable experience documentation.
6. **Receive approval for the ROC examination.** The Qualified Individual must receive the required approval before scheduling the test.
7. **Prepare for the technical exam.** Use the course, practice preparation, and included technical references.
8. **Complete the Roofing Contractor examination.** The Qualified Individual must satisfy the technical examination requirement.
9. **Complete applicable pre-license education.** New Virginia contractor license applicants must meet the Board-approved pre-license education requirement.
10. **Complete applicable Class A or Class B business requirements.** These are separate from the ROC technical examination.
11. **Complete the contractor license application.** Application Service is included in The 1 Package.

Passing the ROC examination is one component of contractor licensing. It does not automatically issue a contractor license. The business must also satisfy the applicable contractor class, Qualified Individual, business, education, application, and regulatory requirements.

### State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors regulates contractor businesses and establishes requirements for contractor license classes, Qualified Individuals, Responsible Management, examinations, and specialties.

A business requesting the Roofing specialty must identify a Qualified Individual who meets Virginia's experience and examination requirements. The Qualified Individual must be either a full-time employee of the contractor business working at least 30 hours per week or a member of Responsible Management.

Current minimum experience requirements for the Qualified Individual are **one year for Class C**, **three years for Class B**, and **five years for Class A**. The experience must relate to the requested classification or specialty.

An Experience Verification Form is required for a Qualified Individual requesting a designation that requires pre-approval for an examination. Roofing Contracting is among the specialties requiring a Board-approved technical examination.

New contractor licenses also require Board-approved pre-license education. For Class A and Class B applicants, the pre-license education must be completed by a member of Responsible Management or the Designated Employee. For Class C applicants, a member of Responsible Management must complete the education requirement.

Class A and Class B applicants also have separate business examination requirements for the Designated Employee. Those requirements are distinct from the Roofing Contractor technical examination completed by the Qualified Individual.

## How does the Roofing Contractor exam work?

The Roofing Contractor exam is the technical portion of the license for that classification. A Class A or B firm usually also needs the business and law portion, and every applicant needs the 8-hour pre-license course.

- Virginia issues contractor licenses in three classes. Class C covers single jobs of $1,000 to under $10,000 and under $150,000 a year; Class B covers single jobs of $10,000 to under $120,000 and under $750,000 a year; Class A has no upper limit.
- Every applicant completes 8 hours of pre-license education before the license is issued.
- Class A and B firms name a designated employee or member of responsible management who passes the exams. PSI runs them, and the fee is $40 for one portion, $72 for two and $85 for three.
- The exams are open book. You bring your own references, which may be highlighted and permanently tabbed; removable flags and loose notes are not allowed.
