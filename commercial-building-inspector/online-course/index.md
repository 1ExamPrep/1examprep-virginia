The exam content includes General Administration, Building Planning, Footings and Foundations, Floor Construction, Wall Construction and Coverings, Roof/Ceiling Construction, and Public Safety and Special Construction. These categories reflect the practical job tasks of a commercial building inspector. Students should be ready to inspect commercial construction for compliance with approved plans, applicable code sections, referenced standards, manufacturer installation instructions when applicable, and required construction details.

Students should understand how inspection duties fit into the code administration process and how the International Building Code establishes the authority used for commercial building inspection work.

This area is important because commercial buildings vary widely by occupancy, size, construction type, risk level, and use. ## How does the Commercial Building Inspector (B2) exam work?

The Commercial Building Inspector (B2) exam is a code official certification. It is taken after the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class and leads to DHCD certification.

- Virginia code officials are certified by the Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD), not by DPOR.
- The path is the Virginia Building Code Academy Core class, the classes for your certification, then the required exam, usually an ICC exam.
- Building, fire and property maintenance officials apply within 12 months of hire; technical assistants have 18 months.
- Certified officials complete 16 hours of DHCD-approved continuing education every 2 years.
