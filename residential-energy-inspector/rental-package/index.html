The ICC 79 Residential Energy Inspector / Plans Examiner exam is a national certification exam offered by the International Code Council. It is associated with residential energy inspection and residential energy plan review and evaluates a candidate’s ability to apply energy conservation code requirements to residential construction documents and field conditions.

The ICC 79 exam is listed as a 50-question multiple-choice exam with a 2-hour time limit. Candidates should expect questions that require code lookup, interpretation, table use, and practical application. The 2021 version of the exam is connected to the 2021 International Energy Conservation Code.
