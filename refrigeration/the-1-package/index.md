### Exam Details

The Virginia Refrigeration Contracting examination is the technical specialty examination associated with the REF designation. Virginia requires a Qualified Individual for each classification or specialty on a contractor license, and REF is one of the specialties requiring pre-approval and successful completion of a Board-approved technical examination.

The Virginia Refrigeration Contracting examination contains **50 questions**. Candidates receive **120 minutes** to complete the exam and must achieve a minimum passing score of **70%, or 35 correct answers**.

The examination content is divided into the following subject areas:

- **General Knowledge and Requirements:** 16 questions
- **Refrigeration Piping:** 10 questions
- **Refrigerants:** 5 questions
- **Refrigeration Systems and Controls:** 12 questions
- **Testing and Inspection:** 7 questions

General Knowledge and Requirements represents the largest single category. Refrigeration Systems and Controls and Refrigeration Piping also account for a substantial portion of the examination. Candidates should devote appropriate study time to all five areas while giving additional attention to the higher-weighted subjects.

The examination may include questions based on the reference materials as well as general trade knowledge and accepted industry practices. Practical experience can be valuable, but candidates should also prepare for technical terminology, written code requirements, tables, system concepts, and questions that require efficient use of reference materials.

Virginia uses PSI as its examination vendor for Qualified Individual contractor specialty examinations. Successful completion of the required examination must occur before applying for contractor licensure for a designation that requires the technical exam.

### Open Book Test

The Virginia Refrigeration Contracting examination is an **open-book test**. Candidates must bring their own permitted reference materials because references are not supplied at the testing site.

The examination is currently based on the **2021 edition of applicable ICC codes** for code questions, including the International Mechanical Code. Candidates should therefore become familiar with the structure of the 2021 International Mechanical Code before examination day.

References may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are permitted. Removable tabs such as temporary flags are not allowed. Reference materials containing handwriting are not permitted, and candidates may not write in their books during the examination session.

Open-book testing rewards preparation and efficient navigation. With 50 questions and 120 minutes, candidates have an average of approximately 2.4 minutes per question. Searching unfamiliar books for every answer can quickly consume testing time.

### Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing is administered through the Virginia Board for Contractors. Contractor licenses combine a license class with one or more classifications or specialties. The class determines the applicable level of contracting authority, while the classification or specialty determines the type of work the business may perform.

1. **Choose the appropriate contractor license class.** Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
2. **Select Refrigeration Contracting.** Refrigeration Contracting is identified by the REF specialty abbreviation.
3. **Identify the Qualified Individual.** Every contractor business must identify a Qualified Individual for each classification or specialty requested.
4. **Meet the applicable experience requirement.** The Qualified Individual must document sufficient experience for the selected contractor class.
5. **Complete the REF examination approval process.** Refrigeration Contracting requires pre-approval and successful completion of the applicable technical examination.
6. **Prepare for and pass the specialty examination.** Technical study should include refrigeration knowledge, reference navigation, and 2021 International Mechanical Code material.
7. **Complete contractor pre-license education.** Virginia requires eight hours of Board-approved pre-license education for new contractor licenses.
8. **Complete applicable business examinations.** Class A and Class B businesses must identify a Designated Employee who satisfies the applicable business examination requirements.
9. **Establish the business entity.** The 1 Package includes Business Formation assistance for an LLC or Corporation.
10. **Obtain the EIN.** EIN Filing with the IRS is included with this package.
11. **Complete and submit the contractor application.** Application Service is included to support the licensing application portion of the process.

The technical specialty examination is only one part of Virginia contractor licensing. The contractor business must also satisfy requirements involving the appropriate class, Qualified Individual, Responsible Management, pre-license education, applicable Designated Employee examinations, and completed application.

### State Requirements

Virginia requires licensed contractor firms to identify a Qualified Individual for every classification or specialty printed on the license. The Qualified Individual must have the appropriate amount of experience and must satisfy examination requirements when an examination is required for that designation.

For a **Class C** contractor, the Qualified Individual must have at least **one year of experience** in the applicable classification or specialty. A **Class B** Qualified Individual must have at least **three years of experience**, and a **Class A** Qualified Individual must have at least **five years of experience**.

The Qualified Individual must also have the required relationship to the contractor business. Virginia requires the individual to be a full-time employee or a member of Responsible Management as provided by the contractor licensing requirements.

REF is specifically identified as a designation requiring pre-approval and successful completion of the applicable examination. An Experience Verification Form is required as part of the qualification process for designations that require a technical examination.

Virginia also requires **eight hours of Board-approved pre-license education** for every new Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor license. For Class A and Class B applicants, the requirement is completed by a member of Responsible Management or the Designated Employee. For Class C applicants, a member of Responsible Management completes the course.

The pre-license course is a basic business course addressing regulations, statutes, and requirements relevant to contractor businesses. It is separate from the REF technical examination and separate from the Class A and Class B contractor business examinations.
