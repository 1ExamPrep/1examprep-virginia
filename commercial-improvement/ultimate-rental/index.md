### State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors licenses businesses engaged in construction, removal, repair, or improvement of facilities on property owned by others. Virginia contractor licenses are issued to business entities and sole proprietors and include both a license class and one or more classifications or specialties.

The license class and specialty serve different purposes. The Class A, B, or C designation establishes the monetary level of projects or contracts that may be performed. The specialty establishes the type of contracting work authorized. Commercial Improvement Contracting is identified by the CIC specialty designation.

Virginia requires a Qualified Individual for each specialty on the contractor license. Commercial Improvement is included among the specialties for which the Qualified Individual must successfully complete a technical examination approved by the Board for Contractors in addition to meeting applicable experience requirements.

New contractor applicants must also complete eight hours of Board-approved pre-license education. For Class A and Class B applicants, the pre-license education must be completed by a member of responsible management or the Designated Employee. The pre-license education requirement is separate from the Class A and Class B contractor examinations.

Class A and Class B applicants have additional requirements involving a Designated Employee. Candidates pursuing one of these license classes should prepare for the applicable business examination requirements separately from the CIC technical examination.

## How does the Commercial Improvement Contractor (CIC) exam work?

The Commercial Improvement Contractor (CIC) exam is the technical portion of the license for that classification. A Class A or B firm usually also needs the business and law portion, and every applicant needs the 8-hour pre-license course.

- Virginia issues contractor licenses in three classes. Class C covers single jobs of $1,000 to under $10,000 and under $150,000 a year; Class B covers single jobs of $10,000 to under $120,000 and under $750,000 a year; Class A has no upper limit.
- Every applicant completes 8 hours of pre-license education before the license is issued.
- Class A and B firms name a designated employee or member of responsible management who passes the exams. PSI runs them, and the fee is $40 for one portion, $72 for two and $85 for three.
- The exams are open book. You bring your own references, which may be highlighted and permanently tabbed; removable flags and loose notes are not allowed.
