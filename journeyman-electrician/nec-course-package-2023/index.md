### Exam Details

The Virginia Journeyman Electrician examination is administered by PSI for the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation. Applicants must receive examination approval from the Board for Contractors before scheduling the test.

The current Journeyman Electrician examination contains 70 questions and allows 210 minutes, or three and one-half hours, for completion. Candidates must answer at least 49 questions correctly to achieve the required passing score.

The examination includes Virginia-specific regulatory material as well as technical electrical questions. Standards of Practice, Standards of Conduct, Renewal and Reinstatement, Definitions, Qualification for Licensure, and Revocation of Licensure are included in the examination outline.

Technical subjects include General Electrical Knowledge, Requirements for Electrical Installations, Electrical-Electronic Fundamentals, Branch Circuits, Services and Feeders, Grounding and Bonding, Conductors and Cables, Raceways and Boxes, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, Electrical Signs and Outline Lighting, Motors and Controls, Utilization Equipment, Lighting, Low Voltage, Communication Systems, Fire Detection and Alarm Systems, and Safety.

Conductors and Cables and Raceways and Boxes represent significant portions of the examination. Candidates should understand conductor ampacity, insulation types, adjustment and correction factors, cable applications, conduit systems, raceway fill, box fill, support requirements, enclosures, and permitted wiring methods.

Grounding and Bonding is another major topic. Preparation should include grounding electrode systems, grounding electrode conductors, equipment grounding conductors, bonding jumpers, separately derived systems, services, enclosures, raceways, and effective ground-fault current paths.

Services, Feeders, and Branch Circuits require knowledge of electrical loads, conductor sizing, overcurrent protection, service equipment, disconnecting means, required circuits, grounding and bonding, receptacle requirements, GFCI protection, AFCI protection, and other NEC provisions governing power distribution.

Motors and Controls require candidates to understand motor conductors, overload protection, short-circuit and ground-fault protection, disconnecting means, controllers, motor calculations, and related code rules.

Safety is also part of the examination. Candidates should understand safe electrical practices and the electrical safety concepts associated with the approved safety reference identified for the Virginia electrician examination.

The simulated practice exams included with this package help candidates apply electrical knowledge under exam-style conditions. Practice results can identify whether additional study is needed in calculations, conductor rules, grounding, raceways, services, motors, Virginia regulations, or code navigation.

### Open Book Test

The Virginia Journeyman Electrician examination is an open-book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing the approved reference materials required for the examination because references are not provided at the PSI testing site.

The current electrician examination bases its National Electrical Code questions on NFPA 70, National Electrical Code, 2020 Edition. The current reference list also identifies NFPA 70E, Standard for Electrical Safety in the Workplace, for electrician testing, together with the Virginia Board for Contractors Individual License and Certification Regulations.

This package includes the 2023 NEC for course instruction and electrical preparation. Because the official Virginia examination currently bases NEC questions on the 2020 edition, candidates should also prepare with the 2020 NEC before test day.

Approved examination references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are permitted. Removable tabs are not allowed.

Reference materials containing written notes are not permitted under the current PSI examination rules. Candidates also may not write in their references during the testing session.

No study guides are permitted in the examination room. The Master Electrician Study Guide, Calculations Study Guide, flash cards, online course materials, simulated practice exams, and instructional videos included with this package should therefore be used for preparation rather than treated as exam-room materials.

The included NEC tabs support the code-navigation skills required for open-book testing. Students can practice locating branch circuits, services, feeders, grounding and bonding, conductors, raceways, motors, utilization equipment, special occupancies, and other important subjects.

Open-book testing still requires substantial preparation. With 70 questions in three and one-half hours, candidates should recognize the electrical subject first and then move directly toward the appropriate article, table, definition, or section.

### Licensing Steps

Begin by selecting the Virginia Journeyman Electrician qualification pathway that matches your education and practical experience. Virginia provides several combinations of vocational training and field experience that can qualify an applicant for examination approval.

An applicant with a bachelor’s degree in an engineering curriculum related to the electrical trade may qualify with one year of practical experience. An applicant with an associate degree or certificate from at least a two-year tradesman-related program may qualify with two years of practical experience.

Another pathway requires 240 hours of formal vocational training together with four years of practical electrical experience. Applicants with 160 hours of formal vocational training may qualify with five years of experience.

Applicants with 80 hours of formal vocational training may qualify with six years of practical experience, while applicants with 40 hours may qualify with seven years. An applicant without formal vocational training may qualify with eight years of practical experience.

Virginia also provides an examination exemption for individuals who successfully complete an apprenticeship program approved by the Commissioner of the Department of Workforce Development and Advancement and provide the required completion documentation.

Applicants who must take the examination are required to submit documentation of identification, vocational training, and experience. Applicants must also be at least 18 years old and satisfy the other general licensing requirements established by the Board.

Submit the Tradesman Exam and License Application to the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation. The Board reviews applications and determines whether the applicant qualifies for examination.

After receiving approval, schedule the Journeyman Electrician examination with PSI. Candidates should review the current PSI candidate bulletin and prepare the approved references before the scheduled test date.

Begin study with the NEC overview and use the included tabs while practicing with the 2023 NEC. Transfer those navigation skills to the 2020 NEC required for the official Virginia examination.

Use the Master Electrician Study Guide for advanced supplemental review and complete the Calculations Study Guide regularly. Combine those resources with flash cards, instructor-led live sessions, recorded videos, and simulated practice testing.

After passing the required examination, complete the remaining licensing process through the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation so the Journeyman Electrician license can be issued.

### State Requirements

Virginia licenses Journeyman Electricians statewide through the Board for Contractors within the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation. Tradesman licenses are issued to individuals rather than business entities.

Virginia establishes multiple education-and-experience pathways for Journeyman Electrician applicants. The available pathways range from qualifying engineering education with one year of practical experience to eight years of practical experience without formal vocational training.

Applicants must document their vocational education and practical experience as part of the licensing process. Examination approval is required before an applicant who is not exempt can schedule the Virginia Journeyman Electrician test.

Individuals who successfully complete an approved Virginia apprenticeship program may qualify for exemption from the journeyman examination when the required apprenticeship completion documentation is submitted.

Virginia currently uses the 2020 National Electrical Code as part of the Uniform Statewide Building Code. The current PSI Journeyman Electrician examination also identifies the 2020 NEC for code questions.

This product remains based on the 2023 NEC and provides current National Electrical Code study, calculations, and navigation practice. Candidates preparing specifically for the Virginia licensing examination should distinguish between the 2023 NEC included in the package and the 2020 NEC currently used for official examination questions.

Virginia Journeyman Electricians are subject to continuing-education requirements as a condition of license renewal. Electricians must complete three hours of Board-approved electrical continuing education during each two-year license term.

The continuing-education course must be directly related to the current electrical code applicable to the trade and must be completed through a provider approved by the Board for Contractors.

Journeyman licensing should also be distinguished from contractor licensing. A Virginia Journeyman Electrician license establishes an individual tradesman credential and does not by itself create a contractor business license.

Licensed electricians remain responsible for complying with Virginia electrical regulations, the Uniform Statewide Building Code, permits, inspections, safety requirements, supervision rules, and other laws governing electrical work.

## How does the Journeyman Electrician exam work?

The Journeyman Electrician exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
