Open Book Test

You confirmed the Georgia exam is open book. Open book gives you access to the NEC—but it also raises the importance of navigation skill. The clock doesn’t slow down because you have the code, and many test questions are written with answer choices that look similar unless you verify the exact NEC requirement.

To perform well in an open-book NEC environment, train this repeatable workflow:

  • Translate the question into a search term: box fill, conduit fill, ampacity, overcurrent protection, grounding electrode, bonding jumper, GFCI, AFCI, service conductors, motor circuit, etc.
  • Choose the fastest path: use the index when you’re unsure; use tabs when you recognize the code neighborhood immediately.
  • Confirm the complete rule: read carefully for exceptions, notes, table footnotes, and conditions.
  • Match the conditions to the scenario: location, voltage, occupancy, wiring method, equipment type, and environment can change the answer.
  • Answer and move on: don’t overthink once you’ve verified the rule.

Your National Electrical Code 2023 Paperback with Tabs supports that process. This set includes the NEC 2023 Code and the affixable tabs that go along with it, helping you reach major article areas faster during practice and on exam day.

How does the Journeyman Electrician exam work?

The Journeyman Electrician exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

  • Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
  • The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
  • Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
  • Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
