### Licensing Steps

A practical licensing path begins with building qualifying electrical experience and formal vocational training. Virginia’s journeyman tradesman rules use combinations of practical experience and education to determine eligibility. A common path includes four years of practical experience and 240 hours of formal vocational training in the trade. Other approved combinations may include additional years of practical experience with fewer formal training hours, or approved education pathways that satisfy the Board’s requirements.

### State Requirements

Virginia tradesman licenses have renewal and continuing education requirements. ## How does the Journeyman Electrician exam work?

The Journeyman Electrician exam is a tradesman exam: one person is licensed, and a contracting firm in that trade also needs its own Board for Contractors license.

- Tradesman licenses are issued by the Board for Contractors through its Tradesmen program, for electrical, plumbing, HVAC and gas fitting.
- The board has to approve you before PSI lets you schedule. The exam fee is $100 for journeyman and $125 for master.
- Pre-license education is required, and renewals need 3 hours of continuing education for electrical, plumbing and HVAC and 1 hour for gas fitting.
- Residential plumbing and residential HVAC mechanic licenses, added on April 1, 2025, need less experience than a journeyman license.
