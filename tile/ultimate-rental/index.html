Exam Details

The Virginia Tile, Marble, Ceramic and Terrazzo examination is the Qualified Individual technical examination associated with the TMC specialty. Virginia requires a contractor business requesting this specialty to identify a Qualified Individual who meets the applicable experience requirements and successfully completes the required specialty examination.

The TMC examination contains 50 questions. Candidates receive 120 minutes to complete the test and must achieve at least 70%, or 35 correct answers, to meet the passing requirement.

The examination covers several areas of tile and terrazzo contracting, including:

  • Plan Reading and Estimating
  • General Tile Knowledge
  • Grouting and Finishing
  • Adhesives
  • Tile Installation
  • Terrazzo
  • Safety

General Tile Knowledge and Tile Installation are central areas of preparation. Candidates should understand tile types, substrates, surface preparation, setting materials, installation methods, layout, movement accommodation, joint requirements, curing, and accepted industry practices.

Terrazzo also deserves dedicated study. Candidates who work primarily with ceramic, porcelain, glass, or stone tile should prepare for terrazzo materials, systems, specifications, installation considerations, and finishing techniques.

Plan Reading and Estimating requires candidates to interpret dimensions, layouts, quantities, drawings, and specifications. Grouting, adhesives, finishing, and OSHA safety should also be included in a balanced study plan.

PSI administers Virginia Qualified Individual specialty examinations after candidates receive the applicable approval through the Virginia Board for Contractors.

Open Book Test

The Virginia Tile, Marble, Ceramic and Terrazzo Contractor examination is an open-book test. Candidates should become familiar with the permitted references before test day so they can identify the correct book and locate technical information efficiently.

Open-book testing does not remove the need for technical knowledge. With 50 questions and 120 minutes, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds per question. Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licenses combine a license class with one or more classifications or specialties. Tile, Marble, Ceramic and Terrazzo Contracting is identified by the specialty abbreviation TMC.

  1. Determine the appropriate contractor license class. Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
  2. Select the TMC specialty. Request Tile, Marble, Ceramic and Terrazzo Contracting for the contractor business.
  3. Identify the Qualified Individual. The business must designate an individual who qualifies the company for the TMC specialty.
  4. Meet the applicable experience requirement. The Qualified Individual must document sufficient experience within the requested specialty.
  5. Submit Experience Verification. TMC is among the specialties requiring pre-approval and a technical examination.
  6. Receive examination approval. Candidates must complete the applicable qualification process before scheduling the TMC exam.
  7. Prepare for the technical examination. Use the one-year course access, practice-oriented review, and technical references.
  8. Complete the TMC specialty examination. The Qualified Individual must satisfy the technical exam requirement.
  9. Complete applicable pre-license education. New Virginia contractor applicants must satisfy the Board-approved pre-license education requirement.
  10. Complete additional business requirements when applicable. Class A and Class B applicants have separate business examination requirements for the Designated Employee.
  11. Complete the contractor license application. Application Service is included with this Ultimate package.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors licenses contractor businesses and requires a Qualified Individual for each applicable classification or specialty.

The Qualified Individual must be either a full-time employee of the business or a member of Responsible Management. Current Virginia application requirements establish minimum experience of 2 years for Class C, 3 years for Class B, and 5 years for Class A.

An Experience Verification Form must be submitted for a Qualified Individual requesting a designation that requires pre-approval for examination. TMC is included among the specialties requiring pre-approval and successful completion of a technical examination.

Virginia defines TMC work as preparation, fabrication, construction, and installation of artificial marble, burned clay tile, ceramic, terrazzo, encaustic, faience, quarry, semi-vitreous, cementitious board, and other tile. Hollow or structural partition tile is excluded.

The Residential Building and Commercial Building classifications and the Home Improvement and Commercial Improvement specialties can also provide authority for TMC work within their respective scopes.

New Virginia contractor applicants must also satisfy the applicable pre-license business education requirements. Class A and Class B applicants have separate Designated Employee business examination requirements in addition to the technical specialty examination completed by the Qualified Individual.
