Exam Details

The Virginia Tile, Marble, Ceramic and Terrazzo Contractor examination is the Qualified Individual technical examination associated with the TMC specialty. A Virginia contractor business requesting this specialty must identify a Qualified Individual who meets the applicable experience requirements and successfully completes the required examination.

The TMC examination contains 50 questions. Candidates receive 120 minutes to complete the test and must achieve at least 70%, or 35 correct answers, to meet the passing requirement.

Major areas of preparation include:

  • Plan Reading and Estimating
  • General Tile Knowledge
  • Grouting and Finishing
  • Adhesives
  • Tile Installation
  • Terrazzo
  • Safety

General Tile Knowledge and Tile Installation are central parts of preparation. Candidates should understand tile types, substrates, surface preparation, setting materials, layout, movement accommodation, installation methods, curing, joints, and accepted industry practices.

Terrazzo requires separate attention because it involves specialized materials, systems, specifications, installation procedures, and finishing techniques. Candidates whose daily work primarily involves ceramic, porcelain, stone, or glass tile should not overlook this portion of the specialty.

Plan Reading and Estimating also require preparation involving dimensions, areas, quantities, layouts, project plans, and specifications. Grouting, adhesives, finishing, and OSHA construction safety are additional subjects that contribute to overall examination performance.

Virginia uses PSI to administer Qualified Individual contractor specialty examinations. Candidates must receive the applicable examination approval before scheduling the TMC test. The current Qualified Individual specialty examination fee is $85, paid separately to PSI.

The open-book format does not eliminate the need for technical knowledge. With 50 questions and 120 minutes, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds per question. Searching several unfamiliar books for every answer can quickly consume available testing time.

Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licenses combine a license class with one or more classifications or specialties. Tile, Marble, Ceramic and Terrazzo Contracting is identified by the specialty abbreviation TMC.

  1. Determine the contractor license class. Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
  2. Select the TMC specialty. The business requests Tile, Marble, Ceramic and Terrazzo Contracting as the applicable specialty.
  3. Identify the Qualified Individual. The contractor business must designate the individual who qualifies the company for TMC work.
  4. Meet the applicable experience requirement. The Qualified Individual must document experience appropriate to the requested contractor class and specialty.
  5. Submit experience verification. TMC requires pre-approval for the technical examination.
  6. Receive examination approval. The candidate must be approved before scheduling the TMC technical examination.
  7. Prepare for the examination. Use the one-year course access, technical books, practice-oriented study, and reference-navigation preparation.
  8. Complete the TMC examination. The Qualified Individual must satisfy the technical examination requirement.
  9. Complete applicable pre-license education. New Virginia contractor applicants must satisfy the required Board-approved pre-license education.
  10. Complete applicable Class A or Class B business requirements. These are separate from the TMC Qualified Individual examination.
  11. Complete the contractor license application. Application Service is included with The 1 Package.
  12. Establish the business entity. Business Formation for an LLC or Corporation is included.
  13. Obtain the EIN. EIN Filing with the IRS is included.
  14. Address contractor compliance. Contractor Compliance Guidance helps customers understand requirements associated with operating a Virginia contracting business.

Passing the TMC technical examination is one part of the Virginia contractor licensing process. The contractor business must also satisfy the applicable business, education, Qualified Individual, application, and regulatory requirements.

State Requirements

The Virginia Board for Contractors licenses contractor businesses and requires a Qualified Individual for each applicable classification or specialty designation.

The Qualified Individual must be a full-time employee of the business working at least 30 hours per week or a member of Responsible Management. Current minimum experience requirements are 1 year for Class C, 3 years for Class B, and 5 years for Class A.

An Experience Verification Form is required when a Qualified Individual requests a specialty that requires pre-approval for a technical examination. TMC is included among the specialties requiring that examination process.

Virginia defines TMC work as preparation, fabrication, construction, and installation of artificial marble, burned clay tile, ceramic, terrazzo, encaustic, faience, quarry, semi-vitreous, cementitious board, and other tile. Hollow or structural partition tile is excluded from the TMC scope.

The Residential Building and Commercial Building classifications and the Home Improvement and Commercial Improvement specialties can also provide authority for this type of work within their respective scopes.

New contractor applicants must also satisfy Virginia's applicable pre-license education requirements. Class A and Class B applicants have separate Designated Employee business examination requirements in addition to the technical examination completed by the Qualified Individual.
