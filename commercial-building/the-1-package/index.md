### State Requirements

Virginia defines Commercial Building Contractors as contractors whose work includes construction, remodeling, repair, improvement, removal, or demolition on real property owned, controlled, or leased by another person involving commercial, industrial, institutional, governmental, and accessory-use buildings or structures.

The CBC classification does not provide authority for electrical, plumbing, HVAC, or gas-fitting services. It also does not authorize construction, repair, or improvement of dwellings and townhouses as defined by the Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code.

Virginia contractor licenses are issued in Class A, Class B, and Class C. Current Virginia licensing information identifies the license class as the portion of the contractor license that determines the applicable monetary limitations on projects, while the classification or specialty determines what type of work the contractor may perform.

The Commercial Building classification requires an appropriately qualified individual. Virginia identifies CBC among classifications requiring examination pre-approval and an Experience Verification Form for the Qualified Individual.

New contractor applicants must also complete eight hours of approved pre-license education. The business may have additional requirements depending on the license class and organizational structure, including financial documentation for applicable license classes.

## How does the Commercial Building Contractor (CBC) exam work?

The Commercial Building Contractor (CBC) exam is the technical portion of the license for that classification. A Class A or B firm usually also needs the business and law portion, and every applicant needs the 8-hour pre-license course.

- Virginia issues contractor licenses in three classes. Class C covers single jobs of $1,000 to under $10,000 and under $150,000 a year; Class B covers single jobs of $10,000 to under $120,000 and under $750,000 a year; Class A has no upper limit.
- Every applicant completes 8 hours of pre-license education before the license is issued.
- Class A and B firms name a designated employee or member of responsible management who passes the exams. PSI runs them, and the fee is $40 for one portion, $72 for two and $85 for three.
- The exams are open book. You bring your own references, which may be highlighted and permanently tabbed; removable flags and loose notes are not allowed.
