### Open Book Test

The Virginia Commercial Building Technical Contractor examination is an **open-book test**. Candidates must bring their own permitted reference materials because the testing center does not provide the books.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs are permitted. Removable tabs are not allowed, and reference materials containing written notes are not permitted. Candidates may not write in their references during the examination session.

### Licensing Steps

Virginia contractor licensing is administered by the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation Board for Contractors. The Commercial Building Contractor classification is one of Virginia's primary contractor classifications.

1. **Determine the appropriate contractor license class.** Virginia issues Class A, Class B, and Class C contractor licenses.
2. **Identify the Commercial Building classification.** Candidates pursuing commercial building authority request the CBC classification as applicable to their contractor license.
3. **Designate a Qualified Individual.** The business must identify a Qualified Individual who meets Virginia's requirements for the CBC classification.
4. **Meet the applicable experience requirement.** The Qualified Individual must satisfy the experience requirement associated with the requested license class.
5. **Complete required pre-license education.** New Virginia contractor license applicants must satisfy the applicable eight-hour pre-license education requirement.
6. **Complete the required examination process.** The Qualified Individual must complete the Commercial Building Technical Contractor examination when required for the classification.
7. **Complete any additional examinations applicable to the selected license class.**
8. **Submit the contractor license application and required documentation.**
9. **Meet the remaining business, financial, and licensing requirements applicable to the selected class.**

Passing the CBC technical examination is one component of the Virginia contractor licensing process. Examination success does not by itself issue a contractor license.

### State Requirements

Virginia defines Commercial Building Contractors as contractors whose work includes construction, remodeling, repair, improvement, removal, or demolition on real property owned, controlled, or leased by another person involving commercial, industrial, institutional, governmental, and accessory-use buildings or structures.

The CBC classification does not provide authority for electrical, plumbing, HVAC, or gas-fitting services. Contractors performing those regulated functions must have the appropriate licensing or specialty authority. The CBC classification also does not authorize construction, repair, or improvement of dwellings and townhouses as defined by the Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code.

The broad scope of the CBC classification explains the range of topics found on the technical examination. Commercial building contractors may encounter site preparation, structural concrete, reinforcement, masonry, structural steel, framing, roofing, moisture protection, interior finishes, accessibility, safety, estimating, plans, and building-code requirements.

Virginia contractor applicants must also satisfy requirements applicable to their selected license class, including Qualified Individual requirements, pre-license education, examination requirements, and application documentation.

## How does the Commercial Building Contractor (CBC) exam work?

The Commercial Building Contractor (CBC) exam is the technical portion of the license for that classification. A Class A or B firm usually also needs the business and law portion, and every applicant needs the 8-hour pre-license course.
